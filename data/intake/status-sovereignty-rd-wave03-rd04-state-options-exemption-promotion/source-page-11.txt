ABAWD Discretionary Exemptions
Section 6(o)(6) | 273.24(g)
An able-bodied adult without dependents (ABAWD) is
a person aged 18–54 who does not meet any of the
exemption criteria set by Federal law. People are not
considered an ABAWD if they are exempt from the
SNAP general work requirements, share a SNAP
household with a person younger than 18, are
pregnant, are experiencing homelessness, are a
veteran, or are younger than 25 and were in foster
care at 18 (or a higher age if the State oﬀers extended
foster care). ABAWDs must work and/or participate in
a work program at least 80 hours per month, or
participate in workfare, to receive SNAP benefits for
more than 3 months in a 3-year period. The law
provides each State agency with a limited number of
discretionary exemptions. State agencies can use
these exemptions to extend SNAP eligibility for
individual ABAWDs on a monthly basis. Some
agencies choose to use discretionary exemptions,
while others do not. The data displayed are based on
State agencies’ use of discretionary exemptions in
FFY 2024.
