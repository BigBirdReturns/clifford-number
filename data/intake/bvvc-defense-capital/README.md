## Evidence rules

`listed_current` means only that a label was visible on the declared page at retrieval. It does not establish historical continuity, a particular role category, a legal investment, current ownership, a fund vehicle, board rights, operating control, or contact with every other listed person.

Self-authored BVVC pages establish what BVVC publicly represents. Counterparty releases establish what the named company publicly announced. SEC Form D records preserve filer-reported identities and roles under the SEC's own accuracy caveat. None of those sources silently supplies missing security terms, check sizes, ownership percentages, governance rights, or beneficial ownership.

The leadership and portfolio pages are dense surfaces and are explicitly **not hop-eligible**. The McRaven fellowship and School.House surfaces remain denominator-incomplete. Joshua Langford enters as an officially described Operations Associate and McRaven Fellow supporting portfolio operations and School.House. That role is not treated as investment authority, a relationship with every portfolio company, or authority to speak for the firm on unrelated matters.
