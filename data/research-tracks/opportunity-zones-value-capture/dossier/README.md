# Opportunity Zones — value-capture reporting bundle

A public-interest research dossier on the 2017 Opportunity Zone program, assembled with the Clifford
research apparatus (tiered retrieval → resolution → adjudication) entirely from **disclosed public
records** — Treasury/IRS designations, SEC Form-D filings, GAO oversight, Treasury IG and Senate
Finance inquiries, and major investigative reporting.

Evidence classes stay separate. Official filings and designations establish disclosed activity and
timing, not motive or causation. Investigative reporting is attributed. Every candidate carries its
own source; nothing here is a finding, and none of it should replace the underlying record.

Start with the **Case Summary**. The **Land-Layer Instances** ledger and the **Fund-Layer** result
are the load-bearing evidence; **Method & Telemetry** shows how the question was reduced; **Open
Residual** states honestly what remains beyond the apparatus's reach.
