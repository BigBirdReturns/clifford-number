# Case summary — where Opportunity Zone value-capture actually happens

## The question, reduced to a layer

The public-interest question is simple to state and hard to answer honestly: *when a 2017 federal
Opportunity Zone designation de-risked private capital, was that capital positioned in the tract
before the public benefit was conferred?* The apparatus resolved it **by layer**, and the answer is
not where most coverage looks.

## Fund layer — a null

Screening the *fund vehicle* (Qualified Opportunity Funds) for "who got in early" is looking at the
wrong layer. SEC EDGAR Form-D filings containing "qualified opportunity fund" number **zero in all of
2018**; the earliest is **2019-01-04**. Even the broader "opportunity zone" phrase's earliest 2018
Form-D is **2018-06-21 — one week after the June 14, 2018 final designation**. The QOF vehicle
entirely postdates designation, by construction: the regulatory framework did not exist until late
2018. There is **no pre-positioning signal at the fund layer.**

## Land layer — the signal

Pre-positioning, where it is documented, lives in **land ownership plus gubernatorial nomination
discretion** — an already-owned parcel or project placed into a designated tract by a governor's
selection. Public reporting and official oversight (a Treasury Inspector General probe; a Senate
Finance Committee inquiry) document specific instances. This reproduces the Arcadia "patient
ownership positioned before activation" pattern on a federal program — the signature's stages pointed
at the correct layer.

## Verification status of the documented instances

- **Port Covington, Baltimore (Plank / Sagamore)** — VERIFIED verbatim. Land-buying began **2012**; a
  Hogan aide wrote **"Port Covington does not qualify"** before a Feb 5, 2018 developer meeting;
  Hogan selected it in April 2018. The cleanest instance of the pattern.
- **West Palm Beach superyacht marina (Huizenga)** — VERIFIED. The state "had not originally intended
  to pick the census tract containing the superyacht marina"; owner lobbied Gov. Scott ~April 2018.
- **Detroit riverfront (Gilbert / Bedrock)** — VERIFIED verbatim: **"Gilbert owns more than 10
  buildings in the tract."** Ownership predates designation.
- **Tahoe Reno Industrial Center, NV (Sandoval + Heller)** — VERIFIED: gubernatorial/senatorial
  pressure reversed Treasury's initial ineligibility finding (tract median income too high).
- **Storey County ~700-acre site (Milken)** — PARTIAL: Milken confirmed as investor in the ~700-acre
  site Treasury designated "at the personal instruction of" Sec. Mnuchin, per NYT (paywalled); no
  acquisition date independently establishable. **Likely a distinct parcel from TRIC** — TRIC's
  on-record owners are Lance Gilman and Roger Norman, not Milken.

## The boundary that keeps this honest

Designating already-owned land is **lawful**, and is precisely the structural pattern the place-
formation signature detects. These are candidate surfaces documenting a reported sequence and the
official oversight response — not assertions of wrongdoing. Whether any instance constitutes abuse is
for the Treasury IG and Senate Finance Committee to determine, not this pipeline. Nothing here is
promoted to a finding.
