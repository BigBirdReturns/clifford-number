# Preference Custody laboratory floor v19

Preference Custody floor v19 composes the qualified twenty-control v18 floor with PC-21, the comprehension construct, scenario transfer, translation, accessibility, choice architecture, intended exit, self-authorship, assent, payment sequencing, validation, correction, and authority control.

The floor remains a synthetic laboratory. It creates no graph effect, thesis evidence, comprehension audit, consent finding, coercion finding, choice-validity opinion, public-authorization result, manipulation claim, legitimacy verdict, or inference of intent.

## Composition

```text
qualified base             Preference Custody floor v18
base controls              20
extension                  PC-21
extension worlds            8
resulting controls         21
resulting status           laboratory_floor_v19_qualified
graph effect               none
thesis-evidence use        false
real-world evidence state  none
```

The v18 build is compiled, validated, and hashed as a frozen base. PC-21 is compiled and admitted separately. The v19 compiler creates a new control summary, frontier transition, promotion boundary, and hash-linked composition chain without rewriting the prior twenty controls.

## Eight choice-effectiveness worlds

```text
verified comprehension, accessible support, self-authored assent, and intended exit completion
message-open receipt treated as comprehension
recognition quiz passing without consequence understanding or scenario transfer
literal translation without semantic or procedural equivalence
accessibility and assisted-comprehension gaps
fully comprehended choice with twenty intended exits but only five completed because of default and friction
representative or call-center operator substitution for affected-person choice
payment before choice with retention treated as assent
```

The positive world preserves a validated consequence-and-choice construct, complete recognition, consequence explanation, scenario transfer, delayed retention, semantically and procedurally equivalent translation, complete accessible formats and assisted comprehension, a neutral default, a meaningful 60-day route, five intended and completed exits, ninety-five explicit assents, independent validation, explanation, objection, appeal, re-administration, and receipted correction.

The negative worlds isolate message-open substitution, recognition without transfer, literal translation, inaccessible support, fully comprehended but suppressed exit, representative substitution, and payment-before-assent.

## Refusal contract

Floor v19 preserves these separations:

```text
notice delivery, message open, or acknowledgement
≠ comprehension

recognition score
≠ consequence understanding or scenario transfer

translation availability
≠ semantic, procedural, or legal equivalence

formal accessibility label
≠ usable access or assisted comprehension

formal choice route or completed field
≠ self-authored meaningful choice

recorded exit rate
≠ intended exit rate or completed agency

representative or operator action
≠ affected-person assent

payment retention
≠ prior informed agreement

explicit-choice field
≠ valid explicit-assent receipt

same recorded 95/5 disposition
≠ same comprehension, intention, authorship, assent, or authority state

comprehension or exit failure
≠ coercion, manipulation, breach, misconduct, or intent

public informed-choice-complete status
≠ validated, accessible, self-authored, correctable, or publicly authorized choice
```


Forty-six uniquely prefixed requirements are added to the existing real-case promotion boundary. The compiler calculates the genuinely new requirement count against the complete v18 boundary and refuses duplicate inflation.

## Frontier transition

The broad v18 frontier:

```text
notice_comprehension_accessibility_exit_and_assent_effectiveness
```

is removed and replaced by:

```text
comprehension_measurement_translation_accessibility_and_transfer_assurance
choice_architecture_exit_authorship_assent_and_payment_sequence_governance
```

The first successor isolates construct validity, instruments, transfer, retention, translations, accessible formats, assistance, subgroup error, and independent validation. The second isolates defaults, friction, timing, latent intention, completion, authorship, representative action, assent, payment sequencing, and correction.

The separate v18 frontier for operative release text, future and unknown claims, and nonparty binding remains open, as do the v17 harm-reference and allocation-execution frontiers and earlier remedy, insolvency, insurance, and service-substitution frontiers.

## Publication boundary

The admissible laboratory statement is that one operative narrow release, one public `informed_choice_complete` status, one 100-person affected population, full notice delivery, one recorded 95-bound/5-exited disposition, and full payment can coexist with verified comprehension and agency, message-open substitution, recognition without transfer, translation failure, accessibility gaps, suppressed intended exit, representative substitution, or payment-before-assent. A real informed-choice claim requires complete population, comprehension construct and validity, scenario transfer, translation, accessibility, assistance, intention, choice architecture, exit, assent, payment sequencing, representation, validation, correction, durability, and authority custody.
