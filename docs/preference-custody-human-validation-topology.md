# Preference Custody human-validation topology v1

This topology compiles three real-world control packets into one comparative evidence surface:

```text
Times ExplorAItion
News UK Nucleus Panel
YouGov Parallax
```

The states describe what the public evidence establishes. They are not a product ranking, maturity score, procurement recommendation, market denominator, or claim that a higher state is universally better.

The topology creates no graph effect, thesis evidence, causal conclusion, preference-change finding, public-authorization verdict, product-quality score, legitimacy judgment, or inference of manipulative intent.

## Evidence-state ladder

### HV-00: synthetic or modelled surface confirmed

A real deployed workflow or publicly available product uses a synthetic or modelled population.

This state does not establish replacement, human validation, performative effect, preference change, or public authorization.

### HV-01: parallel direct-human infrastructure confirmed

A named direct-human research surface exists alongside the synthetic surface within the same institutional or product environment.

This state does not establish that the two methods touch the same decision, use matched instruments, or validate one another.

### HV-02: integrated synthetic-to-human validation architecture confirmed

The public product or workflow specifies a route from synthetic exploration to configurable direct-human validation.

This state does not establish that a particular output passed the route or that every output must be human-validated.

### HV-03: executed matched study with complete receipts

One paired study preserves the synthetic and human instruments, population construction, response distributions, uncertainty, discrepancy handling, final decision disposition, and deployment-specific system lineage.

No current source case reaches this state.

### HV-04: human evidence can bind the operational decision

An attributed rule permits direct-human evidence to amend, suspend, override, or block the synthetic recommendation, and the consequence is receipted.

No current source case reaches this state.

### HV-05: affected public has binding objective control and remedy

The affected public can bind the objective, agenda, decision rule, implementation, challenge, appeal, and remedy.

No current source case reaches this state.

It also distinguishes methodology verification from finding-specific validation.

The architecture is integrated. The public packet does not contain one complete executed matched study, question-level discrepancy record, human override consequence, participant remedy, or binding public-authority instrument.

## Required matched-study packet

A qualifying HV-03 packet requires:

```text
matched synthetic and human instruments
complete option sets and wording
synthetic and human population construction
eligibility assignment sequencing and timing
question-level response distributions
uncertainty and subgroup errors
nonresponse and attrition states
disagreement and reconciliation protocol
attributed decision owner and final disposition
human override consequence or explicit absence
human-to-model feedback reuse or explicit refusal
deployment-specific runtime metric policy and validation lineage
participant correction withdrawal complaint and remedy
```

HV-04 additionally requires an enforceable operational rule that gives human evidence consequence. HV-05 additionally requires affected-public authority over the objective and remedy.

## Custody

The topology compiler reads and compiles the three source packets directly. It does not retype their conclusions into a new narrative ledger.

It emits a SHA-256 hash-linked chain:

```text
topology manifest sealed
→ Times ExplorAItion source case classified
→ Nucleus Panel source case classified
→ YouGov Parallax source case classified
→ aggregate coverage resolved
→ interpretation sealed
```

Each row carries a hash of the compiled source snapshot. The topology therefore fails if a source case changes status or the aggregate silently promotes a row beyond its source evidence.
