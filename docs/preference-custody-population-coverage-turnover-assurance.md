# Preference Custody PC-36: population-coverage and turnover assurance

PC-36 holds one complete-looking `population_coverage_verified` publication constant across eight incompatible hard-to-enumerate, missingness, boundary, turnover, snapshot, lineage, correction, and authority worlds.

It does not create a real population census, missingness estimate, coverage finding, exclusion finding, discrimination finding, named-actor allegation, or public-authority verdict.

## Refusal boundary

Declared population, enumeration coverage, hard-to-enumerate coverage, missingness, entrant coverage, exit coverage, and current-snapshot publications do not establish complete operational enumeration, missingness classification, boundary validity, turnover propagation, period alignment, correction, durability, or public authorization.
