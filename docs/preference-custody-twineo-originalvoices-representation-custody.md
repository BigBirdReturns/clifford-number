# Twineo and OriginalVoices participant representation custody

This packet records a bounded positive control for individual representation custody. Twineo publicly describes a Personal Digital Twin trained from information supplied by one participant. OriginalVoices exposes Digital Twin answers to clients through a research and agent interface while stating that clients receive anonymised rather than identifiable outputs.

The packet creates no graph effect, thesis evidence, legal opinion on Twin ownership, task-level consent finding, fair-compensation conclusion, collective-bargaining finding, public-authorization verdict, causal claim, or inference of manipulative intent.

## Representation-custody ladder

```text
RC-00  fabricated or aggregate persona
RC-01  representation grounded in one real person
RC-02  person can train update review correct and delete
RC-03  person may receive value and exercise withdrawal or portability
RC-04  person controls each task purpose client and downstream use before execution
RC-05  represented people collectively bind the objective terms and remedy
```

These are evidence states rather than a product ranking. The current public packet reaches **RC-03**.

## Confirmed individual custody

Twineo and OriginalVoices public materials support the following bounded operational rights:

```text
one participant supplies the representation source
participant chooses what training information to provide
participant trains and updates the Twin
participant reviews and rates Twin answers or tasks
participant may correct data
participant may request deletion
participant may withdraw consent
participant may object to processing
participant has a data-portability right
participant may receive rewards from Twin tasks
special-category data requires explicit consent
clients receive anonymised rather than identifiable outputs
participant data is not used to train third-party foundation models
```

Twineo also states that participants are notified when their Twin completes a task and can see and rate the answer. The public product description supports post-task visibility and review. It does not establish that the participant approved the specific client, purpose, question, or downstream use before the Twin answered.

## Value and exit

The public materials establish a reward path, account deletion, data access, correction, erasure, objection, consent withdrawal, and portability.

The same materials preserve limits:

```text
reward amount and revenue-share formula: not public
withdrawal and redemption conditions: platform-controlled
inactive or terminated account reward forfeiture: possible
historical anonymised output recall: not public
correction propagation into prior client outputs: not public
deletion propagation across models backups and logs: not public
```

A participant reward path demonstrates that value can flow back to the represented person. It does not establish that the allocation is proportionate, transparent, collectively negotiated, or economically fair.

## Marketing ownership and legal custody

OriginalVoices describes each Twin as owned and trained by the real person it represents. That is meaningful product-positioning evidence and should be preserved exactly as such.

The public legal instruments establish a different set of facts. Twineo makes the participant responsible for User Content and receives a royalty-free worldwide license to use anonymised versions. Twineo and OriginalVoices reserve the platform, models, systems, functionality, branding, and associated intellectual property to the operator.

The current record therefore supports:

```text
marketing ownership language: present
participant operational and data rights: substantial
legal assignment of Twin title to participant: unresolved
platform license over anonymised User Content: confirmed
platform ownership of models and systems: confirmed
```

The packet does not resolve whether “ownership” refers to personal data rights, contractual control, intellectual-property title, model weights, a revocable license, or a broader normative claim.

## Platform and client control

The same architecture retains meaningful authority outside the participant:

```text
platform controls task types availability and frequency
platform controls earning and redemption rules
platform controls question-admissibility policy
client defines audience-selection criteria
platform owns models and underlying systems
clients own generated outputs under the client terms
```

Public materials do not establish that the participant can control audience ontology, aggregation, client interpretation, or the downstream institutional action taken from an anonymised answer. They also do not establish task-specific client disclosure, purpose disclosure, pre-execution refusal, pre-delivery answer approval, or recall of an already delivered anonymised response.

## Individual custody and public standing

Individual representation custody is materially better than an unaffiliated model inventing a persona with no attributed human source, correction route, value return, or exit right. It remains a different object from collective authority.

```text
individual training and correction
≠ control over audience construction

individual deletion and portability
≠ recall of every derived aggregate

participant reward
≠ negotiated value allocation

post-task review
≠ pre-task consent

individual representation custody
≠ collective bargaining or public authorization
```

RC-04 requires task-specific purpose, client, question, and downstream-use disclosure with a pre-execution approval or refusal right. RC-05 requires represented people to organize, negotiate, ratify, enforce, challenge, and remedy the objective and terms collectively. Neither state is established by current public material.
