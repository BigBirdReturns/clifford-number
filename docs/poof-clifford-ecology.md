# POOF and Clifford constitutional publication ecology

**Status:** repository-native contract and generated review aperture. The public origin is not deployed or approved by this change.

POOF and Clifford now occupy different, binding jurisdictions inside one ecology.

```text
Clifford canonical evidence
→ deterministic evidence cases and reports
→ POOF publication projections
→ human and machine interfaces

reader, subject, journalist, examiner, or audit challenge
→ typed graph-inert referral
→ ordinary Clifford intake and review
→ canonical change only after promotion
```

The governing law is:

> Evidence authority moves outward. Challenges move inward. Publication never writes facts backward into the evidence estate.

## Why the separation exists

Clifford is the evidence kernel. It resolves identities, preserves receipts, distinguishes claims from events, applies selection and temporal law, and records review state. POOF is the constitutional publication system. It sequences an argument, exposes the reader’s own objective and thresholds, convenes interdisciplinary examination, and preserves a durable artifact estate.

Neither can substitute for the other. Clifford without POOF can remain technically inspectable while imposing a high comprehension cost. POOF without Clifford can become a persuasive explanatory system whose prose outruns its evidence. The ecology binds each to its proper failure mode.

## Four POOF jurisdictions

The **Report** is the stable linear argument. Every publication-critical passage travels with a projection manifest linking it to the exact Clifford release, source objects, qualification state, review state, and generated artifact hash.

The **Reader File** is local and contestable. It records declared role, objective, threshold, route changes, inspected objects, and teach-back. It may produce a comprehension receipt. It may not infer motive, ideology, intelligence, or agreement.

The **Examination** hosts external disciplines, counterevidence, release audits, and unresolved propositions. A proceeding can issue a referral packet. It cannot create a canonical fact.

The **Estate** preserves Person Files, Decision Files, REAL STEEL and Steel Mirror projections, newsroom packets, audit artifacts, notices, responses, correction history, prior versions, and exact release custody.

## R8 and R9

R8 is the **Epistemic admissibility and ceiling-conversion dossier**. REAL STEEL becomes its human and newsroom projection. The source authority is K0, not a personality score or actor ranking.

R9 is the **Two-tier constitution and safeguard-allocation audit**. Steel Mirror compares an exact self-regime with an exact other-regime only after hazard comparability, control alignment, legal and operational context, error allocation, and practical remedy are inspected.

Both are report projections. Neither adds a conversion stage, graph edge, motive inference, or publication approval.

## Five transaction objects

1. A **Projection Manifest** carries evidence authority outward without detaching qualifications.
2. A **Referral Packet** carries a bounded challenge inward without bypassing review.
3. A **Comprehension Receipt** records demonstrated evidence operations without asserting agreement or case truth.
4. A **Publication Audit Receipt** records interface performance without becoming substantive evidence.
5. A **Right-of-Reply and Correction Record** preserves notice, response, dispute, correction, and unresolved objection.

The schemas live under `schemas/poof-*.schema.json`. Synthetic fixtures under `test/fixtures/` test the contracts and have no factual effect.

## Generated aperture

`npm run build:poof-ecology` creates a complete static review site under:

```text
reports/core-thesis/poof-clifford-ecology/
```

The site contains separate routes for the Report, Reader File, Examination, Estate, Newsroom, Method, Machine, and Audit. It also emits static OpenAPI, MCP server-card, agent-skill, `llms.txt`, sitemap, robots, data, projection-manifest, and release-manifest products.

The machine contracts are explicitly marked **contract-only and not deployed**. `robots.txt` blocks indexing. Route presence proves neither deployment nor publication approval.

## Release loop

1. Freeze a reviewed Clifford evidence release.
2. Compile POOF projections and manifests.
3. Build human and machine apertures.
4. Run structural and interpretation validation.
5. Run cold-reader and newsroom comprehension tests.
6. Audit the deployed origin with WebsiteIQ and other external examiners.
7. Repair publication defects in the publication layer.
8. Route substantive objections through graph-inert referrals.
9. Promote evidence changes only through Clifford’s ordinary gates.
10. Regenerate POOF and preserve the prior release.

## Operational-effect and amendment hardening

The ecology now treats `graph_effect: none` as only one dimension of authority. Every cross-system transaction carries a seven-dimension operational-effect contract, and every projection declares how it compressed its candidate universe. Constitutional changes are themselves receipted repository transactions. R8 separates documented acts, supported inferences, and interpretive models; R9 permits asymmetric, null, and noncomparable outcomes.

### Merge-base constitutional custody

A permanent validator now compares the pull request with its merge base. Constitutional receipt history is append-only, and the newly appended receipts must exactly cover the protected files changed by the proposal. This closes the reusable-receipt bypass.
