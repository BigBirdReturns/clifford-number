Subject: Research protocol request — Times ExplorAItion and Nucleus Panel matched-method workflow

Dear Times Media Sales and News UK Commercial Data and Insight team,

I am maintaining a source-bounded research record on the use of synthetic audiences and direct human research in consequential product, campaign, content, and proposition decisions.

News UK publicly launched Times ExplorAItion on 27 April 2026 as a synthetic-audience decision-support product. On 21 July 2026, News UK launched Nucleus Panel as direct face-to-face reader research and described it as complementing AI-driven insight. I am trying to classify the relationship between these two methods without assuming that institutional proximity means they form one workflow.

I would appreciate a protocol-level response concerning one named or anonymized decision that was processed through both Times ExplorAItion and Nucleus Panel. A useful response may be aggregate, redacted, methodology-only, or a statement that no paired workflow has yet been executed. No customer identity, commercially sensitive campaign detail, or personal data is requested.

For any paired example that News UK can disclose, could you clarify:

1. What decision, campaign, proposition, product concept, content concept, or research question was examined?
2. Were the synthetic and human instruments matched in question wording, option set, order, context, and audience definition?
3. How were the synthetic audience and human panel populations defined or recruited, including eligibility, sample size, weighting, geography, timing, and any subgroup fields?
4. What rule determined whether synthetic work preceded, followed, screened for, replaced, or ran independently from direct human research?
5. What response distributions, uncertainty measures, subgroup results, qualitative themes, nonresponse states, or disagreement signals were preserved for each method?
6. When the methods diverged, who resolved the disagreement, under what rule, and could the human findings amend, suspend, or override the synthetic recommendation?
7. Who owned the final decision, what disposition was recorded, and did the comparison alter the selected option, timing, target audience, campaign, product, or implementation state?
8. Were human findings used to update synthetic audience definitions, prompts, retrieval inputs, postprocessing, metrics, benchmarks, policy thresholds, or later model versions?
9. What disclosure, correction, withdrawal, complaint, challenge, appeal, or remedy routes applied to readers whose direct or behavioural data informed the work?
10. What deployment-specific runtime, model, prompt, retrieval, postprocessing, metric, benchmark, policy, validation, and rollback versions governed the paired case?

A concise answer that identifies the workflow state would also be useful. The admissible states include:

```text
one paired workflow exists and can be described
paired use exists but only aggregate methodology can be disclosed
paired use is planned but has not yet been executed
Times ExplorAItion and Nucleus Panel operate as parallel products without a paired workflow
the relevant information is confidential
another News UK team owns the protocol
```

Any response will be preserved with its exact attribution, scope, and limitations. A refusal, confidentiality boundary, routing failure, partial answer, or nonresponse will not be treated as evidence of misconduct, invalidity, concealment, or absence of records.

Please route this request to the News UK commercial insight team, Times Media commercial data team, Nucleus Panel research lead, or Times ExplorAItion methodology owner if another group is better placed to answer.

This draft seeks workflow and methodology provenance only. It does not presume that a matched workflow exists, that synthetic audiences replaced direct human research, that Nucleus Panel validates Times ExplorAItion, or that either method carries binding public authority.
