**State:** prepared, not sent

Subject: February 1990 Nicaragua election-observer records — locator or referral request

Dear Carter Library reference staff,

I am trying to determine the correct repository for records from Jimmy Carter’s February 1990 Nicaragua election-observer delegation. This draft is a locator request only; it has not been sent and authorizes no reproduction order.

Relevant records would include:

- meeting, telephone, briefing, or delegation notes involving Carter, Elliot L. Richardson, ONUVEN, OAS leadership, Daniel Ortega, or Violeta Chamorro;
- contemporaneous allocation of responsibility for the election-night appeal to accept the result;
- copies, references, routing information, or acknowledgments concerning a post-concession Spanish-language letter delivered to Ortega;
- notes bearing on the final joint press conference and any subsequent correction or changed practice.

Please advise whether the Carter Library holds post-presidential 1990 material responsive to this description. If not, a referral to the appropriate Carter Center, National Archives, United Nations, or other repository would be sufficient.

This request does not presume that the records exist or that any person caused the election outcome.
