# Preference Custody laboratory floor v9

Floor v9 composes the qualified ten-control v8 base with PC-11, network dependence, collective formation, and visibility equifinality. The v8 controls remain frozen and executable. PC-11 is added as a separately compiled extension with its own source fixture, proof matrix, refusal rules, hash-linked custody, promotion boundary, and narrower successor frontiers.

The floor remains synthetic. It creates no graph effect, thesis evidence, named-platform contagion finding, real-world preference-change claim, manipulation claim, product ranking, legitimacy verdict, public-authorization finding, or inference of intent.

## Composition

```text
Preference Custody floor v8
PC-01 through PC-10

+

```

The v9 compiler verifies the complete v8 build before admitting PC-11. It records a SHA-256 snapshot of the base floor and a separate snapshot of the extension control. The base is not silently rewritten to accommodate the new control.

### Independent private evidence

Two hundred B-preferrers receive person-level evidence and independently change latent preference, public report, and public action to A. Removing the private evidence restores 60/40.

### Peer cascade

Fifty B-seed people convert from private evidence. Their converted state reaches 150 B-peer people in the next recorded round through a frozen directed edge class. Removing that edge class leaves only the 50 seed conversions and produces 65/35.

This is the fixture’s bounded peer-influence path. It preserves source, temporal order, target identities, and the network edge. It does not establish manipulation, public injury, or collective deliberation.

### Common broadcast

The same two hundred people convert after one institutional broadcast. No peer round occurs. The final latent state matches the cascade world, while the dependence structure is common-source correlation.

### Pluralistic conformity

Two hundred B-preferrers publicly report A under reputational pressure while privately retaining B and continuing to act B. Private anonymous reporting restores 60/40.

### Coordination action

Two hundred B-preferrers continue to prefer and report B but take action A because coordination with the expected majority has instrumental value. Removing the coordination payoff restores the action distribution to 60/40.

### Ranking amplification

The population retains the original 60/40 latent, report, and action distributions. A visibility function weights A content at 4.0 and B content at 1.5, creating an 80/20 surfaced sample. Uniform ranking restores 60/40.

## Dependence is not deliberation

PC-11 separates network-mediated response from collective reason exchange and binding group disposition.

```text
cascade ≠ deliberation
conformity ≠ consent
coordination ≠ negotiated agreement
ranking visibility ≠ population preference
surfaced majority ≠ public authorization
```

A collective-deliberation finding requires attributed participants, reciprocal reason exchange, proposal history, amendment, agenda control, preliminary and final dispositions, and a consequence bound by the group’s rule. Those objects remain a successor frontier.

## Source separation

A real network-mediated claim must distinguish:

```text
baseline homophily and selection
independent private evidence
common institutional or media exposure
ordered peer exposure
coordination incentive
reputational conformity
ranking or sampling transformation
```

Correlated responses do not identify peer influence without source separation. Similar connected people may have selected one another before the observation. A common broadcast can synchronize change without peer transmission. A ranked surface can manufacture apparent consensus without changing any person-level state.

## Frontier transition

The broad v8 frontier:

## Publication boundary

Floor v9 can refuse unsupported interpretation across eleven synthetic controls. It cannot grant publication authority to a real-world peer-contagion, collective-preference, manipulation, platform, legitimacy, or public-authorization claim. A bounded network path requires temporal source and graph custody. A collective or constitutional finding requires additional deliberation, disposition, and authority evidence.
