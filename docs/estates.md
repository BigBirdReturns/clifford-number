# Estate model

**Status:** current ontology.

## Definition

An **estate** is a durable domain corpus with its own:

- jurisdictional and institutional scope;
- source-family inventory;
- identity and denominator rules;
- raw acquisition frontier;
- case and report projections;
- correction and preservation obligations; and
- explicit boundaries on what cross-estate joins can mean.

An estate is not one company, place, program, annual cohort, investigation, research track, or report.

The project uses **estate slice** for those bounded objects.

```text
source family
→ raw record or denominator
→ estate slice
→ durable estate
→ typed case
→ structured report
→ independent review
→ approved publication
```

The estate layer does not become a new factual ledger. Claims, receipts, events, relations, surfaces, participation, and review state remain in their current canonical sources. An estate is a custody and acquisition boundary over those sources.

## Compatibility correction

The first two acquisition packages used `estate_id` for twenty bounded site, program, agency, chamber, and cohort packets.

Those identifiers remain stable for reproducibility, but the macro registry interprets them as `slice_id`.

This keeps the data and tests intact while correcting the language going forward.

## Fog work order

Estate-level fog is triaged by what can change the interpretation.

### 1. Corpus denominator and nulls

Before interpreting recurrence, freeze the full estate universe and retain nonparticipants, failed paths, unsuccessful bidders, no-award companies, and no-match entities.

### 2. Legal and decision instruments

Retrieve the operative agreement, decision record, rights schedule, evaluation, order, lease, bond, rule, recusal, waiver, or judgment—not another announcement summarizing it.

### 3. Identity-resolved transactions

Resolve people, legal entities, funds, awards, contracts, properties, filings, committees, and offices independently before joining them.

### 4. Temporal order

Separate acquisition, appointment, disclosure, decision, obligation, outlay, performance, departure, investment, and later outcome dates.

### 5. Source custody and contradiction

Preserve bytes, locators, amendments, negative records, disputes, reversals, and legal dispositions. Source disappearance is a custody state, not evidence of motive.

## Cross-estate law

Cross-estate synthesis is allowed only when the join key is explicit and the predicates remain typed.

Examples:

- Dialog listing → separately receipted appointment, contract, transaction, or bounded event;
- UK defense policy → implementing program → supplier denominator → award or no award;
- U.S. defense company → exact legal entity → contract vehicle → obligation/outlay → decision file;
- local development parcel → title chronology → executed agreement → public cash flow → outcome;
- disclosed entity → official registry identity → award/property/filing → temporal screen;
- offshore node → official registration number → precise role → legal disposition;
- public-interest artifact → exact artifact predicate → institutional or court finding.

Name similarity, shared sector, chronological proximity, shared investor, roster co-listing, or repeated appearance cannot complete a cross-estate join by themselves.

## M-02 frontier and game-trail pass

Ten additional macro estates are surveyed under `data/estates/surveys/` and recorded in [`docs/milestones/estate-frontier-game-trails-v1.md`](milestones/estate-frontier-game-trails-v1.md). Each declares a bounded denominator, inclusion and exclusion rules, null controls, official source routes, stopping rules, overlap predicates, residual fog, and a decisive next acquisition. Their source routes remain candidate-only until acquisition; the survey pass records zero acquired rows.

The complete 24-estate trail plane is compiled from 35 preserved legacy trails, canonical custody, source-route infrastructure, and one estate-root summary per estate into `build/estate-game-trails/` and published through `gametrails/`. The pass evaluates every preserved trail against every estate and stops each current trail at the first lawful terminal class:

- typed object overlap;
- shared canonical custody;
- shared source infrastructure;
- bounded non-overlap; or
- unresolved boundary.

An overlap class routes evidence review. It is never a relationship, influence claim, or causal finding. A bounded non-overlap is only a statement about the declared current corpus.
