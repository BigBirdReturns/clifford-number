# Anduril reporter aperture

The Anduril delivery has two layers for two different reading tasks.

1. `briefs/anduril-access-ownership.html` is the finite front door. It uses ordinary reporting language, a two-axis orientation, six bounded case threads, and a compact source index. It does not ask a reader to understand the Clifford compiler or the term “AI.”
2. `#case/anduril-access-ownership` is the evidence layer. It exposes the typed events, verified and review-required claims, claim-level receipts, qualifications, non-causal relations, and the records that would prove or defeat the stronger proposition.

The axes are **decision-gate proximity** and **stack-ownership depth**. They are editorial navigation dimensions, not scores. Lobbying, PAC activity, former-official expertise, acquisitions, temporal sequence, and product integration do not establish influence, causation, coordination, control, wrongdoing, or unlawful procurement.

## Evidence interaction contract

The brief may orient the reader, but the case ledger controls factual status. From the case page, each claim opens the existing evidence dialog with its public receipt links, evidence class, causal status, dates, and qualification. A verified claim must use a public official-class receipt; company acquisition statements and editorial axis placement remain review-required. Relations preserve documented sequence or institutional attribution without upgrading either into a causal claim.

The browser-visible briefing is not embedded into the standalone release. The standalone still includes the compiled evidence case and its public receipts; it omits the external briefing link rather than producing a broken portable URL.

The user-supplied Anduril Guardian Reporting Bundle, Revision 3 remains provenance for the synthesis and records roadmap. Its content hash is preserved in the case receipt registry, but it is not treated as independent support for a real-world claim.
