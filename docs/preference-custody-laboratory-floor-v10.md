# Preference Custody laboratory floor v10

Floor v10 composes the qualified eleven-control v9 base with PC-12, deliberative reason exchange, vote, and summary equifinality. The v9 controls remain frozen and executable. PC-12 is admitted through a separate fixture, proof matrix, custody chain, refusal ledger, promotion boundary, and frontier transition.

The floor remains synthetic. It creates no graph effect, thesis evidence, named-institution deliberation finding, consensus finding, collective-agreement finding, manipulation claim, legitimacy verdict, public-authorization finding, or inference of intent.

## Qualified reason-responsive process

The reciprocal world preserves one joined chain:

```text
B_SHIFT challenges A0 with evidence
→ A_CORE responds with safeguard evidence
→ the response is incorporated into A1
→ B_SHIFT endorses A1
→ private preference changes
→ the ballot tracks the changed position
→ the exact ballot is published
```

This world qualifies a synthetic deliberative process because the challenge, response, evidence, uptake, amendment, private transition, ballot, and publication are all receipted. It does not establish binding public authority, consensus, collective agreement, or legitimacy.

## Information, conformity, strategy, and summary

The other worlds establish the required separations.

```text
independent evidence can change preference without deliberation
one-way expert briefing can change preference without reciprocity
public conformity can change the ballot without changing private preference
strategic logroll can change the focal vote without changing focal preference
facilitator or model synthesis can change publication without changing the ballot
```

A final 80 percent published disposition therefore identifies neither private preference nor the process that generated the result.

## Deliberation is a process object

PC-12 does not classify a process as deliberative merely because participants were present, spoke, received information, or produced a transcript. The minimum qualified transaction requires:

```text
attributed participant and recipient
ordered challenge
responsive answer
reason or evidence provenance
explicit uptake, rejection, or no-disposition state
versioned proposal or amendment consequence
private-preference transition or preserved disagreement
ballot under a declared rule
faithful publication of the ballot
```

Speaking opportunity is a right. Reason uptake is an event. The two must not be collapsed.

## Ballot, summary, agreement, and authority

The floor preserves four distinct objects:

```text
private preference
public ballot
published summary
binding authority
```

A ballot can diverge from private preference under reputational or strategic incentives. A summary can diverge from the ballot. A majority ballot is not consensus. An amendment is not collective agreement without an applicable disposition, ratification, enforcement, and reopen rule. Reason-responsive deliberation is not public authorization without standing and objective control.

PC-06 continues to govern public standing. PC-07 governs agenda authority. PC-08 governs negotiated package agreement. PC-12 governs the communicative provenance connecting reasons, private positions, ballots, and summaries.

## Frontier transition

The broad v9 frontier:

```text
collective_deliberation_reason_exchange_and_emergent_group_preference
```

is replaced by two narrower unresolved frontiers:

```text
collective_reason_quality_epistemic_diversity_and_information_cascades
deliberative_scale_representation_and_nonparticipant_standing
```

PC-12 qualifies reason-exchange and disposition equifinality. It does not establish that the reasons are true, diverse, nonredundant, representative, or sufficient for people excluded from the process.

## Publication boundary

Floor v10 can refuse unsupported interpretation across twelve synthetic controls. It cannot grant publication authority to a real-world consensus, deliberation, collective-agreement, manipulation, legitimacy, or public-authorization claim. A bounded reason-responsive process requires turn, reason, uptake, proposal, private-position, ballot, summary, counterfactual, consequence, and authority custody.
