# Preference Custody laboratory floor v23

Floor v23 composes the qualified twenty-four-control v22 base with PC-25, criterion independence, external replication, population transport, score-use governance, decision feedback, and validation succession. The v22 floor remains a frozen executable base. PC-25 enters as a separately compiled extension with its own fixture, proof summary, refusal ledger, promotion boundary, and hash-linked custody.

The floor remains synthetic. It creates no graph effect, thesis evidence, named-validator audit, criterion finding, score-use authorization, consent finding, discrimination finding, coercion finding, manipulation conclusion, real-world causal claim, legitimacy verdict, public-authorization finding, or inference of intent.

## Composition

```text
Preference Custody floor v22
```

The v23 compiler validates and hashes the complete v22 build before admitting PC-25. The extension cannot silently weaken the existing instrument, comprehension, choice, release, allocation, remedy, provenance, deliberation, network, or authority controls.

### Same-team criterion presented as external

The decision owner designs, collects, adjudicates, scores, stores, and publishes the criterion. Two named internal units are presented as replications, but both belong to the same legal entity and lack independent data and publication control.

```text
criterion institutional center       1
replication legal entities           1
blind adjudication                    false
nonindependent criterion records    100
```

An external-sounding team or unit name does not establish independent design, data, analysis, or publication authority.

### Post-decision outcome used as criterion

Independent organizations collect and analyze the outcome, but the criterion is observed only after the score-informed decision and implementation. The decision changes the outcome later used to validate the score.

```text
criterion timing                     post_implementation
decision-feedback observations     100
published coefficient                0.80
independent predecision reference    0.30
```

Organizational separation does not repair post-treatment timing.

### Shared labels, features, records, and answer material

The validation organization is legally separate, but the criterion derives from the score and shares its data estate.

```text

```

A separate analyst can reproduce the score’s own construction rather than provide an independent criterion.

### Independent but construct-mismatched proxy

The proxy is independently designed, collected, adjudicated, and replicated. It predicts payment retention rather than independent operative choice quality.

```text

```

Organizational independence and statistical agreement do not establish construct relevance.

### Nonrepresentative external replication

Two independent validators reproduce the result among forty selected high-literacy volunteers. The deployed population contains one hundred people, and sixty people fall outside the supported transport surface.

```text

deployment population               population-v1
transported people                  40
selection-bias count                60
```

External replication is not transport to the deployed population.

### Valid prediction used for an unsupported consequential purpose

The score is validated for advisory screening but executed as a binding consequential release choice.

Validity for one purpose does not authorize a different consequential use.

## Criterion timing and decision feedback

The criterion must be situated relative to scoring, intervention, decision, implementation, appeal, and outcome.

```text
predecision criterion
≠ post-decision outcome

independent outcome
≠ outcome caused by the score-informed action

decision agreement
≠ independent validation when the score helped determine the decision
```

A real packet must preserve treatment leakage, feedback, post-treatment bias, collider state, and every path by which the score or decision changed the later criterion.

## Construct relevance

A criterion may be independent and reproducible while measuring the wrong object. Payment retention, compliance, engagement, absence of appeal, or institutional agreement may correlate with a score without representing the intended affected-person outcome.

The required ledger therefore preserves:

```text
criterion construct
intended interpretation
reference outcome
proxy limitations
relationship to the score use
independent adjudication
```

## Replication and transport

Replication independence and population transport are separate questions.

```text
replication count
≠ independent replication

external replication
≠ representative replication

representative validation sample
≠ transport after geography, channel, time, or subgroup change
```

The transport packet preserves validation and deployment populations, eligibility, geography, time, channel, subgroup coverage, selection, weighting, estimator, uncertainty, and sensitivity.

## Score-use governance

The floor distinguishes the score’s validated purpose from its approved and executed action.

```text
valid prediction
≠ authority for every decision purpose

advisory screening
≠ binding disposition

public use label
≠ executed use receipt
```

A consequential use requires the exact decision object, threshold, abstention rule, human authority, override, affected population, implementation consequence, and approved-use boundary. Expansion into a different purpose requires a new validation transaction.

## Publication boundary

The admissible laboratory statement is that one public 0.80 criterion coefficient, two replications, one hundred decisions, one approved consequential use, and one `externally_validated` status can coexist with complete assurance, same-team validation, post-decision criteria, shared-data contamination, proxy criteria, transport failure, unsupported score use, or stale validation lineage.
