# Counter-Selector and Asymmetric Advantage Hunt

## Purpose

Build a role-neutral recognition and acquisition system for people, capabilities, alternatives, interfaces, and correction mechanisms systematically underpriced or discarded by status-dependent selectors.

The program begins from a bounded observation already preserved in the Distributed Counterpower Aversion field hypothesis:

```text
an outsider's social metadata may predict low legitimacy
while the work produces high institutional relevance
```

That mismatch is a routing signal. It is not proof of hidden genius, persecution, collective hostility, coordination, or suppression.

The Counter-Selector asks:

> Who repeatedly produces more valid movement, recovery, custody, and cross-domain understanding than their title, resources, biography, or institutional standing would predict?

The companion Asymmetric Advantage Hunt asks:

> Which people, options, components, rights, interfaces, failed paths, and correction mechanisms become unusually cheap or valuable because dominant selectors systematically fail to recognize, preserve, compare, or govern them?

## Governing distinction

```text
low status + confidence
!= hidden capability

unusual biography
!= cross-domain competence

AI use
!= authorship or fraud

rejection
!= proof of value

recognition lag
!= appropriation

status-output mismatch
= acquisition requirement only
```

Every candidate remains graph-inert until independently reviewed against artifacts, controls, alternative explanations, and falsifiers.

## The operational residue

The Counter-Selector does not search for people who describe themselves as polymaths, visionaries, outsiders, dragons, founders, or geniuses. It searches for repeatable operational residue.

### CS-01 — Support-adjusted surplus

Observed output materially exceeds what the available support, runway, title, authority, staff, or institutional sponsorship would ordinarily predict.

This must not romanticize poverty, instability, disability, caregiving burden, or unsupported work. Support is a production variable, not a moral discount. The question is whether capability remains visible after support and state load are made explicit.

### CS-02 — Cross-domain transfer

The same governing operation appears across different domains:

```text
locate the actual gate
-> acquire local mechanics
-> separate claim from receipt
-> compose available resources
-> produce a valid state transition
-> preserve custody
-> hand off cleanly
```

Breadth without a stable operation does not qualify.

### CS-03 — Exception handling

The candidate becomes more legible when the script fails. They detect propagation, identify the decisive intervention, preserve the viable branches, and restore a recoverable state without requiring permanent control.

### CS-04 — Custody

Other people entrust the candidate with accounts, data, equipment, systems, unfinished work, dependent people, or reputationally sensitive tasks because the object returns usable, inspectable, and without hidden damage.

### CS-05 — Model elasticity

A documented contradiction can enter the candidate's model without retaliation, explanation mutation, secrecy, permanent rupture, or prosecution of the critic. The candidate can state that criticism hurt while preserving the relationship and improving the object.

### CS-06 — Governed capacity

The candidate allocates attention and force according to stakes. They do not perform at maximum merely because an audience, status contest, or opportunity for domination exists.

### CS-07 — Non-zero-sum orientation

Another capable person expands the available system. The candidate can teach, borrow, lend, route, credit, and collaborate without requiring permanent authorship or superiority.

### CS-08 — Epistemic restraint

The candidate distinguishes observation, interpretation, hypothesis, receipt, non-link, and falsifier. Confidence does not erase the evidence ceiling.

## The negative filter

The following patterns are explicit false-positive risks:

```text
every failure proves persecution
every success proves superiority
criticism becomes betrayal
projects remain secret enough to preserve imaginary value
AI is used as an oracle about motives or destiny
artifacts cannot be handed off
no failed path is preserved
other people exist mainly as audiences, obstacles, or validation sources
autonomy is demanded for self while obedience is demanded from others
```

A candidate displaying these patterns may still possess useful skill. They do not qualify as a repair-capable Counter-Selector partner without independent controls.

## Bounded field test

The first test is not an interview about intelligence or ambition. It is a real, bounded, unfamiliar problem with a valid destination, incomplete information, and one deliberate disruption.

The candidate must identify:

```text
the actual gate
the missing inputs
the prohibited assumptions
the execution plan
the proof object
the evidence ceiling
the rollback or recovery path
the clean handoff state
```

After work begins, one premise changes, one source is contradicted, one expected tool disappears, or one dependent actor is shown to bear a previously hidden cost.

A strong result preserves the still-valid work, localizes damage, updates the model, and returns the project to a recoverable state. A final independent operator must be able to continue safely from the handoff.

## Asymmetric Advantage Hunt

The hunt converts selector error into governed inventory.

```text
status-biased recognition
-> discarded person, option, component, or right
-> preserved evidence
-> independent proof
-> bounded acquisition or partnership
-> working integration
-> useful product or counterpower
-> durable field evidence
```

### AAH-01 — Recognition residuals

Find high-output, low-legibility operators before institutional prestige makes them expensive.

### AAH-02 — Repair-capable partner graph

Prioritize collaborators who can expose unfinished work, survive accurate criticism, correct openly, preserve custody, and return with another object.

### AAH-03 — Lost option set

Recover failed bidders, public alternatives, abandoned prototypes, displaced maintainers, rejected architectures, and capabilities denied comparable support.

### AAH-04 — Boring seams

Acquire and automate the maintenance, evidence, documentation, calibration, experiment-log, interoperability, recovery, and handoff layers that glamorous systems chronically neglect.

### AAH-05 — Public contribution and residual rights

Trace public capital, demand, validation, infrastructure, data, personnel, and risk into retained ownership, audit, transition, value, and exit rights.

### AAH-06 — Interface sovereignty

Locate practical control in schemas, ontologies, identity models, APIs, permissions, workflows, accreditation, configuration, audit history, and operating knowledge.

### AAH-07 — Repairability timing

Date the weakening or removal of notice, evidence access, pause, burden shift, reversal, substitution, co-governance, value recovery, custody, and exit before visible failure arrives.

### AAH-08 — Institutional locomotion

Convert current records into valid movement through nomination, grant, procurement, application, sponsor, technical-evidence, and partner-capacity gates without inventing missing qualifications.

### AAH-09 — Claim-to-proof conversion

Turn promotional, technical, investment, and policy claims into governing invariants, required parameters, disclosed evidence, missing measurements, test plans, and bounded adjudications.

### AAH-10 — Community amplitude

Integrate small external tools into a high-variety working estate so one demanding adopter exposes onboarding, payment, connector, custody, and failure requirements that ordinary use would reveal slowly.

### AAH-11 — Question 4 counterpower pilot

Move one real but non-adverse workflow through affected-party standing, independent evidence custody, pre-action stop authority, burden shift, correction, and durable handoff.

### AAH-12 — Attention governor

Externalize branch state, bounded watchkeeping, threshold alerts, anomaly queues, retrieval, and resumability while retaining human sovereignty over problem selection, adjudication, and consequence.

## AI-use boundary

The program distinguishes two materially different uses of AI.

### Cognitive instrument

```text
external working memory
retrieval
branch preservation
requirements extraction
comparison
clerical continuity
artifact production
validation assistance
```

The human retains problem selection, evidence ceiling, adjudication, integration, and responsibility.

### Strategic oracle failure

```text
incomplete personal narrative
-> coherent model-generated interpretation
-> coherence mistaken for verification
-> people managed against the interpretation
-> reaction treated as confirmation
-> model consulted again
```

This pattern is a hypothesis surface, not a diagnosis. No personal case, including any private friendship, may be promoted without direct receipts. The Counter-Selector must reject AI-generated certainty about hidden motives, destiny, superiority, or persecution.

## Priority and recognition ledger

For claims about early discovery, institutional domestication, recognition lag, or appropriation, preserve:

```text
first bounded formulation
first artifact
people shown
initial objections
required proof
later category language
institutional adoption
attribution change
remaining difference from prior art
```

The ledger must distinguish independent parallel discovery, genuine prior art, later recognition of the same mechanism, and appropriation after direct exposure.

### CS-W05 — Integrate Question 4

Select one lawful, non-adverse workflow where a real gateholder, affected-party standing holder, and independent evidence custodian can test binding correction.

## Closure rule

The Counter-Selector does not close when it has found people who resemble the founder. It closes only when a role-neutral recognition method repeatedly finds useful capability before conventional selection, rejects charismatic false positives, survives independent handoff, and produces better outcomes without creating another protected center.
