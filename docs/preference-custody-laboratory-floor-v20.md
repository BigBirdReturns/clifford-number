# Preference Custody laboratory floor v20

Floor v20 composes the qualified twenty-one-control v19 base with PC-22, choice architecture, exit authorship, assent, payment sequence, clawback, and reversal equifinality. The v19 controls remain frozen and executable. PC-22 is admitted through a separate fixture, proof summary, custody chain, refusal ledger, promotion boundary, and frontier transition.

The floor remains synthetic. It creates no graph effect, thesis evidence, named-interface audit, consent or coercion finding, manipulation conclusion, enforceability opinion, real-world causal claim, legitimacy verdict, public-authorization finding, or inference of intent.


## Qualified neutral path


This world qualifies a synthetic neutral and self-authored choice process. It does not establish binding public authority, legal enforceability, legitimacy, or a real-world consent finding.

## Recorded choice and latent intention

The binding-default world preserves twenty intended exits while recording only five completed exits. Eighty records arise through the default rather than an active person-authored transaction.


The same 95/5 record can therefore represent five intended exits or twenty intended exits. The public disposition alone does not identify the latent choice state.

## Urgency and repeated confirmation

The urgency world preserves a one-day deadline, countdown, and expiring banner. The repeated-confirmation world requires one confirmation for binding and four for exit. Both preserve the same recorded outcome as the positive world.

```text
formal option availability
≠ equivalent completion probability

completed administrative record
≠ neutral choice architecture
```

## Benefit bundling and independent assent

The bundled world preserves one hundred self-authored records and ninety-five explicit actions. It still fails the independent-assent control because payment access and release assent are nonseverable.

```text
person-authored action
≠ independent assent

explicit click
≠ independent choice when benefit access is conditioned on release
```

The relevant custody object is the complete bundle, including the benefit, condition, severability, alternative route, and consequence of refusal.

## Representative and operator substitution

The existence of a person-level record does not identify the actor who authored it. Authorship requires actor identity, mandate, scope, conflict, action, ratification, and correction custody.

## Payment timing, repayment, and clawback

The payment-first world pays all one hundred people before the choice process. Retention is treated as assent, and exit requires repayment.


Payment can therefore alter the exit path even when the notice and operative release remain unchanged.

## Cooling-off and reversal
