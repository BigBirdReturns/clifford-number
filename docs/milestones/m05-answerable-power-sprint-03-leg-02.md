# M-05 Sprint 03 Leg 02 — administrative benefits and automated burden

## Domain under test

```text
K.W. v. Armstrong
Idaho Medicaid developmental-disability budget tool

Australian Robodebt
income-compliance and administrative-debt scheme
```

The test does not merge their protections. K.W. is a bounded positive control for a litigation-enabled pre-action stay and burden shift. Robodebt is a negative control for missing ordinary pre-action protections and a positive control for later evidence reconstruction, reversal, reform, settlement, and compensation.

## R1–R7 stress result

### R1 — notice

K.W. supplies court-required individualized notice after litigation. Robodebt had notifications, but the current record does not show ordinary notices that made the transformed income and debt claim independently reproducible before collection.

```text
result:
candidate mechanism supported
ordinary pre-action operation not established
```

### R2 — inspection and correction

Both systems became reconstructable primarily through litigation, discovery, disputes, and compulsory inquiry.

```text
strong retrospective control
weak ordinary pre-action access
```

### R3 — pause and burden shift

K.W. is the positive control:

```text
prior budgets restored
reductions blocked without adequate notice
services preserved while the state justified and redesigned
```

Robodebt does not show an ordinary automatic stay across the scheme before debt action.

### R4 — remedy

K.W. produced restoration and process protection. Robodebt produced later reversal, reform, settlement, and compensation mechanisms.

The remedies remain incomplete where deletion, payment denominator, residual injury, cost, access, and successor-system implementation are unresolved.

### R5 — substitution

K.W. required a replacement tool, but durable validation and outcome evidence remain open. Robodebt ended, but successor automated-decision architecture and recurrence controls remain open.

### R6 — co-governance

Neither system currently supplies a standing affected-party body with binding authority over model purpose, categories, thresholds, use, exceptions, approval, or termination.

```text
class litigation and inquiry voice
≠ beneficiary or recipient co-governance
```

### R7 — value recovery

Neither system currently closes R7.

```text
refund, settlement, or compensation
= R4 financial remedy when it restores loss

R7 requires:
contribution or injury
→ enforceable residual right
→ realized value or capacity
→ affected-party distribution
```

## Action packet for the domain

Before an avoidable reduction or debt, the institution must identify:

```text
program and legal authority
represented person, guardian, household, or class
source data, dates, provenance, missingness, and corrections
assessment, matching, averaging, imputation, coefficient, variable, and rule
model, spreadsheet, policy, software, and notice version
calculated amount, cap, debt, tolerance, and exception
named user and decision authority
proposed consequence and status quo
less restrictive alternatives
notice delivery and assistance
pause and independent review
retention and downstream systems
refund, compensation, learning, and value ledger
```

The burden rule is explicit:

> The institution must establish the complete action packet and lawful reduction or debt before collection or avoidable service loss. A person is not required to disprove a claim whose material records are missing, inaccessible, transformed without disclosure, or held by the institution.
