## Decisive acquisition contract

Acquire:

- exact recommendation number, wording, version, authors and redlines;
- stakeholder submissions and meeting records affecting it;
- ministerial acceptance, rejection, or modification;
- appointment memorandum, criteria, candidate denominator and decision-makers;
- conflict assessment, mitigation, recusal and access records;
- one bounded advice-to-decision-to-implementation-to-outcome chain;
- the alternative policy or implementation path.

Likely custodians include DSIT, the Prime Minister's Office, the Cabinet Office, relevant ministerial private offices, and the Action Plan secretariat.

## Falsifier

The strong hypothesis weakens or terminates if the complete file shows that the adviser role was independently designed and selected, Clifford did not author or materially shape the relevant recommendation, conflict controls operated effectively, ministers or civil servants retained the material decision, and Clifford's advice did not materially alter the bounded outcome.

## Stop rule

Terminate at `actor_specific_institutional_translation` unless the record closes:

```text
recommendation authorship
→ institutional acceptance
→ appointment selection
→ decision-specific advice
→ implementation
→ observed consequence
```

Chronology, prestige, access, or a same-day publication and appointment cannot substitute for those records.
