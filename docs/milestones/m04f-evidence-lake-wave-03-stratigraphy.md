## Stratigraphic law

The release distinguishes:

```text
suspended_fog
unsettled_sediment
settled_sediment
compacted_stratum
bedrock
fault_line
```

Bedrock means only that a bounded proposition is anchored by unusually durable evidence such as compulsory adjudication, direct affected-person voice, independently reconstructable custody, operated counterfactuals, or compulsory remedy. It never promotes the total theory.

Fault lines are preserved rather than repaired away. A bounded non-link or falsifier constrains the map and prevents silent bridge-building.

## Waterline

Each system is tested for seven vertical joins:

```text
inference authority and threshold
direct voice
evidence custody
material consequence
prospective stay or compulsory remedy
evaluated or operated counterfactual
error, learning, benefit, and compensation
```

The new waterline is not a count of supportive records. It is the depth at which those joins become complete enough for bounded human review.
