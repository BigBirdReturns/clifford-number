## First triad result

### Matt Clifford

The official record supports a bounded story of institutional translation across AI policy review, international convening, advisory implementation, and ARIA governance. It does not yet support ministerial sovereignty, procurement authority, or company-specific market shaping.

### Palantir

The source set connects corporate governance, Ontology and action architecture, the Homes for Ukraine Foundry deployment, one completed in-house public replacement, and a bounded NGC2 integration role. It does not support universal dependency or customer-purpose control.

### Anduril

The source set connects TALOS trials and Phase 3, NGC2 prototype and data-baseline roles, an industrial-base award, and public-customer integration. It preserves proposal, prototype, acceptance, operation, effect, and enterprise scale as different states.

## Source ecology

The first proven M-04G orbit completed all twelve basin jobs but reached only 53 of 96 selected public routes. Sprint 01 therefore separates:

```text
execution_complete
coverage_healthy
evidence_sufficient
```

Only the first was true.

The repair contract targets host-aware rate limiting, stable official interfaces, explicit fallback chains, structured failure taxonomy, stronger basin-health thresholds, and continued privacy protection for direct voice.
