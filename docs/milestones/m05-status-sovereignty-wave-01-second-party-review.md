# M-05 · SSC-W01 separated second-party review campaign

`SSC-W01-SPR01` installs the append-preserving external-review infrastructure required after the SG-08 targeted-acquisition checkpoint.

## Infrastructure result

The campaign binds an exact fourteen-packet registry to the maintainer-review and targeted-acquisition release digests. Every packet remains unassigned. Candidate, invitation, failure, invalid-submission, review, withdrawal, and supersession states remain append-preserving.

The public intake route reveals only campaign law, packet identifiers, and release digests. Held evidence and publication-blocked reports remain outside the Pages artifact.

## Authority boundary

External participation is required to claim external review. It is not permission for the project to reason. No invitation, nonresponse, refusal, acceptance, or unvalidated receipt counts as second-party review.

A valid review can recommend or disagree. It cannot rewrite the canonical disposition, adjudicate itself, clear publication, create a graph edge, establish a system-level finding, or advance adoption.

SG-08 remains the current stable-ground checkpoint because this campaign changes review infrastructure, not empirical or adoption authority.
