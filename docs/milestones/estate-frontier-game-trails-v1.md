Each survey declares:

- the durable-estate boundary and why existing estates cannot own the corpus cleanly;
- one bounded denominator unit with inclusion and exclusion rules;
- jurisdiction and time-window requirements;
- required null and unsuccessful controls;
- official or primary-public source routes and stopping rules;
- candidate overlap predicates;
- dominant residual fog;
- decisive output and next acquisition;
- `raw_records_acquired: 0` for this survey pass.

The zero is deliberate. M-02 prepares acquisition; it does not relabel locators as acquired evidence.

## Terminal classes

### Typed object overlap

The trail reaches another estate through the same explicitly typed person, organization, legal entity, vehicle, property, award, instrument, system, or other controlled identifier.

Examples preserved by the pass include:

- Peter Thiel across the convening-context and Palantir state-channel trails;
- the Downtown Arcadia Improvement Association across formation and title trails;
- Pallas Ventures and Morpheus Space across the Sally Donnelly and Tony DeMartino trails.

The repeated identifier routes review. It does not transfer every claim or interpretation between the trails.

### Shared custody overlap

Two estates already reference the same canonical case, slice, track, receipt, or other controlled custody object. This is a reversible crosswalk, not evidence that the subjects coordinated.

### Shared source infrastructure

Two estates depend on the same source family—such as SEC EDGAR, USAspending, Companies House, PACER, or FERC eLibrary. This identifies reusable acquisition infrastructure. It does not create a real-world relationship.

### Bounded non-overlap

The current declared trail reaches no second estate under the controlled predicates. This is a result about the present corpus and search boundary, not proof of absence.

### Unresolved boundary

The trail cannot lawfully terminate because its evidence or ownership predicate remains outside the compiled public estate corpus. M-02 preserves two such legacy trails rather than forcing a match.

## Game-Trail Aperture

`gametrails/index.html` publishes the pass through four addressable modes:

1. **Matrix** — a 24 × 24 directed estate plane showing typed, custody, and shared-source trail counts.
2. **Trails** — all 308 trails with origin, target, family, terminal-class, and text filters.
3. **Frontier** — one estate-root summary for every estate, with the ten prepared surveys exposing their denominator, null controls, official source plan, zero-record boundary, and next acquisition.
4. **Evidence** — exact typed-token recurrence, unresolved trails, and preserved legacy paths.

The interface supports deterministic deep links, bounded JSON export, print, desktop and mobile layouts, reduced-motion and forced-colors modes, and a standalone no-server artifact.
