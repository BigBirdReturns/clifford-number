# M-05 Sprint 03 Leg 04 — workplace-monitoring co-governance, centralization, and bypass

## Domain under test

```text
BAG 1 ABR 7/15   Facebook visitor-post monitoring
BAG 1 ABR 20/21  Office 365 central tenant
BAG 1 ABR 31/19  email monitoring and deletion limit
BAG 1 ABR 16/23  real-time headset listening without recording
```

This is the strongest current R6 positive control in the answer library. Leg 04 asks whether its practical power survives centralized architecture, cloud updates, purpose drift, representative distance, worker categories outside the constituency, incomplete evidence access, and missing R4, R5, and R7 rights.

## Domain adapter

The Answerable Power Constitution becomes the **Worker Monitoring Constitution**.

It applies to systems capable of observing, inferring, scoring, recording, listening to, classifying, routing, disciplining, scheduling, rewarding, excluding, or otherwise making workers and their labor actionable.

## What survives

### Binding R6 is real

The Facebook visitor-post case demonstrates a bounded constitutional veto:

```text
monitoring-capable function introduced
→ representative body challenges
→ court recognizes co-determination
→ function must be disabled
→ operation may resume only with consent
   or substitute conciliation decision
```

That is not consultation, transparency, or feedback. It is binding authority over whether the covered monitoring use may operate.

### Technical architecture can move governance

The Office 365 case demonstrates that a centralized and technically indivisible tenant can move competence from a local council to a central works council.

```text
formal co-governance survives
but
constitutional distance may increase
```

APC-WORK-01 therefore requires:

- proof of technical indivisibility;
- local petition and information rights;
- minority protection;
- accountable selection, removal, conflict, expertise, and resources;
- delegated local authority where technically possible;
- independent review when the central body is captured, remote, or absent.

### Capability defeats one bypass

The headset case demonstrates that monitoring does not cease to be monitoring because the system acts in real time and records nothing.

```text
monitoring capability
≠ stored-record requirement
```

This is a strong functional-equivalence control against relabeling surveillance as ephemeral, passive, optional, unrecorded, safety-related, or merely available.

## R1–R7 stress result

### R1 — notice

Representative process is visible, but ordinary pre-procurement and pre-update worker notice is not established by the frozen cases. Centralized authority also creates a local-communication problem.

### R2 — evidence access

The courts could identify monitoring capability and legal competence. Actual tenant configurations, modules, telemetry, data flows, agreements, users, downstream copies, worker correction, vendor support access, and deletion remain incomplete.

### R3 — pause

The strongest result is the Facebook function-level cessation until consent or substitute decision. The ordinary denominator—systems paused before introduction versus litigated after violation—remains open.

### R4 — remedy

Function-level cessation is observed. Complete person-level correction, deletion, disciplinary reversal, compensation, downstream propagation, residual injury, and vendor compliance are not.

### R5 — substitution

The Facebook page could continue with the monitoring function disabled. That is functional narrowing, not a completed worker-governed migration or public-capacity exit. Comparable exit evidence is absent for the central tenant, email, and headset systems.

### R6 — co-governance

```text
observed:
binding collective authority over monitoring introduction and use

not observed:
direct individual veto
platform-purpose control
complete local proximity
universal workforce coverage
```

### R7 — value recovery

No frozen case joins worker labor, data, risk, surveillance burden, productivity, error, or model learning to ownership, royalty, IP, compensation, savings distribution, worker capacity, or another residual right.

## Worker-monitoring action packet

Before introduction or material update, the employer must disclose:

```text
employer, establishment, representative body, vendor, system, date, and purpose
covered and excluded workers and locations
modules, devices, cloud services, features, defaults, configurations, and integrations
data, content, communications, telemetry, metadata, location, biometric, productivity, safety, and behavior signals
models, analytics, rules, scores, thresholds, alerts, recommendations, and discipline pathways
users, supervisors, vendor support, recipients, access, and cross-border transfers
monitoring capability whether active, passive, optional, recorded, or unrecorded
worker and representative notice, negotiation, consent, dissent, and assistance
competent governance body and the technical basis for that level
individual grievance, correction, review, and non-retaliation rights
conciliation, pause, safety exception, expiry, review, and enforcement
retention, deletion, downstream copies, and proof of compliance
alternatives, minimization, non-monitoring methods, and worker-governed options
migration, continuity, documentation, and successor systems
disciplinary reversal, compensation, injury, and remedy funding
labor, data, risk, productivity, learning, savings, IP, ownership, and distribution ledger
```

A material update, new telemetry source, new model, new threshold, new integration, new recipient, new purpose, new disciplinary use, or successor tool retriggers R1, R2, R3, and R6.
