## Functional differentiation is explicit

The Army identifies distinct functions:

- Anduril leads the NGC2 common data baseline and 4th Infantry Division full-stack implementation.
- Palantir partners with Anduril to provide Foundry within an edge-to-cloud data mesh alongside Lattice and associated deployment tools.
- Raft supplies data and services registries, transformation tools, and federation.
- Lockheed Martin leads the 25th Infantry Division full-stack implementation.
- Additional companies provide applications, data, infrastructure, networking, transport, algorithms, hardware, private 5G, edge compute, and electromagnetic-spectrum integration.

This clears T2 in one named program. It does not establish common ownership, private governance, or a system beyond NGC2.

## Palantir–Anduril coordination is no longer adjacency

The Army's June 2026 common-baseline announcement names a specific integrated product relationship:

```text
Anduril Lattice
+ Palantir Foundry
+ software deployment tools
→ edge-to-cloud data mesh
```

The 2025 prototype agreement separately required Team Anduril and partners to deliver an integrated command-and-control suite spanning hardware, software, applications, and a common data layer.

This clears T4 and BRG-09 for human review in the NGC2 lane. The decisive next records are the architecture, interfaces, schemas, data lineage, identity and access controls, logs, tasking, acceptance, pricing, rights, failure modes, and replacement provisions.

## Feedback altered requirements and the baseline

The Army states that:

- Project Convergence Capstone 5 feedback and technical observations informed further iteration and an updated Capability Characteristics of Need;
- 4th and 25th Infantry Division feedback informed procurement and scaling;
- ten months of operational training data and feedback informed the June 2026 common data layer baseline;
- the program was moving from prototyping toward continuous delivery.

That supplies a dated T6 chain:

```text
operational use
→ soldier and commander feedback
→ requirement and architecture iteration
→ baseline decision
→ procurement and scaling
```

## A bounded operating layer exists

The Army describes NGC2 as an ecosystem and technology stack integrating previously siloed systems. It identifies Anduril- and Lockheed-led full-stack operational implementation for two divisions and describes movement from prototyping to delivery and implementation of common components.

This supports I3 at the bounded division-implementation level. It does not establish Army-wide replacement, irreversible dependency, or vendor control over command decisions.

## The record reaches a military training effect

During Ivy Sting 1, the Army reports:

```text
integrated data and communications
→ target identification and processing
→ human command and artillery workflow
→ live-fire effect
```

The record explicitly preserves soldiers and commanders as responsible actors and says the tools do not replace soldiers.

This is an L5→L6 training chain. It is not proof of combat effectiveness, autonomous targeting, civilian population consequences, ideological governance, or wrongdoing.

## Competition is real counterevidence

The Army awarded a separate competitive $26 million prototype agreement to a Lockheed Martin-led team after more than 80 proposals in the relevant window. It also maintained a continuous open solicitation and continuous onboarding model. Lockheed remained the 25th Division implementation lead while Anduril led the 4th Division.

Therefore:

```text
NGC2 is a single closed Anduril–Palantir vendor program
  → bounded_non_link
```

This does not prove perfect component substitution or prevent later concentration around the common baseline. Contract-, component-, interface-, and replacement-level competition still requires adjudication.

## The system-wide thesis changes—but does not close

Wave 04 defeats ordinary shared ecology as the complete explanation for the bounded Palantir–Anduril NGC2 lane. The companies are not merely adjacent there; they are named partners inside an Army-selected operating architecture.

The stronger inference still fails:

```text
one exact multi-company operating lane
≠ one founder-controlled system spanning
  companies, funds, banks, policy institutions,
  border systems, civilian administration, and residual rights
```

The correct disposition remains `retained_candidate_only` for system-wide common design.

## Institutional-reproduction alternative

NGC2 currently fits a publicly directed vendor-ecosystem assembly at least as well as private-network reproduction:

- Army-defined and iterated need;
- public prototype awards;
- continuous solicitation;
- more than 80 proposals in one window;
- separate implementation leads;
- soldier and commander feedback;
- continuous onboarding.

T5 therefore remains candidate-only until proposal, team-formation, investor, personnel, evaluation, component-replacement, and baseline-control records show who actually assembled and reproduced the vendor ecosystem.
