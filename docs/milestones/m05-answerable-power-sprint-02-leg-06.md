# M-05 Sprint 02 Leg 06 — First bounded R7 value-recovery candidate

## Candidate

```text
TARP Capital Purchase Program
public capital, preferred returns, warrants,
fair-value disposition and realized program income
```

The candidate asks whether public capital, authority and crisis risk were accompanied by rights that allowed the public to recover not only principal but also a measurable share of recipient recovery.

## Primary transaction

The M&I Bank record closes one exact chain:

```text
$1.715 billion public preferred-share investment
→ $214.6 million in earlier dividends
→ $11.9 million final accrued dividends
→ $3.3 million warrant proceeds
→ preferred-share repayment through Bank of Montreal
→ $1.9448 billion total lifetime taxpayer proceeds
→ $229.8 million stated positive return
```

This is a transaction-specific result. It is not a complete social-welfare or risk-adjusted calculation.

## Program architecture

CPP embedded ex ante public recovery rights:

```text
senior preferred stock or debt
+ five-percent dividends for five years
+ nine-percent dividends thereafter
+ warrants or additional securities
+ fair-market-value repurchase and sale rules
+ bounded executive-compensation and clawback conditions
```

Treasury described the warrants as a mechanism enabling taxpayers to receive additional returns as banks recovered.

## Final program result

Treasury's final CPP table records:

```text
$204.895 billion disbursed
$199.675 billion investment repayments
$5.220 billion write-offs and losses
$27.106 billion received from investments
$5.579 billion Treasury interest expense
$16.307 billion final net income
```

A separate CPP summary reports $226.8 billion in gross recovery against $204.9 billion initially invested.

The difference between gross recovery and net income remains explicit.

## Decisive boundaries

```text
repayment alone
≠ residual value recovery

CPP positive income
≠ TARP-wide profitability

positive financial return
≠ full social-cost recovery

aggregate taxpayer recovery
≠ direct compensation for affected households

preferred stock and warrants
≠ public co-governance

one historical bank program
≠ proven cross-sector transfer
```

CBO's final TARP assessment supplies an essential control: TARP as a whole had an estimated $31 billion subsidy cost, while other financial-institution transactions yielded net gains. The profitable subprogram therefore cannot become the entire bailout story.

## Distributional limit

CPP recovered value to the public fiscal account. It did not itself establish:

- direct compensation to foreclosed homeowners;
- ownership or governance rights for displaced workers or borrowers;
- proportional recovery by communities bearing crisis harms;
- a complete accounting of moral hazard, concentration or opportunity cost.

Public institutional recovery and direct affected-person recovery are different R7 forms.

## Transfer question

The mechanism gives the project a concrete design vocabulary for other public-private programs:

```text
public contribution
→ preferred or residual right
→ milestone and performance conditions
→ clawback
→ fair-value disposition
→ public upside
→ observed recovery
```

But transfer to AI, defense, infrastructure, data or platform finance remains acquisition-open. Each receiving program requires exact legal authority, contribution, rights, performance, failure, recovery, affected-public and counterfactual records.
