## What “canonical” means

In this repository, **canonical** means that a project object has merged to `main` and passed its declared schema, reconstruction, and release gates. It does **not** mean:

- the underlying evidence is true merely because the build passes;
- an interpretation has been independently corroborated;
- a field hypothesis is prevalent;
- an external party reproduced the work;
- a real institution adopted or obeyed it;
- a graph edge, motive, coordination, or common-purpose finding exists.

Authority remains tiered:

```text
source and receipt custody
→ canonical method and projection
→ field hypothesis
→ publication projection
→ external empirical effect
```

A higher narrative layer may not silently promote itself into a lower evidentiary layer.

## Backward alignment

### 1. Actor and network orientation remains discovery, not conclusion

Early mapping of actors, funding, affiliations, personnel, and institutions remains useful for routing. It does not close the project and does not establish coordination. Co-presence, shared investors, repeated vocabulary, compatible products, or career movement remain acquisition signals only.

### 2. Bounded surfaces replaced broad edges

Clifford’s durable contribution is the bounded, dated, receipted participation surface. A valid route or hop establishes only the declared overlap under its temporal and density rules. It does not establish motive, causation, command, coordination, or common purpose.

### 3. Estates and the lake introduced waterlines

