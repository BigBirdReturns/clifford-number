# M-05 Sprint 03 Leg 01 — the Answerable Power Constitution

## The design target

The project has bounded examples of notice, inspection, pause, remedy, exit, co-governance, and value recovery. It has not observed those controls functioning as one constitution.

Leg 01 freezes the candidate answer so it can be attacked rather than continuously rewritten around each result.

```text
R1  timely notice and direct voice
→ R2  material evidence inspection and correction
→ R3  automatic pause and institutional proof burden
→ R4  binding reversal, deletion, restoration, compensation, or prohibition
→ R5  durable substitution and public operating capacity
→ R6  represented-party authority over purpose, category, threshold, use, exception, or termination
→ R7  proportionate recovery of value created through public authority, data, labor, risk, error, injury, and learning
```

## Constitutional roles

```text
represented person
affected-party representative
institutional operator
named decision authority
independent counter-sovereign
evidence custodian
public-capacity steward
public and affected-party value trustee
```

Each role carries rights or duties. No vendor score, model output, analyst, nominal human reviewer, public customer, or representative body may dissolve the responsibility of the named decision authority.

## The action packet

Before an avoidable consequential action, the institution must be capable of identifying:

```text
system and deployment
represented person or population
purpose and lawful authority
source data and provenance
category, relationship, score, inference, or rule
model, software, policy, data, and configuration version
threshold, confidence, tolerance, exception, and override
user, query, alert, recommendation, or workflow action
named decision authority
expected consequence and reversibility
alternative explanations and less restrictive alternatives
records available to the rights holder
notice and contestation window
evidence custody and downstream copies
public and private contribution and benefit ledger
```

Absent a bounded emergency exception, an avoidable consequential action may not proceed without a complete packet accessible to the independent authority and in usable form to the represented person or representative.

## The burden shift

The constitutional inversion is:

```text
before:
institution acts on inference
→ person reconstructs injury later

candidate answer:
institution assembles and preserves the action basis
→ person receives timely access and voice
→ material dispute triggers pause
→ institution proves authority, sufficiency, necessity, proportionality, and lack of a less restrictive alternative
```

Missing evidence under institutional or vendor custody creates a presumption against the actor seeking intervention rather than against the represented person.

## Emergency constitution

Emergency authority remains possible, but must contain:

```text
named human authority
specific imminent harm
minimum necessary action
bounded duration
automatic expiry
prompt independent review
full evidence preservation
notice when safe
restoration and compensation for error
public aggregate reporting
no unrelated secondary use
```

National security, administrative convenience, vendor urgency, missing records, indefinite duration, and retroactive designation are not self-validating exceptions.

## Anti-bypass constitution

The rights follow functional reality rather than labels.

```text
new product name
new vendor
manual workaround
successor system
federated data chain
nominal human approval
cloud update
purpose drift
downstream copy
derived output
outsourcing
cross-border hosting
captured representative body
affiliate or IP vehicle
```

None automatically removes coverage.

## Propositions

The design supports domain testing of four hypotheses:

1. A sequenced constitution is necessary because no single transparency, appeal, exit, co-governance, or value mechanism answers the whole asymmetry.
2. The action packet and automatic pause can shift the burden before avoidable injury.
3. R6 requires binding and accountable representation with direct anti-capture routes.
4. R7 must follow contribution and value through financial and nonfinancial forms.

The claim that the constitution already works is an explicit bounded non-link.

## Works standard

The complete stack must be observed in at least three domains and two jurisdictions. Each domain must include:

```text
deployment-specific direct voice
pre-action timing
material evidence access
independent pause
institutional proof burden
binding remedy and implementation
durability after update, leadership change, vendor change, or successor system
practical exit or co-governance
value recovery and distribution test
null, comparator, falsifier, and stopping rule
```
