# M-04F - Epistemic Jurisdiction, Burden Allocation, and Counterfactual Foreclosure

## Purpose

M-04F tests a constitutional asymmetry that the earlier estates repeatedly encountered but did not isolate:

```text
one actor may convert uncertainty into institutional action
while another must convert injury into admissible proof afterward
```

The estate does not ask whether uncertainty, risk management, professional judgment, or preventive authority are inherently illegitimate. It asks who may use them, who sets the threshold, who controls the evidence, who bears the proof burden, which alternatives are allowed to generate evidence, and who can compel the system to stop.

## Strongest positive control

The Palantir DCGS-A2 litigation is the cleanest current example of preventive counterpower. The Federal Circuit required the Army to satisfy the statutory commercial-item inquiry before award while refusing to require selection of Palantir.

That record supports:

```text
independent authority
-> prospective burden shift
-> acting institution must reconsider
-> alternative receives a lawful evidentiary path
```

It does not support corruption, technical superiority, or later-award causation by itself.

## Material non-link

The NGC2 record includes more than eighty proposals, continuous solicitation, an alternate Lockheed-led divisional implementation, and multiple component providers. That defeats a program-level claim of complete Anduril-Palantir counterfactual foreclosure.

Component-level concentration, common-baseline control, accreditation, interface custody, and practical substitution remain open.

## Current result

The first pass supports this bounded statement:

> Multiple public and private systems allocate prospective inference authority, threshold authorship, evidence custody, exception power, and retrospective proof burdens asymmetrically.

That proposition remains candidate-only until the estate obtains complete rights-holder denominators, repeated cross-system identities, direct represented-person voice, consequence files, observed remedies, and causal error-benefit chains.
