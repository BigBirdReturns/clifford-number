This wave converts the M-04B adversarial self-trial's first triage list into bounded evidence dispositions. It does not broaden the actor universe and does not treat an unresolved proposition as a negative finding.

## 1. Historical externalization is no longer merely atmospheric

GAO's DOD contract-management history identifies a dated transition: DOD reduced its acquisition workforce in the mid-1990s, later rebuilt it amid skill-gap concerns and growing reliance on contractors, and continued to face long-running service-acquisition and operational-contractor-support problems.

This supports the P1 lean-hegemon substrate and a P1-to-P2 capacity transition. It does not yet connect a specific transferred capability, contractor, data architecture, or ownership right continuously through P3, P4, and P5.

## 2. One I2 market-shaping lane is documented

In `Palantir USG, Inc. v. United States`, the Federal Circuit affirmed that the Army had not complied with the commercial-item consideration requirements governing DCGS-A2 and required compliance before award. The court explicitly did not require the Army to choose Palantir.

This is the exact shape required by I2:

```text
actor strategy
→ formal gate challenge
→ adjudicated requirement
→ changed acquisition constraint
```

The later Palantir Capability Drop 1 award must still be analyzed through the complete solicitation, evaluation, source-selection, acceptance, performance, data-rights, and unsuccessful-offeror records. Litigation success cannot substitute for award-causation proof.

## 3. Army–Palantir I3 and C5 now clear the review threshold

The Army's 2025 enterprise agreement consolidated 75 contracts into one purchasing framework with a maximum ten-year, $10 billion ceiling. The ceiling is not an obligation.

The associated J&A text describes:

- a proprietary and highly integrated platform environment;
- extensive customization and integration of critical data sources;
- growing dependence across enterprise and warfighting domains;
- complete rebuilding, migration, reaccreditation, workflow disruption, and institutional-knowledge loss if the platform were replaced;
- sole-source necessity for continuity;
- Government ownership and unlimited rights in Government data and transformations;
- Palantir retention of its commercial software rights.

That is sufficient to route the bounded Army lane to human review for I3 institutional replacement and C5 dependency. It is not sufficient to infer abuse, poor performance, capture, or inability to execute a phased transition.

## 4. ICE ICM independently clears I3 and C5

ICE's limited-sources justification describes Investigative Case Management as its official investigative-case system of record and operational backbone. It combines case management, subject records, external law-enforcement data, analytics, approval workflows, and an enterprise data repository.

The record also states that:

- ICE assessed 42 vendors;
- alternate systems generally required extensive re-architecture and 18-to-24-month transitions;
- replacement risked disruption to ongoing investigations;
- Palantir owns the platform software and source code;
- ICE owns all transactional and derived analytical data and requires return of Government information.

This supports a bounded institutional-replacement and dependency disposition. It also advances H3 through data, subject joining, investigative analytics, and case workflow.

## 5. The coercive-consequence join remains open—but is narrower

DHS OIG provides two useful boundaries:

1. A 2023 audit tied a reporting-control recommendation directly to a field or workflow in ICM.
2. A 2024 audit documented a consequential ICE detention/release risk-assessment process, human officer responsibility, and large numbers of assessments not completed.

The 2024 source does not identify ICM or Palantir as the risk-assessment system. Therefore the repository may not connect Palantir ICM to detention or release outcomes without the missing system, rule, data-flow, interface, audit-trail, and case chronology.

The remaining chain is now exact:

```text
ICM subject and analytics record
→ unknown receiving risk-classification system or workflow
→ human assessment and override
→ detention or release consequence
→ correction, appeal, and audit
```
