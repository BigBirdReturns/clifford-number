# M-05 Sprint 03 Leg 06 — Public value-recovery transfer

## Purpose

Transfer-test the bounded TARP CPP R7 control against public financing of dual-use technology, semiconductors, commercial space, and public data platforms.

```text

→ enforceable residual right
→ exercised and realized value
→ transparent public or affected-party distribution
→ durability through dilution, exit, failure, and successor arrangements
```

## Public Contribution and Residual Rights Covenant

APC-VALUE-01 requires:

1. a bounded public and affected-party contribution ledger;
2. an enforceable equity, preferred, warrant, royalty, license, revenue-share, IP, governance, or contingent-upside right;
3. proof that the right can be exercised in the actual legal and capital structure;
4. observed realization rather than valuation alone;
5. named public and affected-party distribution;
6. complete losses, fees, financing cost, tax, opportunity cost, dilution, write-off, and failure denominators;
7. separate investor governance and affected-party co-governance;
8. durable rights through exit, transfer, and successor structures.

## Hard fault lines

```text
public spending ≠ contribution ledger
repayment ≠ residual upside
valuation ≠ realized public return
public fiscal recovery ≠ affected-party recovery
strategic capability ≠ R7 value recovery
clawback ≠ upside sharing
paper equity ≠ exercisable value
public LP status ≠ democratic or affected-party governance
market creation ≠ ownership
public data ≠ public residual right
```
