# M-05 Sprint 02 Leg 05 — First bounded R6 co-governance candidate

## Candidate

```text
German works-council co-determination
for technical employee-monitoring systems
```

The primary deployment is the employer-operated Facebook visitor-post function adjudicated in **BAG 1 ABR 7/15**.

## Constitutional chain

```text
employees elect representative bodies
→ § 87(1)(6) requires co-determination
→ employer and council must agree on introduction and use
→ if agreement fails, the conciliation committee decides
→ a court may order cessation of unagreed monitoring use
```

## Observed binding result

The Federal Labour Court ordered the employer to disable the Facebook visitor-post function unless:

```text
the combine works council consented
or
the conciliation committee issued a substitute decision
```

The broader Facebook page could continue. The monitoring function could not.

That is a real alteration of system use, not consultation or feedback.

## R6 disposition

```text
highest observed level:
R6

maximum ceiling:
R6_bounded_collective_co_governance_over_monitoring_use

candidate state:
supported_for_human_review
```

The result qualifies because an employee-representative body possessed legally enforceable authority over whether and under what agreed conditions a consequential monitoring function could operate.

## Boundaries

```text
collective representative authority
≠ direct individual veto

control over monitoring introduction and use
≠ control over the platform's full purpose

function-level cessation
≠ data deletion or compensation

German private-sector labor constitution
≠ automatic transfer to other jurisdictions or sectors
```

## Durability controls

Later Federal Labour Court decisions apply the same constitutional mechanism to:

- Microsoft Office 365 central-tenant monitoring capability;
- email-system monitoring applications;
- headset systems that permit supervisor listening even without recording.

Those cases show doctrinal durability while also exposing limits:

- centralized architecture may shift competence from local to central works councils;
- actual works agreements are often nonpublic;
- a council may stop unagreed use without possessing complete third-party deletion rights;
- the mechanism does not create ownership, source-code access, compensation or value recovery.

## What remains missing

1. The actual works agreement or conciliation award governing a comparable modern AI or algorithmic-management system.
2. Configuration-level evidence showing which features, thresholds, logs or uses workers changed.
3. Direct worker testimony about practical access, representation and unequal power.
4. Durability under employer restructuring, outsourcing, platform work and successor systems.
5. A matched non-council deployment showing the counterfactual difference.
6. Deletion, compensation, ownership and value-recovery outcomes.
7. Receiving-jurisdiction records for any transfer claim.
