## Findings waterline

`data/project/allocator-war-findings-waterline.json` assigns every retained SSC observation exactly once to one of six classes:

1. bounded anti-DEI allocator transfer;
2. bounded public conversion into selected private capacity;
3. effective independent counterpower controls;
4. ordinary industrial-policy controls;
5. open selector and strategic-capital denominators; or
6. a refused stronger merit-circularity inference.

The cross-class result is deliberately narrower than the source synthesis. The records support a bounded distinction between eliminating selection and reallocating selector, evidentiary, conversion, and correction authority. They do not establish prevalence, a complete preference monopoly, a white-dominant racial order, or one common purpose across political, administrative, and defense-capital surfaces.
