## Policy-to-product design is now source-bounded

Executive Order 14159 directs total and efficient immigration-law enforcement, prioritization of specified noncitizens, enhanced identification and information sharing, detention, voluntary departure, and efficient or expedited removal.

The recovered ImmigrationOS procurement text describes:

- targeting and enforcement prioritization;
- self-deportation tracking;
- immigration-lifecycle management from identification through removal.

That is a bounded doctrine-to-system-design chain:

```text
official policy
→ agency requirements
→ named software capabilities
```

It does not establish that every feature derives exclusively from the executive order, that the policy or product is unlawful, or that a particular person experienced a consequence because of the system.

## The system crossed from prototype into operations

The continuation record describes ImmigrationOS as an existing system deployed for Enforcement and Removal Operations. The May 2026 USAspending record separately identifies an approximately $86.27 million Palantir award for ERO modernization and operational capability for the enforcement case-management system on the case-management and analytics platform.

This supports a bounded I3 operating-layer and public-resource-flow review. It does not establish technical indispensability, impossible exit, award-level profit, or the number and outcome of enforcement actions.

## The enforcement workflow exists at the design level

The procurement requirements supply a designed chain:

```text
identity and case information
→ targeting and enforcement prioritization
→ self-departure tracking
→ removal-lifecycle support
```

The missing evidentiary chain remains:

```text
source data
→ identity join
→ priority rule or recommendation
→ human decision and override
→ apprehension, detention, departure, or removal
→ error, correction, notice, review, and final disposition
```

Until that chain is independently receipted, the repository may not promote a designed workflow into an actual platform-caused consequence.

## I4 remains below threshold

Wave 05 establishes several I4 predicates:

- an explicit enforcement doctrine;
- software requirements aligned to that doctrine;
- operational ERO funding and deployment;
- a designed path toward consequential enforcement workflows.

It does not yet establish:

- encoded categories and decision rules at operative detail;
- mandatory or practically inescapable use;
- absence of meaningful discretion or override;
- a completed consequential case chain;
- weak correction, appeal, substitution, or exit;
- intent connected to the actual implementation and effect.

Therefore complete I4 coercive ideological governance remains `requires_additional_acquisition`.

## Oversight is active counterpower, not proof of misuse

Members of Congress requested that DHS identify the relevant systems, contractors, data sources, legal authorities, safeguards, sharing practices, accuracy controls, and operational uses.

That is a material H4 inspection intervention. The request does not prove that the expressed concerns are true, that misuse occurred, or that corrective action failed.

## The IRS data-transfer error is an explicit adjacent non-link

A congressional record reports that the IRS acknowledged a flawed transfer process that may have misidentified taxpayers and improperly shared information with ICE.

The bounded record does not identify Palantir or ImmigrationOS as the transfer, matching, receiving, or decision system. The correct disposition is:

```text
documented adjacent federal data-quality failure
+ no current Palantir or ImmigrationOS join
→ bounded_non_link
```

That non-link may be reopened only by operative interface, field-lineage, audit-log, or receiving-system evidence.

## The strongest system-wide inference still fails

Wave 05 establishes one policy-to-enforcement-system-design lane. It does not establish:

- common governance across companies, funds, banks, policy organizations, and agencies;
- a complete Palantir-to-RCA or ImmigrationOS-to-consequence chain;
- weak contestability across affected cases;
- system-wide ideological implementation;
- personal extraction.

The system-wide I4 proposition therefore remains `retained_candidate_only`.
