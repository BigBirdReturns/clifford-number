## Result

ACES had a public nomination route, a seventeen-member balanced roster, one public meeting, and three subcommittee workstreams. The first meeting agenda called for recommendations; the planned second meeting called for subcommittee reports and recommendation finalization. The second meeting was canceled, and ACES was terminated effective February 28, 2025.

```text

completed recommendations recovered:   0
agency dispositions recovered:          0
binding authority classes:              0
```

The available official record therefore supports activity and advisory representation, not a complete recommendation-to-agency-output chain.

## Matched authority control

The National Science Board was selected from the same representation-explicit federal-governance universe. Its official selection rules include broad and equitable representation. Unlike ACES, the NSB establishes NSF policy, approves major awards, and exercises recorded budget-vote authority.

```text
matched binding authority classes: 3
matched affected-party remedy:      not recovered
```

The control shows that representation and binding authority are separately observable. It does not prove that NSB representation guarantees neutrality or that ACES members lacked influence.

## Authority ceiling

This acquisition strengthens the ordinary advisory-representation control and identifies a real binding-governance comparator. It does not establish neutrality, tokenism, suppression, representation-legitimacy, racial hierarchy, effective affected-party counterpower, the complete SSC compact, a graph edge, or publication authority.
