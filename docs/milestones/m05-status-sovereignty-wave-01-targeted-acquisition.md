# SSC-H01 Wave 01 targeted acquisition

`SSC-W01-TA01` is a non-adjudicative acquisition supplement for the three denominator obligations retained by `SSC-W01-MR01`.

It acquires source-addressable records for:

- the 2026 NatSec100 selected roster, methodology, eligibility rules, and explicit assessed nonselections;
- the SBIC Critical Technologies application, approval, first-cohort, leverage, governance, and monitoring denominator;
- the Office of Strategic Capital equipment-finance application universe, selection criteria, named loan instruments, and current program status.

The pass partially repairs all three obligations and closes none. It does not change a reviewed disposition, self-award second-party review or adjudication, promote a complete compact, create a racial-order or prevalence finding, create a graph edge, or clear publication.

The remaining absences are explicit: complete preselection candidate and applicant universes, rejected rows, exact scoring and terms, FOCI and committee custody, disbursement and performance, defaults, substitution, repayment, clawback, and public recovery.
