### Operational validation

The 4th and 25th Infantry Divisions used NGC2 stacks in Project Convergence, Ivy Sting, Ivy Mass and Lightning Surge environments. Official accounts describe commander and Soldier tailoring, feedback, identified deficiencies and increasingly contested conditions.

The record supports operational validation and a selected common baseline. It does not yet establish final production acceptance of the entire stack.

## Human authority

Official Army sources repeatedly state that commanders retain final judgment, Soldiers tailor views and applications, and technology is a decision aid rather than a replacement.

That earns only:

```text
stated and partially observed human judgment
```

It does not yet prove a meaningful veto because the public record does not expose:

- the exact information presented;
- confidence and uncertainty;
- time available;
- practical rejection authority;
- override, abort and termination logs;
- responsibility after an erroneous effect.
