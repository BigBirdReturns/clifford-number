# M-05 Sprint 03 — compose and stress-test the answer to asymmetry

## Starting waterline

Sprint 02 established bounded observed examples at every level from R1 through R7:

```text
R1  notice and voice
R2  inspection and correction
R3  pause and burden shift
R4  reversal and remedy
```

That does not establish one working answer. The controls arise in different systems, jurisdictions, legal traditions, time horizons, and distributions of authority. Sprint 03 tests whether they can be composed without silently importing one system's protection into another.
