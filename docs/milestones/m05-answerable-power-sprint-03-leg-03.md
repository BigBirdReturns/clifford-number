# M-05 Sprint 03 Leg 03 — surveillance, enforcement, and system prohibition

## Domain under test

```text
ICE ELITE and the Woodburn M-J-M-A- chain
Dutch SyRI fraud-risk system
```

The test preserves their non-equivalence. ELITE reaches a directly affected person, field operation, stop, detention, litigation, and arrest-practice relief. SyRI reaches collective challenge, independent proportionality review, and system-level legal prohibition. Neither supplies the complete R1–R7 constitution.

## Domain adapter

The Answerable Power Constitution becomes the **Inference-to-Intervention Covenant**.

It covers:

```text
geographic and target prioritization
identity and relationship resolution
watchlist, risk, fraud, and threat indications
surveillance and investigation initiation
stop, search, arrest, detention, transport, removal, or denial
cross-agency and public-private sharing
analyst or model recommendations that narrow operational attention
retention, correction, deletion, export, and successor reuse
```

## Constitutional invariants

```text
public decision authority retains responsibility
representation → inference → threshold → user → field action remain separate transitions
human review requires evidence, time, authority, protection, and logged rejection capacity
serious coercive action requires an action packet and independent authorization
notice may be delayed only under a bounded and reviewed emergency constitution
missing institutional records create an adverse presumption against the actor
correction and deletion follow copies, derived outputs, watchlists, vendors, and successors
system prohibition is incomplete without shutdown, deletion, remedy, and recurrence review
mission value does not prove necessity, proportionality, accuracy, or lawfulness
successful collective challenge is not standing R6 co-governance
configurability does not dissolve vendor contribution or custody
person-level injury and system-level constitutional violation remain separate proof objects
```

## R1–R7 stress result

### R1 — notice

ELITE does not show pre-action person notice. The represented person experienced stop, arrest, detention, and transport before litigation generated access.

SyRI shows collective framework challenge, not person-level notice, risk-report access, or contestation before investigation or another consequence.

```text
result:
pre-action person notice not observed
```

### R2 — evidence access and correction

ELITE litigation exposed parts of the field chain and preserved the distinction among geographic prioritization, plate checks, observation, Mobile Fortify, officer judgment, and arrest. The operation-specific ELITE queries, configuration, maps, thresholds, audit, retention, correction, and deletion remain unavailable.

SyRI's opacity and lack of verifiability were judicially recognized, but complete models, indicators, thresholds, versions, exceptions, individual reports, and correction paths remain open.

```text
result:
strong later review
incomplete person and technical access
```

### R3 — pause and burden shift

ELITE litigation produced prospective arrest-practice constraints after the initial person-level consequence.

SyRI produced collective independent review and a framework-level burden shift leading to prohibition.

Neither record presently demonstrates an ordinary person-level automatic pause before coercive action.

### R4 — remedy

ELITE supplies release activity and classwide prospective arrest-practice relief. It does not close software correction, deletion, final compliance, compensation, or vendor-side change.

SyRI supplies system-level legal prohibition. It does not close shutdown, data and model deletion, individual remedy, compensation, or successor-function recurrence.

### R5 — exit and substitution

ELITE termination or replacement is not observed.

SyRI's legal basis was prohibited, but durable decommissioning, deletion, alternative operation, public capacity, performance, and successor systems remain open.

### R6 — co-governance

Neither system supplies an ongoing affected-party institution with binding authority over purpose, categories, thresholds, users, sharing, exceptions, deployment, or termination.

```text
successful litigation or collective challenge
≠ standing co-governance
```

### R7 — value recovery

Neither system closes a contribution or injury → enforceable residual right → realized value → affected-party distribution chain.

```text
litigation, public learning, vendor revenue,
compensation, or system change
≠ R7 by themselves
```

## Coercive action packet

Before an avoidable coercive action, the institution must identify:

```text
system, vendor, authority, deployment, operation, date, and lawful purpose
represented person or bounded population
source data, provenance, age, reliability, corrections, and sharing chain
identity, location, address, relationship, match, score, risk, or target package
software, model, rule, data, configuration, watchlist, and policy version
threshold, confidence, tolerance, relationship depth, exception, and override
querying user, analyst, supervisor, and decision authority
all joined systems and their distinct outputs
proposed coercive consequence
necessity, proportionality, imminence, and reversibility
less restrictive and non-coercive alternatives
notice or delayed-notice basis, expiry, and later notice
independent authorization and emergency review
evidence, audit, field record, and downstream copies
correction, deletion, prohibition, compensation, and successor routes
public and private contribution, learning, benefit, and value ledger
```

The institution bears the burden. A vendor output, risk indication, geographic priority, aggregate accuracy claim, or missing institutional record is not self-proving.
