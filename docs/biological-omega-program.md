## Scope correction

The prior Omega Accord work modeled nuclear proliferation and post-zero reconstitution. It did not contain a biological threat class, a laboratory-associated incident model, a biosafety or biosecurity control plane, a pathogen or outbreak lifecycle, or a Clifford Number cross-reference.

An Omega-tier biological problem is one in which containing or destroying the current manifestation does not reliably terminate the threat because natural source conditions, knowledge, material access, distributed infrastructure, capability, motive, or successor pathways remain. Biological threats differ from nuclear breakout because the hazardous object may replicate and spread after release, natural background complicates intent and attribution, legitimate capability is distributed across many institutions and providers, and public-health response cannot wait for blame.

A programme is not qualified merely because one incident can be handled. It must preserve positive conservative portfolio margins under concurrent natural outbreaks, laboratory incidents, deliberate deception, false alarms, supply disruption, cyber compromise, and response-node failure.

## Clifford Number boundary

The Clifford Number remains the shortest evidenced actor-to-actor path through a shared named, bounded, receipted surface with a valid temporal overlap. It is not a biological-risk, origin, guilt, intent, negligence, or probability score.

A university, laboratory, agency, country, journal, funder, provider class, or scientific field is too broad to create a hop. Co-presence does not establish contact, coordination, influence, agreement, causation, endorsement, or wrongdoing.

The biological programme keeps four analytical objects separate:

1. **Actor-surface ledger.** Who occupied a specific bounded decision, review, grant, inspection, exercise, or incident surface, in what role and period, with which receipts.
2. **Capability and custody ledger.** How safely abstracted material, methods, information, models, instruments, services, or facilities moved through transfers, access decisions, provider services, or custody events.
3. **Authority and control ledger.** Who possessed a defined right to inspect, pause, impose a burden, reverse, substitute, restore, or terminate.
4. **Incident and answer ledger.** Which notices, investigations, interventions, remedies, substitutions, and after-action changes produced an observed result and whether that result endured.

No cross-ledger join automatically becomes an actor hop, origin finding, or causal conclusion.

## Answerable-power translation

| Level | Biological interpretation |
|---|---|
| R0 | People are represented or consulted but hold no effective authority. |
| R1 | Timely notice, protected reporting, and bounded public-health communication exist. |
| R2 | Independent inspection and correction of inventory, protocol, screening, reporting, or access defects are possible. |
| R3 | A competent body can pause an activity or service while the proponent carries the burden of demonstrating control. |
| R4 | Material can be secured, work terminated or redesigned, exposed people monitored and treated, and injury remedied. |
| R5 | Safer methods, providers, platforms, diagnostics, surveillance, manufacturing, or public capacity can replace the risky dependency. |
| R6 | Workers, public-health authorities, and legitimate affected representatives possess enforceable authority over purpose, categories, exceptions, and termination. |
| R7 | Public funding, risk, data, labour, and affected-community contribution return as access, licensing, preparedness, manufacturing, compensation, or durable public capability. |

A committee, policy, appeal, audit, ethics statement, or human reviewer does not satisfy the ladder without observed authority, timing, implementation, outcome, durability, and a defined transfer limit.

## Authoritative seed frameworks

The programme begins from the CDC/NIH *Biosafety in Microbiological and Biomedical Laboratories*, WHO *Laboratory Biosecurity Guidance*, WHO *Global Guidance Framework for the Responsible Use of the Life Sciences*, BWC confidence-building measures, the UN Secretary-General's Mechanism for investigation of alleged biological-weapons use, UNIDIR work on possible BWC verification models, and public synthesis-screening work from NIST and IBBIS.

These sources already establish substantial practice around protocol-driven risk assessment, consequence-driven life-cycle biosecurity, shared responsibility across the research lifecycle, laboratory and biodefence transparency, international investigation, and sequence and customer screening. The research question is therefore the control topology and observed authority across interfaces, rather than the invention of biosafety or biosecurity.

## Security and privacy boundary

The public programme excludes pathogen-design instructions, sequence data, enhancement targets, laboratory exploit details, facility vulnerabilities, operational targeting information, patient-level health data, unnecessary worker identity, home addresses, protected-trait aggregation, and unverified allegations.

Restricted evidence may support an authorized review but may not be copied into the public graph. A missing or confidential record cannot be narrated as factual absence. A laboratory association cannot establish outbreak origin. A provider flag cannot establish malicious intent. Capability cannot establish deployment.

## Stop conditions

The programme remains held when urgent containment depends on prior attribution; no competent owner can lawfully pause an activity; nominal oversight lacks action rights; market or laboratory coverage excludes a material share of capability; incident reporting predictably drives concealment; a capability can reappear through an unmonitored successor; an innocent target lacks correction and restoration; response capacity fails under one major-node loss and two concurrent anomalies; controls suppress beneficial science without demonstrated risk reduction; or intervention destroys essential public-health capacity without a qualified substitute.

## Control question

Can the repository show, through bounded and receipted surfaces, who possessed effective authority to detect, pause, correct, contain, investigate, reverse, substitute, restore, co-govern, and learn before irreversible spread, evidence loss, response saturation, or capability reconstitution, without converting scientific proximity into suspicion or publishing information that increases biological risk?
