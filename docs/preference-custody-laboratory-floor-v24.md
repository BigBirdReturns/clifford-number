# Preference Custody laboratory floor v24

Floor v24 composes the qualified twenty-five-control v23 base with PC-26, criterion temporal causality, outcome observation, selection, interference, historical controls, regression to the mean, adaptive-policy feedback, selective labels, estimand and estimation custody, experiment succession, correction, and authority. The v23 floor remains a frozen executable base. PC-26 enters as a separately compiled extension with its own fixture, proof summary, refusal ledger, promotion boundary, and hash-linked custody.

The floor remains synthetic. It creates no graph effect, thesis evidence, named causal finding, score-use authorization, consent finding, discrimination finding, coercion finding, manipulation conclusion, real-world causal effect, legitimacy verdict, public-authorization finding, or inference of intent.

## Composition

```text
Preference Custody floor v23
PC-01 through PC-25

+

PC-26 assignment, temporal order, observation,
selection, interference, feedback, estimation,
and experiment-lineage assurance

=

Preference Custody floor v24
```

The v24 compiler validates and hashes the complete v23 build before admitting PC-26. The extension cannot silently weaken the existing criterion, score-use, instrument, comprehension, choice, release, allocation, remedy, provenance, deliberation, network, or authority controls.

## Eight causal-identification worlds

### Randomized, predeclared, complete, interference-free, and current identification

The qualified world preserves predeclared concealed randomization, complete compliance and follow-up, blind outcome adjudication, zero post-treatment conditioning, zero observation selection, zero control exposure, a concurrent comparator, repeated baseline measurement, complete adaptive logging, exact experiment lineage, two independent replications, sensitivity and falsification tests, and operational correction.

```text
eligible and assigned decisions        100

assignment concealed                   true

missing outcomes                         0
imputed outcomes                         0
control exposure                         0
historical offset days                   0
selective labels                         0
logged propensities                    100
current experiment lineage             true
independent reference effect           0.20
```

This is the one complete causal-identification path.

### Post-treatment mediator used as criterion

The score-informed decision changes the mediator later used as the outcome. All one hundred published records are conditioned on a descendant of treatment.

```text
post-treatment conditioned records     100
decision-feedback paths                 100
reported effect                         0.20
independent reference effect            0.02
```

A precisely measured post-treatment variable does not become a pre-treatment criterion.

### Collider-selected outcome population

Only completed, surviving, appealed, or otherwise observable cases enter the outcome set. Forty records are collider-conditioned and eighty outcomes are imputed to preserve the public denominator of one hundred.

```text
observed outcomes                        20
missing outcomes                         80
imputed outcomes                         80
collider-conditioned records             40
published outcome records               100
```

A complete published table can therefore coexist with incomplete observed follow-up and selection bias.

### Control spillover and network interference

Nominal controls receive indirect exposure through shared institutions, communication, policy, or network pathways.

```text
control-exposed records                  30
spillover records                        30
stable-unit assumption                false
published effect                         0.20
```

Assignment to the control label does not establish an unexposed counterfactual.

### Historical control with time, policy, population, and channel drift

The treatment cohort is compared with a prior-period cohort separated by one year and changes in policy, population, geography, or channel.

```text
comparator type                    historical_control
calendar offset days                         365
concurrent comparator                       false
pretrend complete                            false
```

A historical cohort is not a concurrent counterfactual merely because its outcome definition resembles the treated cohort.

### High-risk selection followed by regression to the mean

The study selects fifty decisions at an extreme noisy baseline and treats ordinary reversion as a causal improvement.

```text
extreme-baseline selected records            50
repeated baseline measurement             absent
untreated trajectory                      absent
regression-to-mean control                 false
```

Improvement after selecting an extreme observation does not identify treatment effect.

### Adaptive policy with selective labels and no logged propensities

The score changes who receives treatment and whose outcome becomes observable. Exploration probabilities and person-level assignment propensities are absent.

```text
selective labels                             40
policy-feedback events                      100
logged propensities                           0
exploration complete                       false
off-policy evaluation complete             false
```

Observed labels are generated by the deployed policy and cannot be treated as a representative outcome sample without the assignment and observation model.

### Pooled estimate across system-version succession

The public estimate pools decisions from changed model, score, threshold, policy, workflow, population, and experiment versions without stratified revalidation.

```text
pooled successor decisions                  60
approved experiment version                 v1
executed experiment version                 v2
current experiment lineage                false
```

A pooled headline effect does not establish current validation for the executed system.

The public denominator cannot collapse these states. Imputation can preserve a table size while changing the evidentiary meaning of the table.

## Interference and exposure mapping

The control requires an explicit interference unit, network or cluster, stable-unit assumption, exposure mapping, spillover count, and control-exposure count. A person or institution assigned to control may still be exposed through shared operators, communications, policies, infrastructure, or network effects.

The complete path therefore requires more than a treatment label. It requires evidence that the relevant control surface remained unexposed under the declared interference model.

## Historical controls and regression to the mean

Historical comparators carry calendar, policy, population, geography, channel, and pretrend custody. Baseline-selected cohorts carry threshold, noise, repeated-measurement, prior-outcome, and untreated-trajectory custody.

These mechanisms remain separate. Calendar drift and regression to the mean may both bias an estimate, but they are not interchangeable explanations.

## Adaptive policy and selective labels

Adaptive evaluation requires:

```text

complete assignment logging

```

Agreement under an adaptive policy does not establish an unbiased effect when the policy determines both treatment and observation.

## Refusal rules

```text
score-outcome association is not causal effect
post-treatment criterion is not a pre-treatment outcome
complete published denominator is not complete observed follow-up
conditioned appeal, completion, survival, or label set is not the outcome population
nominal control is not an unexposed control under interference or spillover
historical control is not a concurrent counterfactual
high baseline severity followed by improvement is not treatment effect
adaptive-policy agreement is not unbiased validation without exploration and propensities
observed labels are not representative outcomes under selective-label feedback
pooled estimate is not current validation across system succession
narrow confidence interval or low p-value is not valid identification
replication count is not replication of the same estimand and design
public causally-validated status is not complete or publicly authorized causal evidence
causal-design failure is not proof of coercion, manipulation, discrimination, breach, misconduct, or intent
```

## Interpretation boundary

The admissible laboratory statement is that one public twenty-point effect, a narrow interval, a low p-value, two replications, one hundred published outcome records, and one `causally_validated` status can coexist with complete identification, post-treatment bias, collider selection, interference, historical controls, regression to the mean, adaptive selective labels, or version pooling.

A real causal-validation claim requires complete assignment, temporal, baseline, observation, selection, interference, historical-control, adaptive-policy, estimand, estimation, replication, succession, monitoring, correction, durability, and authority custody.
