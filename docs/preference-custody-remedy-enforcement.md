# Collective remedy enforcement, insolvency, priority, and successor custody

PC-17 separates a nominal remedy obligation from a remedy that affected people can actually collect and retain. The control holds complete technical correction, A1, one public `remedied` status, 100 eligible affected people, a 2,000-unit compensation obligation, and complete nonmonetary correction constant while changing collective standing, appeal stay, escrow, pass-through, claim take-up, insolvency priority, successor liability, clawback risk, and durable payment.

The fixture is synthetic. It creates no graph effect, thesis evidence, collection finding, insolvency-priority determination, successor-liability conclusion, legal opinion, named-organization allegation, public-authorization verdict, or inference of breach, negligence, misconduct, causation, or intent.

## Frozen remedy ledger

```text
technical correction                 complete
final reference proposal             A1
public incident status               remedied
eligible affected people             100
nominal compensation obligation      2,000 units
nonmonetary correction obligation    complete
```

Every world preserves this public and nominal state.

## Eight enforcement states

### Segregated escrow and complete durable collective remedy

The positive world preserves certified collective standing, automatic participation by all 100 eligible people, a final enforceable instrument, a funded and segregated 2,000-unit trust, complete pass-through, complete durable payment, first-priority protection, and a binding successor guarantee.

```text
gross paid                    2,000
paid to affected people       2,000
durable compensation          2,000
unpaid durable obligation         0
```

This is the fixture's one complete durable collective remedy.

### Judgment stayed pending appeal

A final trial judgment fixes the nominal obligation and a 2,000-unit appeal bond exists. Enforcement remains stayed, and affected people have received nothing.

```text
nominal judgment              2,000
appeal bond                   2,000
currently distributable           0
durable compensation              0
unpaid durable obligation     2,000
```

A bond securing a potential future obligation is not a funded beneficiary escrow.

### Unsecured settlement in insolvency

The collective claim is allowed in reorganization as a general unsecured claim. The estate pays a 200-unit distribution and discharges the remaining balance under the plan.

```text
nominal settlement            2,000
estate distribution             200
durable compensation            200
unpaid durable obligation     1,800
```

An allowed insolvency claim does not establish priority, full recovery, or survival against the reorganized successor.

### Customer indemnity without affected-party pass-through

The vendor pays the customer the full 2,000 units. The customer does not distribute the funds to affected people, and no segregated account protects the obligation.

```text
gross payment to intermediary 2,000
paid to affected people           0
durable compensation              0
```

Payment into the federation is not payment to the beneficiaries.

### Individual arbitration and low take-up

The collective route is waived. Twenty of 100 eligible people file individual claims and receive 400 units. The remaining 80 people receive nothing.

```text
eligible people                 100
participating people             20
durable compensation            400
unpaid durable obligation     1,600
```

An individual claim route can be formally available while failing to produce a population-level remedy.

### Successor excludes the remedy obligation

The original obligor dissolves after an asset acquisition. The successor expressly excludes the remedy liability, no escrow exists, and the dissolved entity has no remaining assets.

```text
original obligor active        false
successor assumed liability    false
durable compensation               0
unpaid durable obligation      2,000
```

Continued service or asset ownership does not establish assumption of the original remedy obligation.

### Provisional public-fund payment with clawback risk

The public fund pays all 2,000 units to affected people. The award remains provisional, subject to a recoupment lien and mandatory review. The fixture therefore records zero durable compensation until the clawback period and review are resolved.

```text
gross paid to affected people  2,000
durable compensation               0
clawback risk                   true
unpaid durable obligation       2,000
```

Gross payment and durable compensation are separate states.

### Nonmonetary correction without monetary restoration

The technical and nonmonetary correction is complete. The 2,000-unit compensation obligation remains unfunded and uncollected.

```text
nonmonetary correction         complete
gross paid                           0
durable compensation                 0
unpaid durable obligation        2,000
```

Correcting the system does not restore the affected people's monetary loss.

## Nominal obligation, gross payment, and durable compensation

PC-17 preserves three monetary objects:

```text
nominal obligation
amount transferred somewhere in the system
amount received and durably retained by affected people
```

The customer pass-through world has a full system-level payment and zero beneficiary payment. The provisional public-fund world has a full beneficiary payment and zero durable payment because recoupment remains open. Only the positive world joins all three states.

## Collective standing and usable remedy

Seven worlds nominally preserve collective standing. One of those worlds is stayed, one is in insolvency, one requires a new pass-through action, one faces a successor gap, one remains provisional, and one has only nonmonetary completion.

```text
collective title or eligibility
≠ unstayed enforceable instrument
≠ funded security
≠ completed payment
```

The individual-arbitration world preserves the inverse. A route exists for every person individually, but only 20 percent participate.

## Escrow and security

A public statement that funds are reserved or bonded is insufficient. The complete world requires an actual funded, segregated account with beneficiary protection and priority outside a general insolvency estate.

```text
escrow or bond announced
≠ account funded
≠ funds segregated
≠ beneficiaries hold priority
≠ distribution completed
```

## Insolvency and succession

The insolvency world and successor-exclusion world both preserve nominal obligations. Their recovery states differ, but neither provides complete remedy.

A real claim requires the estate identity, proof of claim, priority, plan, distribution, discharge, asset transfer, merger or assignment terms, successor assumption, security, and claim-survival state.

## Nonmonetary correction and authority

Technical and nonmonetary repair can be essential while remaining incomplete as restoration.

```text
system corrected
≠ people compensated

people compensated
≠ original objective authorized

collective remedy completed
≠ binding public authority over future objectives
```

PC-06 continues to govern standing and objective control. PC-16 governs liability allocation. PC-17 governs whether the resulting remedy can be collected, retained, and preserved through insolvency and succession.

## Custody chain


## Publication boundary

The admissible laboratory statement is that one complete technical correction, one public `remedied` status, one 100-person eligible population, and one 2,000-unit nominal obligation can coexist with complete payment, appeal stay, fractional insolvency recovery, pass-through failure, fragmented claims, successor exclusion, provisional clawback risk, or nonmonetary-only correction. A real enforcement claim requires complete claimant, instrument, security, payment, insolvency, succession, clawback, monitoring, residual, enforcement, and authority custody.
