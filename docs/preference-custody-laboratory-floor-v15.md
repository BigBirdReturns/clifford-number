# Preference Custody laboratory floor v15

Floor v15 composes the qualified sixteen-control v14 base with PC-17, collective remedy enforcement, insolvency priority, successor liability, and payment-durability equifinality. The v14 controls remain frozen and executable. PC-17 is admitted through a separate fixture, proof matrix, custody chain, refusal ledger, promotion boundary, and frontier transition.

The floor remains synthetic. It creates no graph effect, thesis evidence, collection finding, insolvency-priority determination, successor-liability conclusion, legal opinion, named-organization allegation, breach or negligence finding, public-authorization verdict, or inference of causation, misconduct, manipulation, or intent.

## Composition

```text
Preference Custody floor v14
PC-01 through PC-16

+

PC-17 collective remedy enforcement, insolvency, and successor durability

=

Preference Custody floor v15
PC-01 through PC-17
```

The v15 compiler validates and hashes the complete v14 build before admitting PC-17. The extension does not rewrite the earlier controls governing preference, standing, agenda, bargaining, technical recovery, federation, liability allocation, insurance, payment, or public remedy.

## Judgment, bond, and collection

The appeal world contains a final trial judgment and a 2,000-unit bond. Enforcement remains stayed and no beneficiary distribution is available.

```text

```

## Payment-path custody

PC-17 preserves three monetary states separately:

```text
amount transferred somewhere in the system
amount received by affected people
amount durably retained by affected people
```

The customer pass-through world records a full 2,000-unit system payment and zero affected-party payment. The provisional public-fund world records a full 2,000-unit affected-party payment and zero durable compensation because the full amount remains subject to recoupment.

Only the complete world aligns all three states.

## Collective standing and population coverage

Seven worlds preserve nominal collective standing. That state does not eliminate appeal stay, insolvency priority, pass-through failure, successor exclusion, clawback, or nonmonetary-only completion.

The individual-arbitration world shows the opposite boundary:

```text
individual route formally available to 100 people
participating claimants                         20
population durable compensation                400
```

An individual route does not establish complete population remedy.

## Insolvency and priority

The insolvency world preserves an allowed collective claim. It remains general unsecured, receives a 200-unit distribution, and loses the remaining balance under the plan.

The successor-exclusion world preserves a final obligation against a dissolved entity with no remaining assets. The asset purchaser does not assume the remedy liability.

```text
claim filed or allowed
≠ priority
≠ distribution
≠ claim survival

asset acquisition or continued service
≠ successor-liability assumption
```

## Technical and nonmonetary correction

The nonmonetary-only world preserves complete correction and monitoring while the monetary obligation remains entirely unpaid.

```text
technical system repaired
≠ affected loss restored

nonmonetary compliance complete
≠ compensation collected
```

PC-16 governs allocation of the remedy obligation. PC-17 governs collectability and durability after that obligation exists.

## Publication boundary

Floor v15 can refuse unsupported interpretation across seventeen synthetic controls. It cannot grant publication authority to a real collection, insolvency-priority, successor-liability, breach, negligence, remedy, legitimacy, or public-authorization claim. A bounded enforcement statement requires complete claimant, instrument, security, payment, insolvency, succession, clawback, monitoring, residual, enforcement, and authority custody.
