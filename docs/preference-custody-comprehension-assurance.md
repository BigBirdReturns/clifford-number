# Comprehension measurement, translation, accessibility, and scenario-transfer assurance custody

PC-23 separates public certification and score publication from the population, construct, instrument, item security, administration, translation, accessibility, transfer, retention, validation, and correction state beneath the claim. The control holds one operative release, one hundred affected people, one hundred issued certificates, an 85-point published mean, a one-hundred-percent published pass rate, one threshold, and one public `comprehension_validated` status constant across eight different measurement systems.

The fixture is synthetic. It creates no graph effect, thesis evidence, named-assessment audit, consent finding, discrimination finding, coercion finding, manipulation conclusion, public-authorization verdict, legitimacy finding, or inference of intent.

## Frozen publication surface

```text
operative release             RELEASE-INCIDENT-V1@1
affected people               100

certificates issued           100
published mean score           85
published pass rate           100%
published threshold            70
```

Every world therefore presents the same headline:

```text

100 percent passed

```

That publication does not identify who was assessed, what was measured, how the test was administered, whether items were secure, whether results transferred to operative scenarios, whether understanding persisted, or whether language and accessibility groups were measured equivalently.

## Eight measurement-assurance systems

### Complete construct, transfer, retention, and access assurance

The positive world measures operative consequence understanding through recognition, free explanation, and scenarios. All one hundred people are assessed. All one hundred demonstrate consequence understanding, scenario transfer, and delayed retention. Items are rotated and randomized, answer keys are segregated, administration is independent, multilingual equivalence is validated, accessibility accommodations preserve the construct, subgroup functioning is supported, an external replication exists, and reassessment, revocation, and reissue are operational.

```text
assessed                         100
certified                        100
consequence understanding       100
scenario transfer               100
delayed retention               100
item leakage                       0
coaching                            0
translation DIF affected           0
accessibility failures              0
```

This is the one qualified complete measurement-assurance path.

### Recognition-only substitution

The second world uses multiple-choice term recognition. Every person receives an 85-point score and passes, but only forty can explain the operative consequence, twenty can transfer the rule to a scenario, and twenty retain it after delay.

```text
recognition                      100
consequence understanding         40
scenario transfer                 20
delayed retention                 20
```

Recognition identifies familiarity with the instrument. It does not establish operational understanding.

### Leaked item bank and memorized answers

The third world reuses a fixed item bank whose answer key is compromised. All one hundred participants encounter leaked material, and eighty rely on memorized answers.

```text
item leakage                    100
memorized answers                80
consequence understanding        30
scenario transfer                15
delayed retention                15
```

A high score remains compatible with minimal operative understanding when the response surface is contaminated.

### Administrator coaching and prompting

The fourth world uses a potentially valid instrument but contaminates administration. Sixty people receive coaching and answer prompts, and twenty answers are substituted.

```text
coached participants             60
answer prompts                   60
answer substitutions             20
consequence understanding        50
scenario transfer                35
delayed retention                30
```

Assistance and accommodation must be separated from answer production. A score does not identify whose cognition generated the response.

### Literal translation and differential item functioning

The fifth world supplies three language versions but validates only lexical correspondence. Semantic, procedural, and consequence equivalence fail, and thirty people fall within a material differential-item-functioning state.

```text
languages covered                 3
translation DIF affected         30
consequence understanding        85
scenario transfer                70
delayed retention                65
```

Translation availability does not establish measurement equivalence.

### Nominal accessibility and construct failure

The sixth world lists five formats but performs no assistive-technology or usability testing. The accommodation changes or blocks the intended construct for twenty people.

```text
formats listed                    5
assistive-technology tested   false
usability tested              false
construct preserved           false
accessibility failures           20
```

An accommodation label does not establish usable access, and a proxy answer does not establish person-level comprehension.

### Partial assessment and certificate imputation

The seventh world assesses sixty people, excludes forty, and issues one hundred certificates by imputing the unobserved population.

```text
eligible                         100
assessed                          60
passed                            60
imputed certificates              40
certified                        100
```

The public certificate count is identical to the complete world even though forty certificates have no person-level observation.

### Immediate pass and delayed-retention collapse

The final world supports immediate consequence understanding for all one hundred people and near-complete scenario transfer, but only twenty-five retain the operative understanding at delayed follow-up.

```text
consequence understanding       100
scenario transfer                95
delayed retention                25
```

Immediate performance does not establish durable comprehension.

## The certificate is a publication object

PC-23 distinguishes:

```text
eligible population
sampled population
assigned population
assessed population
completed population
passed population
certified population
imputed population
excluded population
```

A certificate count can exceed the observed assessed population. The certificate therefore requires its own source, rule, and issuance custody rather than inheriting the authority of the instrument.

## Comprehension is a construct, not a score

The intended construct is operative consequence understanding. A complete validation argument must identify the domain and preserve evidence that the instrument distinguishes:

```text
recognition
recall
free explanation
scenario transfer
counterfactual application
delayed retention
confidence calibration
```

A mean score can remain stable when the construct changes from operative understanding to term recognition or memorized answer production.

## Item security and administration

A secure-instrument claim requires:

```text
item identity and version
exposure and reuse history
leakage and compromise state
answer-key custody
randomization
memorization evidence
security audit
```

Administration custody separately requires the proctor, coach, interpreter, model, advocate, prompts, hints, corrections, answer substitutions, and assistance type. Construct-preserving assistance is not the same object as answer production.

## Translation and subgroup functioning

Translation custody must preserve source text, terminology, reviewer, semantic equivalence, procedural equivalence, consequence equivalence, and differential item functioning. Aggregate pass parity does not establish subgroup equivalence when the item response function differs across languages or access groups.

## Accessibility and construct preservation

Accessibility requires more than format availability. The control preserves:

```text
format identity
assistive technology
compatibility testing
usability testing
accommodation procedure
construct-preservation analysis
access failure
person-level result
```

An accommodation may improve access, leave access unchanged, or alter the construct being measured. Those states must remain distinct.

## Transfer and retention

A person can reproduce the correct term immediately without applying it to a new scenario. A person can also demonstrate transfer immediately and lose the understanding before the consequential choice or implementation event. PC-23 therefore preserves separate scenario-transfer and delayed-retention states.

## Independent validation and correction

Expert review, face validity, aggregate score recalculation, or instrument approval does not establish independent validation. The positive path requires a complete validation argument, subgroup analysis, independent administration, external replication, audit, explanation, objection, appeal, reassessment, certificate revocation, correction, and reissue.

## Real-case promotion boundary

A real comprehension-validation claim requires:

```text
population denominators and sampling frame
operative release and notice crosswalk
construct identity and intended interpretation
instrument and item-bank identity and version
item security, exposure, leakage, reuse, and answer-key custody
administration, coaching, prompting, assistance, and answer substitution
recognition, consequence explanation, transfer, retention, and confidence calibration

accessibility, usability, accommodation, and construct preservation
missingness, attrition, exclusion, imputation, weighting, and uncertainty
subgroup and external validation
independent administration and replication
audit, explanation, objection, appeal, reassessment, revocation, correction, durability, and authority
```

## Custody chain

Each world emits a SHA-256 hash-linked chain:

```text
release, public certificate, score, threshold, and authority
→ eligible, sampled, assessed, passed, certified, excluded, and imputed population
→ construct, instrument, item bank, score, consequence, transfer, and retention
→ item exposure, leakage, memorization, coaching, prompting, and assistance
→ translation equivalence, differential functioning, accessibility, and construct preservation
→ uncertainty, subgroup validation, replication, audit, correction, and authority
→ mechanism classification
→ interpretation seal
```

Mutating the population, construct, instrument, score, item security, administration, translation, accessibility, validation, classification, or interpretation breaks the chain.

## Publication boundary

The admissible laboratory statement is that one hundred certificates, a published mean score of 85, a one-hundred-percent pass rate, and one public `comprehension_validated` status can coexist with complete assurance, recognition-only measurement, item leakage, coaching contamination, translation differential functioning, accessibility construct failure, denominator imputation, or delayed-retention collapse. A real comprehension claim requires complete measurement, validation, correction, durability, and authority custody.
