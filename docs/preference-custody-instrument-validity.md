# Construct validity, item security, administration independence, and score-provenance custody

PC-24 separates a published validation label and stable score distribution from the construct, item, criterion, administration, scoring, equating, replication, and correction state beneath the claim. The control holds one operative release, one hundred assessed and certified people, an 85-point mean, a one-hundred-percent pass rate, a 0.90 reliability coefficient, a 0.80 validity coefficient, and one public `instrument_validated` status constant across eight score-production systems.

The fixture is synthetic. It creates no graph effect, thesis evidence, named-instrument audit, consent or discrimination finding, coercion finding, manipulation conclusion, real-world causal claim, legitimacy verdict, public-authorization finding, or inference of intent.

## Frozen publication surface

```text
operative release              RELEASE-INCIDENT-V1@1
affected people                100
assessed people                100
certificates issued            100
public instrument status       instrument_validated
published mean score            85
published pass rate            100%
published threshold             70
published reliability           0.90
published validity coefficient  0.80
```

Every world therefore presents the same validation surface. That surface does not identify construct coverage, construct-irrelevant variance, criterion independence, item security, instructional contamination, adaptive exposure, response authorship, score overrides, cross-form comparability, external replication, or correction.

## Eight score-provenance systems

### Complete construct, security, independence, and equating assurance

The positive world predeclares all four operative consequence domains, rotates and randomizes a secure item bank, segregates answer-key custody, uses an independent administrator and scorer, validates against a temporally and materially independent criterion, preserves secure anchors and stable equating, performs subgroup validation and external replication, and supports reassessment, certificate revocation, and reissue.

```text
reference domains covered       4 of 4
excluded domains                0
item leakage                    0
high-exposure participants      0
criterion overlap               0
model assistance                0
score overrides                 0
independent criterion result    0.80
form comparable                 true
external replication            true
```

This is the one qualified complete instrument-assurance path.

### Construct underrepresentation

The vocabulary-only world tests release terminology while omitting choice consequence, exit consequence, and payment-and-clawback consequence.

```text
reference domains covered       1 of 4
excluded domains                3
published reliability           0.90
published validity              0.80
independent criterion result    0.25
```

High internal consistency can coexist with a systematically incomplete construct.

### Construct-irrelevant variance

The third world includes all four domains but allows reading speed and technical familiarity to dominate score variance.

```text
construct-relevant variance     40%
construct-irrelevant variance   60%
independent criterion result    0.30
subgroup validation             false
```

Content coverage does not establish that the score variance comes from the intended construct.

### Criterion contamination

The criterion world publishes a 0.80 validity coefficient using a criterion that shares ten items, twenty curriculum elements, and ten answer-key elements with the assessment.

```text
item overlap                    10
curriculum overlap              20
answer-key overlap              10
criterion independent           false
published validity              0.80
independent criterion result    0.35
```

A validity coefficient does not identify criterion independence.

### Item leakage and teaching to the test

The fifth world uses twenty fixed items, exposes the entire population to leaked material, records eighty memorized answers, and teaches eighty people directly to the assessment surface.

```text
unique administered items       20
maximum item exposure          100
item leakage                   100
memorized answers               80
teaching-to-test exposure       80
answer key compromised          true
```

Stable scores and reliability remain compatible with compromised response provenance.

### Adaptive exposure concentration

The adaptive world routes eighty participants through a small set of repeatedly exposed anchor items.

```text
unique administered items       30
anchor items                     5
maximum item exposure          100
high-exposure participants      80
anchor security                 overexposed
form comparable                 false
```

Adaptive delivery does not establish exposure diversity or cross-route comparability.

### Administration, prompting, and score overrides

The seventh world gives sixty people model assistance and answer prompts, completes twenty answers, and overrides twenty-five scores through ten human and fifteen automated changes.

```text
model assistance                60

answer completions              20
score overrides                 25
human overrides                 10

```

An automated score is not a complete score-provenance receipt.

### Form equating and version drift

The final world approves `FORM-REFERENCE-A`, executes `FORM-DRIFT-B`, carries the legacy validity coefficient forward, and rescales the new form to preserve the 85-point mean and one-hundred-percent pass rate.

```text

form comparable                 false
```

Stable aggregate outputs do not establish form equivalence.

## Reliability, validity, and construct custody

PC-24 distinguishes several objects that are often compressed into one validation label:

```text
score reliability
construct coverage
construct-relevant variance
criterion independence
criterion validity
item security
response provenance
score provenance
form comparability
external replication
```

Reliability can remain high when an instrument consistently measures vocabulary, reading speed, memorized answers, or a contaminated administrative process.

## Item and answer-key security

A secure-item claim requires:

```text
item identity and version
item-bank identity and version
exposure history
reuse and rotation
leakage and compromise
answer-key custody
memorization evidence
anchor exposure
retirement state
security audit
```

Secrecy policy and access-control labels do not establish the observed exposure state.

## Criterion independence

A validation criterion requires identity and provenance separate from the assessment:

```text
item overlap
curriculum overlap
answer-key overlap
temporal independence
scorer independence
criterion validity
contamination audit
```

A coefficient generated from shared material measures agreement inside one response-production system rather than independent criterion performance.

## Administration and score provenance

The score ledger must preserve the administrator, proctor, model, coach, prompt, hint, answer completion, scorer, override actor, override reason, and pre- and post-override values. Construct-preserving assistance and answer production are different events.

## Equating and form succession

Cross-form comparability requires more than a common threshold or stable mean. The control preserves:

```text
approved form
executed form
form and item-bank versions
anchor identity and security
equating method
score transformation
comparability result
version drift
equating audit
```

A legacy validity coefficient cannot automatically succeed to a changed construct, form, item bank, scoring model, or equating procedure.

## Real-case promotion boundary

A real instrument-validation claim requires:

```text
population and intervention denominators
construct identity, domain map, blueprint, exclusions, and interpretation
instrument, form, item bank, item, anchor, scoring, and threshold versions
reliability and validity methods, coefficients, uncertainty, and data
item difficulty, discrimination, exposure, reuse, leakage, compromise, and retirement
instruction, curriculum, practice, coaching, prompts, completions, and answer substitution
construct-relevant and irrelevant feature provenance
criterion identity, independence, overlap, timing, scorer, and outcome
administrator, model, scorer, rater, override actor, mandate, conflict, and action
form assignment, adaptive routing, anchor exposure, equating, drift, and comparability
subgroup validation, replication, audit, explanation, objection, reassessment, correction, revocation, durability, and authority
```

## Publication boundary

The admissible laboratory statement is that one hundred assessments and certificates, an 85-point mean, a one-hundred-percent pass rate, a 0.90 reliability claim, a 0.80 validity claim, and one public `instrument_validated` status can coexist with complete assurance, construct underrepresentation, construct-irrelevant variance, criterion contamination, item leakage and teaching-to-test, adaptive exposure concentration, administration and scoring overrides, or noncomparable form drift. A real instrument-validation claim requires complete measurement-provenance and authority custody.
