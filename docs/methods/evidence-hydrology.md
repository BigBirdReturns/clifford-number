# Evidence Hydrology: from bounded lake to inland sea

## Purpose

The project has reached the point where adding isolated examples produces diminishing returns. The next requirement is a durable source metabolism: broad enough to discover changing systems, precise enough to preserve exact legal and technical records, deep enough to recover hidden or disappearing instruments, and disciplined enough that volume cannot substitute for proof.

This method treats the evidence estate as an inland sea with four inputs:

```text
ocean discovery
+ authoritative freshwater streams
+ direct-voice tributaries
+ archival and restricted aquifers
→ estuary joins
→ normalized inland sea
→ human adjudication
```

The metaphor is operational, not decorative. Each water source has a different evidentiary function, failure mode, refresh cadence, storage rule, and promotion ceiling.

## 1. Ocean discovery

Ocean sources maximize recall. They include web crawls, archives, scholarly indexes, news/event corpora, collaborative graphs, code search, document repositories, and investigative entity datasets.

Ocean output can:

- reveal a new entity, program, interface, contract, term, deployment, court case, source URL, or historical source state;
- show that a page changed or disappeared;
- identify candidate chronology and exact acquisition routes;
- surface alternative explanations and non-networked comparators.

Ocean output cannot by itself:

- establish exact identity;
- establish production use;
- establish causal contribution;
- establish coordination, intent, coercion, extraction, or wrongdoing;
- promote a cross-estate thesis.

Its default terminal state is `locator_only`.

## 2. Authoritative freshwater streams

Freshwater sources recur from public institutions and other primary custodians: law, regulations, executive actions, courts, audits, procurement, spending, corporate filings, lobbying disclosures, standards, official inventories, public datasets, and enforcement records.

These sources provide dated instruments, public authority, exact legal entities, amounts, stated purposes, formal rights, adjudicative findings, and official custody. They are cleaner than ocean discovery but not self-interpreting.

Official custody does not prove:

- that the record is complete;
- that the system operated as described;
- that a safeguard was effective;
- that a human reviewer possessed meaningful veto;
- that a formal owner possessed practical custody;
- that an appeal could prevent the original harm;
- that the represented person had standing over the system objective.

Freshwater sources therefore terminate at `source_bounded_candidate_only` until joined to exact deployment, consequence, voice, counterfactual, and remedy records.

## 3. Direct-voice tributaries

Tributaries carry material that the institutional archive routinely lacks: affected people, families, communities, workers, analysts, engineers, public defenders, plaintiffs, witnesses, whistleblowers, and local observers.

Direct voice answers questions that contracts and product pages cannot:

```text
What did the person know before action?
When did notice arrive?
What record could the person inspect?
What had to be proved?
Could the action be stayed?
Could the category be challenged?
What remedy actually changed the consequence?
What injury persisted after correction?
```

Direct voice is never transferable by analogy. A person affected by one ICE tool does not supply voice for another deployment. A worker speaking about one company does not supply operator evidence for another. A plaintiff challenging a local camera network does not speak for every person represented by similar systems.

Tributaries are acquired manually, consensually, through public records, or through lawful docket material. They are not bulk-scraped. Their ceiling is `deployment_specific_voice_candidate_only`.

## 4. Archival and restricted aquifers

Aquifers are slow, deep, and often decisive. They include:

- FOIA reading rooms and records requests;
- state and municipal records;
- bid evaluations and technical proposals;
- contract modifications and exhibits;
- vendor customer, trust, and release-note portals;
- archived websites and historical records;
- sealed, redacted, or restricted docket gaps;
- inspector-general workpapers;
- committee exhibits;
- discovery productions and expert reports;
- decommission, deletion, transition, and destruction certificates;
- model, version, threshold, override, abort, and audit logs.

An aquifer entry records a lawful acquisition route. It never authorizes bypassing authentication, confidentiality, robots controls, rate limits, court restrictions, privacy duties, or contractual access controls.

Its default ceiling is `locator_or_acquisition_route_only`.

## 5. The estuary rule

Evidence enters the estuary when independently governed source classes converge on the same exact object.

The minimum join is:

```text
same exact entity or deployment
+ same dated instrument or source state
+ at least two independent hydrology classes
+ chronology
+ counterevidence
+ falsifier
```

Person-level claims additionally require deployment-specific direct voice or an equivalent adjudicated record.

Examples:

```text
ocean discovery locates a vanished policy page
+ freshwater filing establishes the operative date
+ aquifer archive preserves the prior text
→ bounded version-change candidate
```

```text
freshwater contract establishes the platform
+ tributary testimony establishes the consequence
+ aquifer logs establish the threshold and user action
+ court record establishes remedy
→ bounded person-level chain for human review
```

An estuary join is still not a conclusion. It is a better acquisition object.

## 6. Sea states

Every incoming object must occupy an explicit state:

```text
unrouted_discovery
source_bounded_locator
cross_class_candidate
acquisition_contract_open
bounded_fact_for_human_review
bounded_non_link
falsified
source_restricted
source_unavailable
```

No object may silently move from discovery to proof.

## 7. Storage and provenance

The sea stores the minimum material required to reconstruct source state:

- source locator;
- request method and timestamp;
- response status and final URL;
- content type;
- ETag and Last-Modified when available;
- SHA-256 body fingerprint;
- bounded public metadata or excerpt;
- source and catchment identifiers;
- change event;
- acquisition and promotion ceiling.

Full text is not the default. The program avoids warehousing copyrighted, sensitive, or direct-voice material merely because it is technically retrievable.

When a public source disappears, the sea preserves a tombstone and prior fingerprint. Disappearance may justify archive recovery. It does not prove deliberate concealment.

## 8. Continuous refresh

The scheduled refresh program has two rhythms.

### Freshwater pulse

Daily or near-daily polling of allowlisted public official endpoints:

```text
new law or order
new court opinion
new audit
new award or procurement notice
new filing
new enforcement action
new public technical inventory
```

### Ocean tide

Weekly broad discovery and archival change detection:

```text
new source
new term
new entity
new historical capture
source moved or disappeared
new scholarly or investigative reference
```

The poller is fail-soft. Network failure, rate limiting, and authentication are recorded rather than converted into missing evidence. Scheduled automation stores hashes, headers, and summaries only. Direct voice and restricted aquifers remain manual or consent-governed.

## 9. Adversarial calibration

The sea must learn its sources' failure modes.

For each source family, track:

- duplicate rate;
- stale-record rate;
- missing-identifier rate;
- entity-resolution ambiguity;
- publication delay;
- update and deletion behavior;
- claimant, institutional, or ranking bias;
- coverage denominator;
- access failures;
- source disappearance;
- mismatch between formal safeguard and observed remedy.

This creates a source reliability ledger without pretending that a numerical weight can adjudicate a claim.

## 10. Cross-estate feeding

The hydrology program supplies, but does not supersede:

### M-04B

Common purpose, functional differentiation, metabolism, coordination, reproduction, feedback, strategic invisibility, coercion, extraction, theaters, and bridges.

### M-04F

Inference authority, thresholds, burdens, timing, evidence custody, counterfactual access, exceptions, remedy, uncertainty allocation, direct voice, stratigraphy, and waterline.

### F20–F46

### Pattern-to-Proof

## 11. Constitutional boundary

```text
continuous ingestion proves completeness: false
freshness proves accuracy: false
ocean volume proves recurrence: false
source count proves coordination: false
official custody proves practical truth: false
direct voice transfers across deployments: false
source disappearance proves intent: false
shape proves integration: false
recurrence proves common governance: false
candidate can self-promote: false

promotes_to: candidate_only
graph_effect: none
conclusion_generated: false
estate_completion_claimed: false
```

The inland sea is successful when it makes fog exact, keeps fault lines visible, and continuously directs the next lawful acquisition. It is not successful merely because it becomes large.
