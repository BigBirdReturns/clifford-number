# Counter-Selector Batch 04 blind review

Each identity-minimized packet receives two fresh-context passes: artifact validity and adversarial countermodel. Procedural separation is recorded but is not external independence.

Dimension support remains categorical and scoped to the packet's actual unit of analysis. Collective, individual, and system-mechanism packets are not interchangeable. Disagreement is retained as a separate ledger rather than averaged into confidence.

A bounded support does not authorize contact, field testing, promotion, identity release, ranking, or graph construction.
