# Evidence-grounded judgment authority

## The failure this corrects

The estate had begun to use independent review as two different things at once:

1. additional evidence and adversarial pressure; and
2. permission for the project to form a judgment at all.

The first is essential. The second is an abdication.

A reviewer can expose selection bias, missing evidence, bad causal attribution, or a stronger alternative explanation. A reviewer cannot be treated as a future magical source of judgment whose absence converts a large receipted record into institutional paralysis.

## Governing distinction

```text
bounded judgment ≠ independent clearance
independent clearance ≠ infallibility
missing clearance ≠ no decision
uncertainty ≠ zero knowledge
reversibility ≠ refusal to act
```

The project therefore separates:

- **judgment authority** — whether the available evidence supports a bounded conclusion or action;
- **confidence** — how strongly the current evidence supports it;
- **review state** — whether a distinct party has challenged or reproduced it;
- **publication state** — what may be communicated and with which caveats;
- **consequence class** — whether the decision is internal, public, reversible, or irreversible.

## Decision ladder

- **J0 · observed:** preserve the observation, null, failure, or source limitation.
- **J1 · candidate judgment:** accept, reject, or bound a candidate; identify the missing transition.
- **J2 · bounded working judgment:** use a supported mechanism or causal prefix in analysis with receipts, counterevidence, and uncertainty attached.
- **J3 · provisional public judgment:** communicate a bounded judgment with correction route and explicit provisional status.
- **J4 · operational decision:** execute reversible research, indexing, acquisition, prioritization, or governance work.
- **J5 · independently corroborated:** a distinct reviewer or reproducer has added evidence or successfully attacked the judgment.

J5 can raise, lower, or overturn a judgment. It is not the source of permission for J0–J4.

## Blocker law

A decision may be blocked by a material defect:

- missing source content;
- broken receipt custody;
- a missing defining causal transition;
- unresolved identity;
- asymmetric selection;
- privacy, security, or lawful-access risk;
- contradictory primary evidence;
- an irreversible consequence without a sufficient evidence threshold;
- a nonreproducible build.

A decision may **not** be blocked solely because a human reviewer, expert, or independent second party has not appeared.

## K0 application

The maintainer field audit already made substantive corrections. It found six records with contiguous CCD 6–7 chains and two additional records with supported CCD 3–5 prefixes. Calling those records merely “supported for human review” discards information the audit has already earned.

The new rule converts them into bounded working judgments:

```text
supported audit + CCD ≥ 4 + multiple sources + counterevidence + alternatives
→ bounded mechanism judgment

CCD 3–5 causal prefix without full chain
→ bounded partial-mechanism judgment

missing defining transition
→ candidate or non-link judgment with acquisition target
```

This does not determine private psychology, guilt, motive, legality, or common purpose. It does decide what the current evidence supports and what work follows.

## Selection-lane application

Every valid lane gets an operational decision:

- continue active symmetric execution;
- execute the highest-value material gap;
- preserve support-only material without public inference;
- hold a suspended or retired lane for its stated material reason.

Pending adversarial review does not create a wait state. It remains a confidence and challenge surface.

## Lake application

When the lake-index products are present, the same builder consumes the basin work queue. Each basin receives a J4 reversible action based on measured gap burden. The next basin is selected by encoded evidence and repair value rather than salience or reviewer availability.

## Correction and reversibility

Every judgment is append-preserving. New evidence produces a superseding decision; it does not silently erase the prior record. Counterevidence and alternative explanations travel with the judgment. The project is required to be decisive and corrigible at the same time.

## Boundaries

A working judgment is not:

- evidence truth by decree;
- publication clearance;
- a guilt or motive finding;
- a relationship edge;
- proof of common purpose;
- immunity from later correction.

The point is not certainty. The point is to stop confusing uncertainty with helplessness.
