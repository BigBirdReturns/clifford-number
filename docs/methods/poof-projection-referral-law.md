# POOF projection and referral law

The ecology permits two lawful directions of movement.

## Evidence authority moves outward

A publication object must carry a `poof-projection-manifest@1` record. The manifest names the source release and commit, lists the source claim, event, and receipt identifiers, exposes qualification and review state, records the generated artifact hash, and carries a copy-ready interpretation contract.

A projection may sequence, compare, explain, or visualize. It may not add a fact. Empty source arrays are allowed only for a synthetic fixture, an open-record target, or purely methodological text whose status is explicit.

## Challenges move inward

A challenge enters as `poof-referral-packet@1`. It must name the exact proposition, current evidence ceiling, required record or test, lawful acquisition route, responsible custodian, consequence of nonresolution, and privacy boundary.

A referral promotes no further than `candidate_only`. It creates no hop, claim, event, score, finding, conclusion, or publication approval. A referral can close as a bounded non-link, falsified proposition, source-restricted question, or reviewed candidate.

## Reader and audit objects

A comprehension receipt proves only that the declared evidence operations were demonstrated under the recorded test. A publication-audit receipt proves only the observed quality of the audited release surface within the stated limitations. Neither enters the substantive receipt ledger.

## Right of reply

The right-of-reply record preserves notice, response state, attributed claims, disputed predicates, correction disposition, publication versions, and unresolved objections. No response is never represented as admission. A subject response is itself evidence with a source and status, not an automatic override or a rhetorical footnote.

## Publication boundary

The generated aperture is reviewable but nonpublic. Public release requires a publication allowlist, a real deployment origin, external audit of that origin, and a release-specific decision that the selected projections are publication-ready. The existence of an HTML route, MCP card, OpenAPI document, or sitemap does not satisfy those requirements.

## Non-graph effects

Graph-inert referrals may open a bounded review queue and may require a separate publication decision. Those powers are declared in the transaction's operational-effect contract. Visibility and ranking effects remain prohibited.

## Compression custody

A projection manifest must disclose its candidate universe, selection and exclusion rules, ordering, truncation, missingness, counterevidence, null-result policy, and overrides. Included-source lineage alone is not a complete account of publication authority.
