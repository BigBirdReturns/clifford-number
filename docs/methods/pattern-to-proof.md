# Pattern-to-Proof

## Purpose

Pattern recognition is a discovery instrument, not a verdict.

A human observer may perceive a durable institutional shape before the final causal joins are public, legible, or obtainable. The correct response is neither blind belief nor automatic dismissal. The signal must be preserved, decomposed, attacked, and updated.

```text
instinct scouts
method decomposes
evidence constrains
comparators challenge
falsifiers attack
receipts adjudicate
```

## The seven stages

### P0 - Signal

Record the perceived invariant in the observer's own language before institutional or model normalization changes it.

### P1 - Bounded hypothesis

Freeze the system, actors, period, proposed mechanism, alternative explanations, and maximum evidentiary ceiling.

### P2 - Transition graph

Decompose the pattern into every transition that would have to be true.

```text
actor
-> instrument
-> gate
-> operating role
-> consequence
-> remedy
-> residual benefit
```

### P3 - Receipt contract

For each transition, name:

```text
exact missing record
custodian
lawful acquisition route
comparator
falsifier
stopping rule
```

### P4 - Adversarial orbit

Search support, contradiction, ordinary controls, non-links, failed paths, inaccessible sources, and alternative mechanisms symmetrically.

### P5 - Terminal adjudication

Each proposition must end as one of:

```text
supported_for_human_review
retained_candidate_only
bounded_non_link
falsified
requires_additional_acquisition
source_restricted
source_unavailable
```

No average score may replace proposition-level adjudication.

### P6 - Update and provenance

Version the original language, factual correction, surviving mechanism, new evidence, and reason for every change.

## The final-five-percent rule

The last causal join is often the most expensive part of the proof chain. It may sit behind:

- privileged communications;
- private contracts;
- redacted procurement attachments;
- proprietary logs;
- sealed litigation;
- fragmented record custody;
- undocumented discretion;
- a counterfactual the intervention prevented from existing.

An unfinished final join does not prove that the observer perceived nothing. It creates a named acquisition problem.

It also does not permit the project to write the missing bridge as fact.

## Model disagreement

Model resistance may reveal that the available ontology recognizes explicit acts more easily than distributed institutional forms. It is not evidence that the substantive theory is true.

The disagreement log should preserve:

```text
original human claim
model reformulation
reason for rejection
missing distinction
later evidence
current disposition
```

## Seed proof of concept

The March 2025 manuscript *Beyond Forced Compliance: Building an Accountable AI Future* is the first preserved proof of concept for this protocol.

It detected, before the later estate existed:

- strategic ambiguity;
- action outrunning review;
- fragmented responsibility;
- opaque technical mediation;
- retrospective proof burdens;
- the need for immutable logs;
- a right to pause;
- structured appeal;
- outcome-based reality tracking.

Its event-level overreach remains preserved alongside the durable mechanism. The protocol succeeds only if correction makes the theory more precise rather than merely rewriting history.

## Boundary

```text
an early signal is not a finding
an unavailable receipt is not a non-event
model resistance is not proof
survival under correction is stronger than agreement
```
