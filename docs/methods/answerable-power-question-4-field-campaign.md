## No-adverse shadow law

F4 permits only historical replay, synthetic rehearsal, de-identified retrospective analysis, or prospective observation whose output is technically and legally inaccessible to the operative decision maker until the real decision is final and unalterable by the shadow result.

The output may not determine, inform, delay, escalate, worsen, or create a record used to determine a real person's rights, benefits, employment, liberty, access, eligibility, reputation, or material opportunity.

Admission requires all predicates. No score may average away a missing standing, custody, non-use, publication, stopping, retaliation, remedy, expiry, or successor condition.
