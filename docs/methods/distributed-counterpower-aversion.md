# Distributed counterpower aversion

## Status

`DCA-H01` is a canonical **AT-2 field hypothesis**. It is not an actor edge, prevalence finding, motive attribution, psychological diagnosis, coordination conclusion, common-purpose conclusion, publication clearance, or adoption claim.

## Working proposition

> Self-sealing sovereignty can emerge as distributed counterpower aversion: separate actors with different ideologies and local objectives repeatedly preserve the protected center's freedom from binding correction.

The negative constitution under test is:

> No external contradiction may become binding unless the protected center admits it on terms the center controls.

## Personal and outsider-recognition boundary

A status–output mismatch can generate a research hypothesis. It cannot establish that an industry, political tendency, venture network, defense ecosystem, or audience is collectively monitoring, repelled by, coordinating against, or suppressing a person.

Those claims require bounded receipts for the exact audience, communication or observation, decision or block, citation or invitation, nonresponse, and institutional consequence. Silence is not motive evidence.
