# Counter-Selector Batch 02 artifact acquisition

Wave 07 applies the Wave 02 qualifying-artifact contract to `CS-AQ-B02`, one object from each denominator class.

The acquisition unit is a source-addressable work object, formal decision record, correction sequence, or bounded failure ledger. A source route, public label, settlement, policy architecture, later outcome, or institutional summary does not qualify by itself.

Four packets pass the acquisition gate:

- two artifact packets preserve a specialist evidence-channel failure and an Inspector General audit work product;
- one mechanism packet preserves observed external merits adjudication and record correction;
- one failure packet preserves a distributed institutional failure ledger.

The mechanism and failure packets are not person or partnership findings. Every packet removes identity, class, status, and source-route cues before review. Acquisition creates no review result, operator finding, field-test eligibility, ranking, or graph effect.
