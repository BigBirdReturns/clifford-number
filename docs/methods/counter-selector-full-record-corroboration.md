# Counter-Selector full-record corroboration

This method distinguishes four states that are commonly collapsed:

```text
full participating-institution report
≠ independent Counter-Selector review

named participation in a joint action
≠ sole authorship or sole causality

institutional continuity through a transition
≠ a direct person-to-person state-package handoff

archival series description
≠ custody of the underlying item
```

A corroboration may strengthen the source addressability, chronology, or scope of an already supported dimension without creating a new dimension support. The builder therefore preserves separate counts for person-action corroboration, existing-dimension corroboration, custody-scope refinement, new dimension support, sole-person causality upgrade, and direct handoff receipt.

Support evidence is accumulated adversarially. A record that makes a candidate's action more visible can simultaneously make support-adjusted surplus less supportable by exposing staff, funding, institutional access, observer infrastructure, and other operators.

Promotion remains prohibited unless the complete Counter-Selector contract, direct handoff requirements, external review, and field-test authority are independently satisfied.
