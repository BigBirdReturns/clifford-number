# Counter-Selector method

## Purpose

The Counter-Selector is a reverse-recognition protocol for finding operators and teams whose **observed operational residue exceeds what conventional social metadata would predict**.

It does not search for people who resemble one founder, one personality, one political tendency, or one biography. It searches for bounded evidence of locomotion:

```text
current state
→ real constraint
→ valid next state
→ preserved custody
→ recoverable handoff
```

The program exists because conventional selectors often use title, employer, credential, narrow specialization, polish, and sponsorship as low-cost proxies for capability. Those proxies can be useful. They become dangerous when an evaluator's inability to reconstruct a capability becomes a negative capability finding.

The governing rule is:

> Evaluate the artifact and state transition before evaluating the biography. An evaluator's inability to reconstruct a capability creates an acquisition requirement, not a negative capability finding.

## Parent and authority boundary

The program is an AT-2 candidate protocol under `DCA-H01`. It is a practical response to status-dependent recognition failure, selection effects, and counterfactual foreclosure. It does not prove DCA-H01, SSC-H01, personal hostility, industry suppression, or prevalence.

```text

```

No candidate record may create a person edge. No score may authorize employment, funding, exclusion, adverse action, or public identity release.

## The reverse filter

Conventional selection typically begins with legibility:

```text
credential
title
employer
network
specialty
polish
institutional endorsement
```

The Counter-Selector begins with traces:

```text
What valid object changed because this person acted?
What difficult state became recoverable?
What did another operator safely inherit?
What happened when a requirement changed?
What did the candidate update after contradiction?
What costs were externalized onto other people?
```

Identity and support context are not erased. They are sequenced. The artifact receives a blind-first review where lawful and practicable. Identity, support, authority, and state load are then reintroduced before any disposition.

## Eight operational residues

1. **Support-adjusted surplus** asks whether the output exceeds what documented support, time, tools, authority, and stability would normally predict. Precarity itself is not evidence of talent.
2. **Cross-domain transfer** asks whether the same locomotion method survives a genuinely different local grammar. A long interest list is not transfer.
3. **Exception handling** asks what happens when the mez breaks: false source, removed tool, changed requirement, dependency failure, or revealed harm.
4. **Custody and restore** asks whether borrowed accounts, data, equipment, projects, people, and records return usable and attributable.
5. **Model elasticity** asks whether accurate contradiction can enter without retaliation, concealment, or permanent rupture.
6. **Governed capacity** asks whether force and attention remain proportional rather than being performed for rank.
7. **Non-zero-sum orientation** asks whether another capable actor enlarges the system or must be diminished, absorbed, or displaced.
8. **Epistemic restraint** asks whether observation, inference, proof ceiling, missing receipt, alternative explanation, and falsifier remain distinct.

These dimensions are recorded as a vector. They are not summed into a universal merit score.

## Eight review stages

```text
CS-S0 artifact intake
CS-S1 blind-first review
CS-S2 support ledger
CS-S3 transfer test
CS-S4 disruption test
CS-S5 custody and handoff
CS-S6 second-party review
CS-S7 bounded collaboration
```

A candidate may stop or withdraw at any stage. A candidate who performs well on one bounded task has not received a finding of general intelligence, moral worth, founder quality, or universal fit.

## False-positive controls

The protocol must fail closed against the mythology of the persecuted outsider genius.

It explicitly tests for:

- grievance-only narratives in which every failure proves persecution;
- secret projects kept unfalsifiable to preserve imaginary value;
- AI-oracle motive analysis that substitutes narrative coherence for receipts;
- charismatic novelty claims without artifact, transfer, or custody;
- high output generated by externalizing cleanup or damage;
- capability that disappears when one tool or privileged interface is removed;
- scripted performance that collapses under disruption; and
- affinity mimicry, where shared politics, taste, language, or enemy selection is mistaken for operational fit.

The Counter-Selector is successful only if it can reject candidates who flatter its founding theory.

## Privacy, fairness, and candidate sovereignty

Protected characteristics, political alignment, poverty, trauma, and AI use are excluded from scoring. They may appear only where required to understand lawful support conditions, access constraints, privacy, safety, or task context.

Identity is withheld by default. Public traces do not authorize identity release. Collaboration requires consent, bounded scope, reciprocal value or compensation, a stop rule, and a clean exit.

## Field use

Wave 00 begins without named candidates. It freezes ten search lanes, positive controls, false-positive controls, the schema, and the review order.

The first empirical sequence should be:

1. one credentialed positive control;
2. one consented low-legibility positive control;
3. one grievance-only false-positive control;
4. one identity-blind versus identity-visible comparison;
5. one bounded unfamiliar task;
6. one deliberate disruption;
7. one handoff to a second operator; and
8. one independent disposition review.

Every reversal and reviewer disagreement remains in the denominator.

## Failure conditions

The program weakens or fails if blind review adds no information, the residue vector does not predict bounded collaboration value, disruption and custody tests add no predictive value, low-legibility candidates do not outperform matched controls after support adjustment, or the system begins rewarding grievance, confidence, similarity to the founder, or political affinity.

The final anti-capture rule is:

> The Counter-Selector must remain capable of publishing that its own selector failed.
