# Counter-Selector method

## Purpose

Wave 00 is preserved as the protocol-hardening parent. `CS-W00-SUP-01` append-preservingly replaces only its premature numeric scale with explicit evidence states; review order, controls, search lanes, privacy, consent, and anti-monopoly boundaries remain intact.

The Counter-Selector is a reverse-recognition protocol for recovering operators, teams, partnerships, controls, and discarded alternatives whose **observable operational residue may exceed what conventional social metadata predicts**.

It is not a hidden-genius list. It does not search for people who resemble one founder, one biography, one political tendency, or one preferred personality. It searches for bounded state transitions:

```text
current state
→ real constraint
→ valid next state
→ preserved custody
→ recoverable handoff
```

The governing rule is:

> Evaluate the artifact and state transition before evaluating the biography. An evaluator's inability to reconstruct a capability creates an acquisition requirement, not a negative capability finding.

## Six-class denominator

The first denominator contains exactly five unique objects in each class:

1. **Positive operator traces** justify a bounded test without presuming capability.
2. **False-positive outsider controls** attack the theory where rejection, grievance, accident, or discipline could be mistaken for capacity.
3. **Ordinary specialists** keep strong bounded expertise separate from cross-domain transfer.
4. **High-status selected operators** prevent low legibility from becoming an automatic bonus and status from becoming an automatic penalty.
5. **Repair-capable partnership traces** preserve possible stays, reversals, remedies, evidence custody, or resumability.
6. **Brittle or failed partnership traces** preserve possible contradiction, custody, authorship, or handoff breakdowns.

Every source object counts once. Failed paths, controls, reversals, inaccessible evidence, and later class reassignments remain in the denominator.

## Blind-first sequence

The source route establishes provenance, not a blind review. The operational sequence is:

```text
CS-S0 source-routed intake
CS-S1 blind-first artifact review
CS-S2 support and state-load ledger
CS-S3 bounded transfer test
CS-S4 contradiction and disruption test
CS-S5 custody and handoff

```

Identity and support context are sequenced rather than erased. The blind packet withholds title, employer, school, follower count, demographic identity, political alignment, and network sponsorship where lawful and practicable. Identity, support, authority, and constraints return before any disposition.

## Eight operator dimensions

1. **Support-adjusted surplus** asks whether valid output exceeds documented support, authority, tools, staff, runway, and stability.
2. **Cross-domain transfer** asks whether the same governing operation survives a materially different local grammar.
3. **Exception handling** asks whether the operator detects propagation, preserves valid branches, and restores a recoverable state when the script breaks.
4. **Custody** asks whether entrusted accounts, data, equipment, systems, people, and unfinished work return usable and inspectable.
5. **Model elasticity** asks whether an accurate contradiction can enter without retaliation, concealment, or explanation mutation.
6. **Governed capacity** asks whether attention and force remain proportional to stakes rather than audience or rank pressure.
7. **Non-zero-sum orientation** asks whether another capable operator enlarges the system rather than triggering compulsory dominance or authorship capture.
8. **Epistemic restraint** asks whether observation, interpretation, hypothesis, receipt, non-link, and falsifier remain distinct.

The dimensions form a vector. They are not summed into a universal merit score or rank order.

## The bounded field test

`CS-FT-01` supplies an unfamiliar problem, incomplete information, one accurate contradiction, one deliberate disruption, and an independent handoff operator.

Success requires the candidate to preserve still-valid work, localize damage, update the model, restore a recoverable state, and leave another operator able to continue safely. Failure, withdrawal, and inability to hand off are permanent results, not embarrassing residue to remove.

## False-positive filter

The protocol must reject stories that flatter its founding theory. It fails closed against:

- every failure proving persecution;
- every success proving superiority;
- criticism becoming betrayal;
- secret projects preserving imaginary value;
- AI narratives about motives or destiny replacing chronology and receipts;
- artifacts that cannot be handed off;
- failed paths that disappear;
- other people treated only as audiences, obstacles, or validation sources; and
- autonomy demanded for self while obedience is demanded from others.

A negative control is not a public finding that a person is fraudulent or untalented. It is an attack surface for the selector.

## Privacy and candidate sovereignty

Private friendship or relationship evidence cannot create a public actor edge. Public records do not authorize identity release. Protected characteristics, political alignment, poverty, trauma, and AI use are not scoring inputs. Collaboration requires consent, bounded scope, reciprocal value or compensation, a stop rule, exit, and withdrawal support.
