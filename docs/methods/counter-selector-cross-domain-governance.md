# Counter-Selector cross-domain governance audit

This method treats cross-domain transfer as a repeated governing operation, not a résumé count. A candidate must carry the same evidence-bounded operation across materially different problem and authority surfaces. Adjacent medical delivery, multiple stages of one engineering programme, or appointment to several offices are retained as controls rather than silently promoted.

Custody may advance at a bounded institutional-transition scope when an outgoing state, successor-capable operating body, and later continuity are all source-addressable. That state still does not equal a direct person-to-person handoff, an inspectable state package, finality, or the field-test handoff gate.

Every supported trace retains support context, countermodels, missing receipts, and a graph-inert authority ceiling. Identity-label-removed review exports remain potentially inferable and are not external reviews until a genuinely independent reviewer returns a source-addressable adjudication.
