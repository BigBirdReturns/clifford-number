# Counter-Selector exception falsification

Wave 20 does not ask whether Elliot Richardson held an unusual title or was associated with a successful election mission. It asks whether the acquired record preserves person-attributable interventions at failed or contested gates, and whether those interventions survive subtraction of the professional ONUVEN staff, Jimmy Carter, the Supreme Electoral Council, distributed observers, communications infrastructure, high reputation, and formal authority.

The audit treats a retrospective first-person transcript as attributable evidence with a self-report ceiling. Four intervention objects may support bounded exception handling; mission outcome, appointment prestige, and collective safeguards may not. Retrospective assessment changes and candid self-critique remain below model-elasticity threshold unless a contradiction produces an attributable changed decision, a repaired relationship, and a recoverable object.

Custody is tested adversarially. The prior Law of the Sea institutional-transition finding remains, but the Nicaragua letter is counterevidence because no copy or recipient acknowledgment was acquired. Archival finding aids are routes, not receipts; no archive request, reviewer request, or subject contact is authorized.
