# Counter-Selector observability Batch B blind review

Wave 28 applies the Wave 27 review discipline to the remaining Wave 26 observability packets.

The review unit is the inspectable artifact, not the public identity. Packet construction removes identity, project, platform, publisher, source-route, reputation, traffic, and prior-class cues while preserving chronology, observable state change, support context, counterevidence, and falsifiers. The resulting artifacts remain highly inferable, so the programme claims identity-label removal rather than identity blindness.

Two fresh-context passes are required:

1. artifact validity: determine whether the bounded transition and work objects are coherent;
2. adversarial countermodel: test ordinary explanations, team and infrastructure causality, support dependence, attribution ceilings, and missing handoff state.

Collective assurance can retain function-level dimensions without becoming a person. A living service founder can retain person-level update evidence while service custody, access control, and public-interest policy remain at function scope. Support ledgers must expose commercial, team, proprietary, platform, customer, partner, and reputation inputs before any surplus claim.

No same-system pass counts as external review. No review authorizes contact, collaboration, field testing, promotion, ranking, identity profiling, or graph construction.
