# Counter-Selector post-review gap audit

Wave 05 tests what survives after blind review. It does not enlarge a bounded support because a biography is impressive or because an official correction mechanism exists.

For the decision packet, the method acquires support, authority, delegation, custody, and transfer context. Substantial formal support lowers—not raises—the authority to claim support-adjusted surplus. An unrelated role is not cross-domain transfer without a work artifact and outcome.

For the correction packet, the method orders every known event chronologically. A later MSPB order corrects the earlier broad statement that the agency declined reinstatement: the agency declined voluntary correction, then asserted reinstatement in compliance with a stay. Reinstatement under external order is not internal partnership repair, and a stay is not final merits.

Public-search absences are bounded search receipts, never proof of nonexistence. Historical files remain immutable; corrections are append-only supersessions.
