# Counter-Selector same-matter custody and public-finality method

Wave 06 separates four states that are easy to collapse:

1. **Matter continuity** — a later office or custodian acts on the same bounded matter.
2. **Successor operational custody** — successor actors possess enough of the matter to produce an observable work object.
3. **Direct handoff** — an attributable transfer record links outgoing and successor custodians.
4. **Prior-operator attribution** — evidence supports crediting the earlier operator for later outcomes.

Only the first two are observed for `CS-BLIND-0016`. The March 29, 2007 indictment announcement, indictment, and later appellate-finality advisory establish a successor-action chain on the same courthouse matter. They do not identify a handoff memorandum, transferred decision object, or successor acknowledgment attributable to the outgoing operator.

For `CS-BLIND-0021`, public Board surfaces remain a bounded absence rather than proof of no disposition. MSPB states that initial decisions and case files may require FOIA access. Wave 06 records that access route without sending a request or treating the route as evidence.

The method therefore enforces:

```text
same matter ≠ direct handoff
successor action ≠ predecessor attribution
later success ≠ original work object
retrospective testimony ≠ contemporaneous decision object
FOIA route ≠ acquired evidence
public absence ≠ nonexistence
```
