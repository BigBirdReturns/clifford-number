# Allocator-war lake integration Wave 21

Wave 21 joins two canonical but unequal authority states to the sealed evidence-lake stack.

The first import is the merged `AW-WL-01` allocator-war waterline and its SSC Wave 01 source, maintainer-review, and targeted-acquisition lineage. Those fourteen observations may retain their bounded reviewed classifications. They do not become second-party reviewed, adjudicated, publication-cleared, or graph-active.

The second import is the merged SSC Wave 02 intake. Its eight observations remain unreviewed. They may create acquisition routes and controls, but they may not create a finding or inherit the Wave 01 evidence ceiling.

## Lake objects

```text
observation registry     reviewed and intake rows with separate authority
waterline registry       six reviewed classes plus eight intake frontiers
estate registry          eleven consolidated acquisition consumers
program registry         six one-way program feeds
projection               exact generated mirrors of the source rows
reconciliation           source, projection, index, reachability, and graph custody
```

Each imported source is pinned by repository commit, path, byte count, and SHA-256. The imported file remains authoritative for its own state. The Wave 21 registries are routing and indexing products.

## Routing law

Controls, non-links, refused inferences, nulls, and open denominators travel with supportive records. A consumer receives an observation and an acquisition obligation. It does not receive a finding, relationship, participation row, prevalence estimate, common purpose, or publication authority.

The estate queues cover executive appointments and ethics, regulatory markets, public money and industrial policy, U.S. defense, transatlantic defense innovation, Dialog, IP and data rights, labor and workforce mobility, legislative and political finance, public-interest crossings, and judicial or administrative adjudication.

The program feeds cover K0, DCA-H01, M-05 Answerable Power, POOF, Counter-Selector, and the core-thesis C1 through C7 conversion chain.
