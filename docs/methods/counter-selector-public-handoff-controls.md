# Counter-Selector public handoff positive-control method

Wave 30 calibrates the handoff gate against two public transitions that expose different custody components.

A handoff claim is decomposed into outgoing intent, authorizing rule, incoming identity, authority or credential transfer, recipient role occupancy, dependency and open-decision inventory, rollback and safe-decline conditions, successor action, and observed continuity.

The method records a bounded authority-transfer positive when public evidence joins authority change to successor operation. It records a complete direct handoff only when an attributable outgoing state package, recipient acknowledgment, recoverable authority and dependencies, rollback conditions, and successful continuation are all inspectable.

Role succession, key rotation, and uninterrupted operation are useful evidence. None is silently converted into person-level custody, support-adjusted surplus, operator status, contact authority, or a graph edge.
