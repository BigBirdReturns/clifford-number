# Counter-Selector repair-continuation audit

Wave 18 distinguishes **refusal**, **repair continuation**, **verification custody**, **learning-object custody**, and **safe handoff**. A person may receive bounded support only when an official, source-addressable object attributes the relevant conduct at the stated scope. Team success is not silently converted into individual surplus or universal capability.

Identity-block review is fail-closed. Searchable PDF text may establish that no name was located in the acquired text, but failed visual confirmation cannot prove that no visual-only identity evidence exists. Named meeting attendance is not warning authorship.

External-review exports remove the identity label but do not claim true identity blindness: a historically distinctive artifact may remain inferable. Export preparation, source routing, and review readiness do not count as an independent review or authorize contact.
