# Clifford–Thiel–Trump wrap-up

This is the repository-grounded conclusion after applying the Arcadia game-trail
lessons to the data already present. It adds no new external research.

## The answer

The current corpus does **not** establish a material Matt Clifford–Peter
Thiel–Donald Trump path.

- Matt Clifford is the graph anchor. The authoritative ledger supports his
  official AI Opportunities Action Plan role. A prior Faculty-investor row was
  removed because it rested on an unrecoverable analysis note, lacked dates,
  and was not supported by the master investor list.
- Peter Thiel and Clifford are both named on the Dialog directory surface, but
  that 116-name roster is deliberately `hop_eligible: false`. It proves a
  listing, not attendance, agreement, a transaction, policy influence, or
  coordination. Thiel's compiled Clifford Number is therefore `null`.
- Donald Trump belongs to the separate presidential officeholder and
  disclosure pipeline, not the surface ledger. Twenty-one official OGE PDFs
  have been parsed for Trump, but the cross-corpus process currently reports
  zero resolved legal entities and zero promoted crossings.

The machine-readable disposition is in
`data/research/clifford-thiel-trump-wrap-up.json`.
The companion `data/research/officeholder-predicate-disposition-matrix.json`
spells out all 40 president-by-predicate cells so coverage gaps and unexecuted
predicates cannot be mistaken for negative findings.

## What survives

The repository supports three separate observations:

1. Clifford occupies source-addressed UK government-policy roles. Additional
   venture and agency roles remain in the research layer at varying receipt
   strength and are not silently promoted into hops.
2. Thiel occupies source-addressed company, fund, and Dialog roles in broader
   repository material; only Dialog is present in the authoritative surface
   ledger, where it remains context-only.
3. Trump has a large, source-addressed disclosure and campaign-finance intake,
   but the identity and temporal screen has not promoted any office-business
   crossing.

Those observations are useful. They are not a three-person relationship.

### The Faculty shortcut was removed

The graph previously encoded Clifford as a reported personal investor in
Faculty. That participation generated direct hops to Ben Warner, Marc Warner,
and Saul Klein, plus downstream paths. Its only participant receipt was an
unrecoverable analysis note, its dates were blank, and the master investor list
did not name Clifford. The participation is now excluded from authoritative
hop computation and preserved as a blocked candidate in the wrap-up JSON.

## What fails

### Clifford ↔ Thiel through Dialog

This is a real co-listing but not a Clifford Number hop. The roster is too
dense and its predicate too weak. Treating every listed member as directly
connected to every other member would manufacture 6,670 pairwise implications
from one directory and erase the distinction between a list, a contract, a
board, an investment, and a dated working group.

### Clifford ↔ Thiel through capital or AI policy

The existing corpus contains the two sides of a plausible research question,
but no named co-investment, board, contract, procurement, or advisory surface
that joins the men. Shared sector prominence is the ordinary-market null, not
an edge.

### Thiel or Clifford ↔ Trump

Trump-related brand, property, committee, payee, family-company, and disclosure
names must remain separate legal and predicate nodes. The current OGE/FEC
pipeline screened 1,172 lexical candidates across the presidential cohort,
found no temporally eligible pair, resolved no cross-source legal entity, and
promoted no crossing. These are pipeline results, not proof of absence.

## Why the Arcadia lesson matters

Arcadia showed that a person identity can resolve while corporate equality and
authorship remain refused; a registered-agent relationship can be real without
proving ownership; and a transaction date can be official while its parties
remain unverified. The same firewall now governs this wrap-up:

1. exact person or legal entity;
2. material source-explicit predicate;
3. compatible dated interval;
4. independent receipt support;
5. eligible bounded surface or transaction;
6. ordinary institutional or market alternative considered.

The conclusion is therefore narrower and stronger than a network collage:
Clifford is clear, Thiel is contextually adjacent but not connected by an
eligible path, and Trump is currently in a separate screened corpus lane. A
future path must arrive as a named legal vehicle, dated transaction, contract,
appointment, board, co-investment, or other material receipt—not as another
name on a list.
