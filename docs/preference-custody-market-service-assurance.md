3. Capacity stock-flow mismatch produces queues, rationing, denial, unmet need, and long waits beneath complete-looking coverage.
4. Fees, pass-through, reduced availability, affordability loss, demand adaptation, and uptake changes remain hidden beneath a zero price publication.

8. Historical assurance is inherited after market, service, supplier, provider, price, capacity, access, policy, workflow, population, and scale succession.

## Refusal rules

- A declared market boundary is not the operational system boundary.
- Nominal untreated units are not an untreated-market counterfactual under cross-market exposure.
- Published capacity coverage is not staffed, available, usable, or unconstrained capacity.
- Completed services are not the eligible, attempted, queued, denied, abandoned, or unserved denominator.
- A zero published denial rate is not zero true denial when denied and unserved units can be excluded.
- A zero published price change is not zero fee, pass-through, availability, affordability, demand, or uptake change.
- Nominal channel availability is not usable geographic, temporal, language, disability, digital, documentary, or administrative access.
- Published access coverage is not complete access for the eligible population.
- Published service records are not a complete service denominator.
- Aggregate quality is not stable service version, dose, provider mix, severity mix, or survivor-independent quality.
- A stable quality score is not absence of cream-skimming, deterioration, or provider selection.
- Historical market assurance is not current assurance after system succession.
- A public `market_service_verified` status is not complete, current, correctable, or publicly authorized market-service evidence.
- Market-service assurance failure is not proof of coercion, manipulation, discrimination, breach, misconduct, coordination, common purpose, or intent.
