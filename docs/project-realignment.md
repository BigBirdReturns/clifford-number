# Project realignment under the core thesis

## New center of gravity

The project is moving from broad relationship discovery toward longitudinal reconstruction of public-private conversion chains.

```text
previous center:
actor, source, case, estate, and trail topology

new center:
problem framing
→ option-set formation
→ public conversion
→ control architecture
→ dependency or compulsion
→ residual value
→ reversibility and counterpower
```

This is an extension of the existing architecture, not a replacement. The bounded-surface hop compiler, identity controls, receipt custody, estate registry, game trails, case ledgers, structured reports, and independent-review boundary remain authoritative.

## What changes immediately

### 1. Acquisition priorities

New breadth is subordinate to decisive depth. A source acquisition should answer at least one of these questions:

- who authored the public problem or option set;
- which gate or instrument made a private proposition publicly operative;
- who acquired durable control rights;
- when an optional arrangement became default, mandatory, indispensable, or irreversible;
- who retained residual value after public risk or authority was applied;
- whether public inspection, appeal, substitution, termination, recovery, or exit remained practical.

Biographies, rosters, announcements, and contract ceilings remain useful routing records. They are not substitutes for operative instruments, complete denominators, decision files, rights schedules, implementation records, and counterweights.

### 2. Case structure

High-value cases should progressively add:

- problem-frame objects;
- role claims with date windows and authority;
- option-set snapshots with exclusions and unsuccessful alternatives;
- conversion events tied to a public gate;
- control-rights records;
- dependency and compulsion states;
- residual-value positions;
- intent records;
- reversibility and counterpower tests.

Existing claims, events, receipts, and qualifications remain the factual source of truth.

### 3. Estate work

Every estate remains a durable domain corpus. The alignment file supplies its phase, conversion-stage, archetype, record, report, and falsification obligations.

Estate work should now produce one of four outputs:

1. an independently receipted conversion-chain link;
2. a bounded non-link or ordinary comparator;
3. an unresolved transition with a named decisive record;
4. a falsifier that narrows or rejects a thesis proposition.

### 4. Game trails

Game trails remain candidate-only routing objects. Future trail families should prefer transitions such as:

```text
problem frame → option-set rule
option-set rule → public gate
public gate → operative instrument
instrument → control right
control right → dependency
public risk → private residual value
dependency → appeal, substitution, or exit
```

A trail may never infer one of these transitions from chronology, shared source infrastructure, ideology, or actor recurrence alone.

### 5. Visualizations

The estate map and game-trail matrix remain the broad index. They are supplemented by:

- post-Cold War phase rivers;
- doctrine-to-deployment maps;
- option-set narrowing funnels;
- role-stacking matrices;
- institutional control stacks;
- public-risk/private-rights flows;
- compulsion and exit matrices.

Each visualization must state its task, evidence basis, missing transitions, and non-inference boundary.

### 6. Reports

Reporter reports evolve from a general evidence matrix into task-specific conversion dossiers. The required report types are defined in `data/project/core-thesis.json`.

Every report must still:

- resolve factual language to canonical claims and events;
- display qualifications and receipt status;
- show counterweights, nulls, and unsuccessful paths;
- distinguish structural convergence from actor-specific intent;
- identify the decisive missing records;
- and stop before generating a conclusion or approval.

### 7. Human review

Intentionality review becomes explicit. A reviewer must adjudicate structural convergence, opportunistic exploitation, deliberate market shaping, institutional replacement, coercive or ideological governance, and personal extraction separately.

No workflow success, graph position, contract value, marketing phrase, ideology, or profit can assign a level.

## Initial migration sequence

The first conversion-oriented migrations should be:

1. **Anduril access and ownership** — option-set formation, acquisition gates, data and interface rights, public risk, substitution, and residual control.
2. **UK AI policy / Clifford Number** — private agenda formation, adoption, state-capacity design, supplier implementation, platform position, and exit.
3. **Palantir institutional position** — doctrine, litigation and gate shaping, enterprise deployment, third-party ecosystem role, data and ontology control, and public operating capacity.
4. **Arcadia Formation** — planning narrative, authority formation, signed instruments, public cash flow, parcel/title/debt control, and residual land value.
5. **Public money and industrial policy** — authority, instrument, ceiling, obligation, outlay, milestone, performance, recovery, and residual rights.

The migrations do not promote claims or change publication status. They expose which conversion links already exist and which remain open.

## Completion law

M-04A is complete when the thesis and estate alignment compile deterministically and are visible as a public atlas.

M-04B is complete when the new analytical objects can be attached to canonical claims, events, instruments, and receipts without creating independent factual prose.
