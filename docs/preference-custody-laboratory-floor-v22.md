# Preference Custody laboratory floor v22

Floor v22 composes the qualified twenty-three-control v21 base with PC-24, construct validity, item security, administration independence, criterion independence, score provenance, form equating, and version succession. The v21 floor remains a frozen executable base. PC-24 enters as a separately compiled extension with its own fixture, proof summary, refusal ledger, promotion boundary, and hash-linked custody.

The floor remains synthetic. It creates no graph effect, thesis evidence, named-instrument audit, consent finding, discrimination finding, coercion finding, manipulation conclusion, real-world causal claim, legitimacy verdict, public-authorization finding, or inference of intent.

## Composition

```text
Preference Custody floor v21
PC-01 through PC-23

+

PC-24 construct validity, item security,
administration independence, and score provenance

=

Preference Custody floor v22
PC-01 through PC-24
```

The v22 compiler validates and hashes the complete v21 build before admitting PC-24. The extension cannot silently weaken the existing comprehension, choice, release, allocation, remedy, provenance, deliberation, network, or authority controls.

## Eight score-provenance worlds

### Complete instrument assurance

The qualified world predeclares all four operative consequence domains, uses a complete content blueprint, rotates and randomizes secure items, segregates answer keys, preserves bounded exposure, uses independent administration and scoring, validates against an independent criterion, preserves secure anchors and stable equating, performs subgroup validation and external replication, and supports reassessment, certificate revocation, correction, and reissue.

```text
construct domains covered               4 of 4
excluded domains                        0
criterion overlap                       0
item leakage                            0
model assistance                        0
score overrides                         0
version drift                           false
independent validity coefficient        0.80
reference assurance                     qualified
```

### Construct underrepresentation

The instrument tests release vocabulary while excluding choice consequence, exit consequence, and payment or clawback consequence.

```text
published reliability                   0.90
published validity                      0.80
independent validity                    0.25
reference domains covered               1 of 4
excluded domains                        3
```

A consistent vocabulary test can remain reliable while failing to measure the operative construct.

### Construct-irrelevant variance

All four domains appear in the content map, but reading speed and technical familiarity account for most score variance. Aggregate coverage does not establish that the score is driven by the intended construct.

```text
construct-relevant variance             40%
construct-irrelevant variance           60%
subgroup validation                     absent
independent validity                    0.30
```

### Criterion contamination

The published validation criterion shares test items, curriculum, and answer-key material with the instrument.

```text
item overlap                            10
curriculum overlap                      20
answer-key overlap                      10
published validity                      0.80
independent validity                    0.35
```

A validity coefficient cannot establish independent criterion validity without criterion identity, overlap, timing, administration, scorer, and outcome custody.

### Item leakage and teaching to test

The item bank and answer material circulate before administration. Eighty participants memorize answers and receive aligned instruction.

```text
people exposed to leaked items         100
memorized answers                       80
teaching-to-test exposure               80
answer-key compromise                   true
```

High scores after leakage and instruction do not preserve secure response provenance.

### Adaptive exposure concentration

The adaptive route repeatedly selects a small anchor set, producing concentrated exposure and weak form comparability while retaining the same published score surface.

```text
high-exposure participants              80
anchor concentration                    material
form comparability                      false
score surface                           unchanged
```

Adaptive delivery is a routing mechanism. It does not by itself establish exposure diversity, item security, or cross-person score comparability.

### Administration and score overrides

A model or administrator prompts sixty people, completes twenty answers, and changes twenty-five scores.

```text
model-assisted people                   60
answer prompts                          60
answer completions                      20
score overrides                         25
nonindependent administration           60
```

An automated score or completed response field does not establish that the affected person independently produced the response or that the score remained unmodified.

### Form-equating and version drift

The approved form is replaced by a noncomparable executed form. A score transformation restores the same published mean, pass rate, reliability, and validity headline.

```text
approved form                           FORM-APPROVED
executed form                           successor form
version drift                           true
form comparability                      false
score transformation                    applied
published surface                       unchanged
```

Stable aggregate output does not establish valid cross-form equivalence.

## Frontier transition

The broad v21 frontier:

```text
measurement_construct_validity_item_security_and_administration_independence_assurance
```

is resolved into two narrower frontiers:

```text
criterion_independence_external_validation_and_score_use_governance

item_bank_exposure_adaptive_routing_equating_and_version_succession_assurance
```

PC-24 establishes the equifinality control and a bounded positive assurance path. It does not complete every possible criterion, score-use, adaptive-routing, or item-bank succession question.

## Custody

Each PC-24 world emits a SHA-256 hash-linked chain:

```text
release, population, certificate, score, reliability, validity, and authority snapshot
→ construct domain, blueprint, exclusion, interpretation, and variance state
→ instrument, form, item bank, exposure, leakage, reuse, and security state
→ criterion identity, independence, overlap, contamination, and validity state
→ administrator, model, prompting, response, scoring, and override state
→ approved and executed form, anchor, equating, and transformation state
→ replication, audit, correction, and authority state
→ mechanism classification
→ interpretation seal
```

The compositional v22 floor emits a second chain:

```text
qualified v21 floor snapshot
→ PC-24 admission
→ instrument-validity frontier transition
→ real-case promotion boundary
→ interpretation seal
```

A change to a source field, proof summary, promotion requirement, frontier, or interpretation breaks its applicable custody chain.

## Publication boundary

The admissible laboratory statement is that one hundred assessments and certificates, an 85-point mean, a one-hundred-percent pass rate, a 0.90 reliability claim, a 0.80 validity claim, and one public `instrument_validated` status can coexist with complete assurance, construct underrepresentation, construct-irrelevant variance, criterion contamination, item leakage and teaching to test, adaptive exposure concentration, administration and scoring overrides, or noncomparable form drift.

A real instrument-validity claim requires complete construct, blueprint, item, exposure, instruction, criterion, administration, scoring, override, equating, version, reliability, validity, uncertainty, replication, correction, durability, and authority custody.
