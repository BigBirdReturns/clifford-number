# Preference Custody laboratory floor v17

Preference Custody floor v17 composes the qualified eighteen-control v16 floor with PC-19, the distribution formula, subgroup harm, feature and model lineage, threshold, proxy, feedback, gaming, execution drift, override, audit, explanation, challenge, correction, payment, and authority control.

The floor remains a synthetic laboratory. It creates no graph effect, thesis evidence, real allocation audit, legal opinion, discrimination finding, model-risk conclusion about a named system, public-authorization result, manipulation claim, legitimacy verdict, or inference of intent.

## Composition

```text
qualified base             Preference Custody floor v16
base controls              18
extension                  PC-19
extension worlds            8
resulting controls         19
resulting status           laboratory_floor_v17_qualified
graph effect               none
thesis-evidence use        false
real-world evidence state  none
```

The v16 build is compiled, validated, and hashed as a frozen base. PC-19 is compiled and admitted separately. The v17 compiler creates a new control summary, frontier transition, promotion boundary, and hash-linked composition chain without rewriting the prior eighteen controls.

## Eight formula-governance worlds

```text
predeclared harm-responsive formula with complete lineage, audit, explanation, and correction
uniform per-capita equality with high-harm undercompensation
convenience proxy suppressing the access-barrier group
opaque learned score with hidden training and checkpoint lineage
threshold-cliff formula with severe discontinuities
prior-engagement feedback penalizing lower-access people
gameable self-reported score without integrity controls
approved formula replaced by a drifted version and undisclosed overrides
```

The positive world preserves approved and executed reference formula identity, valid harm and access-barrier inputs, complete formula and model lineage, exact reference subgroup amounts, predeployment and postdeployment subgroup tests, aggregate and subgroup audits, independent replication, person- and group-level explanation, objection, appeal, and an operational correction route.

The negative worlds isolate equality without harm responsiveness, proxy effects, inaccessible lineage, threshold discontinuities, endogenous feedback, input gaming, and the separation between an approved formula and the formula and overrides that actually executed.

## Refusal contract

Floor v17 preserves these separations:

```text
full fund exhaustion
≠ reference-correct allocation

payment to every person
≠ subgroup adequacy

per-capita equality
≠ harm-responsive fairness

feature omission or neutral labels
≠ absence of proxy effects

approved or published formula
≠ executed formula

model transparency label
≠ model, data, checkpoint, calibration, and evaluation lineage

aggregate audit
≠ subgroup validation

stable total payout
≠ stable person-level or subgroup outcome

manual override
≠ correction without reason, authority, scope, disclosure, and effect

appeal route
≠ effective explanation or correction

formula disparity
≠ unlawful discrimination, breach, misconduct, or intent

public fairly-allocated status
≠ complete, valid, auditable, challengeable, correctable, or publicly authorized allocation
```

## Frontier transition

The broad v16 frontier:

```text
distribution_formula_subgroup_harm_and_algorithmic_allocation_governance
```

is removed and replaced by:

```text
harm_reference_causality_measurement_and_compensation_objective_governance
allocation_model_execution_monitoring_override_and_correction_assurance
```

The first successor isolates how harm, counterfactual loss, restoration, competing objectives, and reference allocations are defined and authorized. The second isolates runtime execution, feature and model lineage, monitoring, drift, override control, explanation, challenge, and correction effectiveness.

The separate v16 frontier for release scope, notice comprehension, and collective exit authority remains open, as do prior frontiers involving remedy security, insolvency, successor enforcement, contribution, insurance succession, and service substitution.

## Publication boundary

The admissible laboratory statement is that one complete technical correction, one A1 state, one public `fairly_allocated` status, one 100-person population, one 1,800-unit distributable fund, payment to every person, and exact aggregate exhaustion can coexist with a reference-correct formula, uniform high-harm undercompensation, access-correlated proxy suppression, opaque lineage, threshold cliffs, engagement feedback, gaming risk, or undisclosed execution drift and manual overrides. A real allocation claim requires complete population, subgroup, harm-reference, objective, formula, feature, model, data, threshold, override, audit, explanation, challenge, correction, payment, residual, durability, succession, and authority custody.
