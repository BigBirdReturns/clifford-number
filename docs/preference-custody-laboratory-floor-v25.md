## Eight exposure-governance worlds

### Complete predeclared exposure map with stable treatment, stable network, unexposed controls, and current lineage

The qualified world preserves complete assignment, pre-treatment network and institutional-channel census, correct exposure classification, stable treatment versions and doses, stable topology, supported partial interference, identified peer-spillover estimand, complete falsification tests, current lineage, and operational correction. This is the one complete interference-assurance path.

### Direct peer spillover to nominal controls

Thirty nominal controls are connected to treated peers and are truly exposed while the public surface still reports zero exposed controls and zero spillover. A nominal control label is not an unexposed counterfactual.

### Shared institutional-channel contamination

Forty nominal controls are exposed through operators, facilities, policy, workflow, or other shared channels outside the person-level graph. A person network does not establish complete institutional or market exposure coverage.

### Cross-cluster diffusion beyond the partial-interference boundary

Twenty-five controls receive cross-cluster exposure through fifty cross-cluster edges. Zero observed cross-cluster edges cannot establish partial interference when the census or channel map can omit the relevant path.

### Incomplete network census with hidden exposure

The public node count remains complete while four hundred decisive edges are missing and forty controls are exposed through hidden paths. One hundred percent node coverage is not complete edge, channel, path, or exposure coverage.

### Multiple treatment versions and doses collapsed into one label

Thirty units receive multiple versions or doses while the publication treats all exposure as one stable treatment. A treatment label does not establish version, dose, delivery, or timing stability.

### Post-assignment endogenous network rewiring

One hundred edges rewire after assignment and twenty controls become exposed through the changed topology. Current topology cannot substitute for the pre-treatment map, and a post-treatment network cannot be used as if it were exogenous.

### Market and institutional saturation with general-equilibrium effects

All one hundred units receive ambient exposure or equilibrium feedback. The unit-level untreated counterfactual is unavailable even though the public surface remains `interference_adjusted`.

## Assignment, network, and channel custody

The floor separates assignment labels from exposure state. Every real-case promotion must preserve eligible, assigned, clustered, treated, controlled, directly exposed, indirectly exposed, truly unexposed, observed, decided, and affected denominators. Cluster identity, membership, randomization, concealment, compliance, crossover, timing, and succession remain machine-addressable.

Network custody records source, owner, node and edge denominators, identity resolution, version, collection time, missingness, censoring, direction, sign, weight, multiplex layers, hyperedges, and pre-treatment status. Channel custody separately records operators, facilities, policies, workflows, media, markets, geography, households, communications, and shared contexts.

## True and classified exposure

PC-27 preserves true exposure independently from the published exposure label. False negatives, false positives, ambiguity, censoring, and unresolved states cannot be collapsed into one coverage percentage. Exposure maps carry identity, version, owner, predeclaration, category, threshold, temporal window, path length, accumulation, decay, dose, treatment version, and uncertainty.

## Treatment versions and dynamic topology

Stable assignment is not stable treatment and is not stable topology. The floor requires an approved-versus-executed treatment crosswalk, version and dose custody, pre- and post-assignment topology, rewiring events, change points, and tests that prevent post-treatment network state from being laundered into the assignment model.

## Saturation and equilibrium

Partial interference, stable-unit, stable-treatment, network-stability, no-general-equilibrium, and positivity assumptions remain explicit. Saturation and equilibrium are not merely large spillovers: they can remove the untreated system counterfactual and change prices, capacity, availability, substitution, institutional behavior, and outcomes for every unit. PC-27 opens a narrower successor frontier rather than claiming that this broad control resolves those mechanisms.

## Estimation and falsification

A network-adjusted estimator is not a valid exposure model. Cluster-robust uncertainty does not correct exposure misclassification. Real-case promotion requires estimator identity, code, version, runtime, weighting, clustering, network adjustment, support, misclassification correction, uncertainty, negative controls, placebo edges, permutation tests, hidden-edge sensitivity, saturation tests, and alternative exposure maps.

## Boundary

The admissible laboratory statement is that cluster randomization, one hundred percent published network coverage, zero published exposed controls, a zero spillover report, one reported direct effect, and one public `interference_adjusted` status can coexist with complete assurance, peer spillover, institutional contamination, cross-cluster diffusion, hidden edges, treatment-version interference, endogenous rewiring, or saturation. A real interference-adjusted claim requires complete assignment, network, channel, exposure, treatment-version, topology, saturation, estimand, estimation, falsification, succession, correction, durability, and authority custody.
