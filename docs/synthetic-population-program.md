# Synthetic population research program

Status: staged research intake. This document and its manifest do not alter the canonical graph, a Clifford Number, a score, or publication-ready narration.

The program began with the Electric Twin capability-lineage case in GitHub issue #29. Issue #30 is the program index. The manifest at `contributions/inbox/research-batches/synthetic-population-program.json` freezes eighteen independently bounded child cases and twenty frontier candidates. Every row carries `graph_effect: none`; the frontier is explicitly inadmissible until each candidate is promoted to a bounded case.

## Why the cases are separate

The program distinguishes five mechanisms that cannot safely be collapsed:

1. **State-to-market continuity:** people, units, acquisitions, and capabilities moving across public and private venues.
2. **Legitimacy and governance:** validators, advisers, nonprofits, post-office controls, and analyst category-making.
3. **Deployment and influence:** publishers, consultation workflows, political use, and government or classified procurement.
4. **Technical and regulatory substrate:** data rights, benchmark design, cultural extrapolation, models, clouds, and subprocessors.
5. **Ownership and industry effects:** capital rights, board control, workforce change, and professional duties.

A person, company, investor, customer, academic, department, or broad professional network appearing in more than one case does not itself create an actor hop. Cross-case recurrence is computed only after every case independently establishes its selection universe, receipts, evidence state, dates, and refusal conditions.

## Selection rule

The same predicates and evidentiary burdens apply to the complete or declared comparator universe regardless of party, ideology, nationality, affiliation, investor identity, founder biography, customer identity, or anticipated news value. A named target may route discovery, but it cannot define membership.

Missing records, failed searches, ordinary career movement, successful independent validation, clean controls, and evidence against a working hypothesis remain first-class results. Source failure never becomes absence. Capability never becomes deployment. Investment never becomes operational control. Role overlap never becomes scientific compromise. Co-presence never becomes coordination.

## Manifest contract

Each bounded case must provide:

- a stable case and batch identifier;
- a public-interest question;
- a neutral selection unit and universe;
- at least three source families;
- allowed predicates and forbidden inferences;
- a measurable acceptance condition;
- `graph_effect: none`;
- machine-proposed and publication-blocked status until human review.

Each frontier candidate must provide a stable `F-XX` identifier, a research question, and a promotion condition. Frontier rows cannot be used as claims, profiles, edges, scores, chains, or publication sentences.

## Validation

Run:

```bash
node tools/validate-synthetic-population-program.mjs
node test/synthetic-population-program.test.js
```

The dedicated GitHub Actions workflow runs both commands when the manifest, validator, test, documentation, or workflow changes.

## Promotion

Promotion follows the repository constitution and ordinary candidate-surface or case-ledger process. A human reviewer must establish the bounded object, actors or organizations, roles, dates, receipts, evidence class, temporal handling, density treatment, privacy boundary, selection declaration, allowed language, forbidden inference, and review state. The staged program does not bypass any existing gate.
