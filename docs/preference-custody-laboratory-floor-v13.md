The v12 controls remain frozen and executable. PC-15 is admitted through a separate fixture, proof matrix, custody chain, refusal ledger, promotion boundary, and frontier transition.

The floor remains synthetic. It creates no graph effect, thesis evidence, named-vendor failure, contractual-breach finding, security verdict, liability conclusion, manipulation claim, legitimacy verdict, public-authorization finding, or inference of intent.

## Complete federated recovery

The positive world requires every required organization to receive and acknowledge the revocation. Every technical holder must enforce it, move to the current trust epoch, purge or replace the compromised artifact, replay the clean inputs, pass validation, and either restart cleanly or preserve an explicit safe terminal state. The public interface must deliver complete notification and remedy coverage. Residual exposure must be zero.

```text
revocation issue
→ delivery
→ acknowledgement
→ enforcement
→ trust update
→ cache purge
→ replay
→ validation
→ restart or abstention
→ notification and remedy
→ residual-exposure closure
```

Only one world completes that joined transaction.

## Propagation failures

The remaining seven worlds each break a different link while the public status remains `recovered`.

### Stale customer cache

The customer receives the revocation but does not acknowledge or enforce it. Its cache and implementation retain the compromised artifact for 200 people.

### Continued cloud route

The customer recovers locally. The cloud route never receives the revocation and continues serving the compromised artifact to 150 people.

### Trust-list lag

Operational holders recover, but the auditor remains on the prior trust epoch for six hours and issues stale validation affecting 100 cases.

### Contractual authority gap

The customer has technical capability to stop and replay but lacks contractual authority. Acknowledgement does not become enforcement, and 200 people remain exposed.

### Notification and remedy gap

Every technical holder recovers. The affected-public interface receives no complete notification, challenge route, or remedy. Technical completion does not close the rights transaction.

### Source-restricted abstention

The downstream holder receives and enforces the revocation but cannot access the clean inputs. It blocks service rather than claiming a successful replay.

### Secondary-tenant exposure

The primary customer recovers and the public label is issued. The secondary tenant remains outside the revocation scope with a stale cache and 100 residual exposures.
