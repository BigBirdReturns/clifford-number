# Notice comprehension, accessibility, exit, and assent-effectiveness custody

PC-21 separates delivered notice and a recorded 95-bound/5-exited disposition from validated comprehension, scenario transfer, semantic translation, usable accessibility, intended exit, effective exit, self-authorship, explicit assent, payment sequencing, correction, and authority.

The control is synthetic. It creates no graph effect, thesis evidence, comprehension audit, consent finding, coercion finding, choice-validity opinion, public-authorization result, manipulation claim, legitimacy verdict, or inference of intent.

## Frozen choice ledger

```text
technical correction          complete
final reference proposal      A1
operative release             RELEASE-INCIDENT-V1@1
public choice status          informed_choice_complete
affected people               100
notice delivered              100
formal choices recorded       100
recorded bound                 95
recorded exited                 5
people paid                   100
amount paid                 1,800 units
```

Every world therefore produces the same recorded choice and payment surface. That surface does not identify whether people understood the consequence, intended their recorded state, authored the choice, or gave assent before payment and binding treatment.

## Eight choice systems behind one disposition

### Verified comprehension and self-authored choice

The positive world preserves a validated consequence-and-choice construct, complete recognition, consequence explanation, scenario transfer, delayed retention, semantically and procedurally equivalent translation, complete accessible formats and assisted comprehension, a neutral default, a meaningful 60-day route, five intended and completed exits, ninety-five explicit assents, independent validation, explanation, objection, appeal, re-administration, and receipted correction.

```text
consequence understanding       100
scenario transfer               100
delayed retention               100
intended exits                    5
completed exits                   5
self-authored choices           100
explicit assents                 95
inferred assents                  0
complete path                  true
```

### Message-open receipt treated as comprehension

All 100 messages are opened and acknowledged. Only twenty people receive any comprehension assessment, fifteen transfer the consequence to a scenario, and ten retain it after delay. The system nevertheless labels the entire population informed.

```text
messages opened                 100
population assessed              20
consequence understanding        20
scenario transfer                15
delayed retention                10
```

Opening and acknowledgement are attention receipts, not comprehension measures.

### Recognition quiz without scenario transfer

Ninety-five people pass a term-recognition quiz. Only forty explain the operative consequence, twenty-five apply it to a scenario, and twenty retain the understanding after delay.

```text
recognition                       95
consequence understanding         40
scenario transfer                 25
delayed retention                 20
```

Recognition is distinct from consequence understanding and transfer.

### Literal translation without semantic or procedural equivalence

Twenty people receive translated material. Only eight receive semantically equivalent terms and five receive procedurally equivalent exit instructions. The recorded choice surface remains 95/5.

```text
translated population             20
semantic equivalence               8
procedural equivalence             5
consequence understanding         70
scenario transfer                 60
```

Translation availability does not establish usable equivalence.

### Accessibility and assisted-comprehension gaps

The process claims formal coverage while only eighty people receive covered formats and seventy obtain usable access. Assisted comprehension reaches ten people even though the access groups requiring assistance total twenty.

```text
format coverage                    80
usable access                      70
assisted comprehension             10
consequence understanding          70
scenario transfer                  65
```

A format label or policy statement is not an access receipt.

### Comprehended choice with suppressed intended exit

All 100 people understand and transfer the consequence. Twenty intend to exit. A seven-day, high-urgency, notarized-mail route produces only five completions, ten failed attempts, and five abandonments. The remaining fifteen people are treated as bound through inferred assent.

```text
consequence understanding         100
intended exits                     20
completed exits                     5
failed exits                       10
abandoned exits                     5
suppressed exit intentions         15
explicit assents                   80
inferred assents                   15
```

Recorded exit rate and intended exit rate are different variables.

### Representative or operator substitution

All 100 people understand the consequence. A representative or call-center operator nevertheless records thirty choices without later ratification. The final surface remains 95 bound and five exited.

```text
self-authored choices              70
representative substitutions       30
representative ratifications        0
explicit assents                   65
inferred assents                   30
```

Operator completion is not affected-person assent.

### Payment before choice and retention as assent

Payment reaches all 100 people before the choice transaction. Only fifty explain the consequence and forty transfer it to a scenario. Five people explicitly author an exit. The other ninety-five are treated as assenting because they retain payment.

```text
payment before choice             100
consequence understanding          50
scenario transfer                  40
self-authored choices               5
explicit assents                    0
inferred assents                   95
```

Payment retention is not prior informed agreement.

## Comprehension custody

A real comprehension claim requires more than a read receipt or topline score:

```text
construct identity and intended interpretation
instrument identity, version, item bank, administration, and scoring
predeclared threshold and uncertainty
recognition, recall, consequence explanation, scenario transfer, and delayed retention
subgroup validity, translation equivalence, accessibility, and assistance
confidence calibration and error analysis
```

## Agency custody

A recorded choice must preserve:

```text
latent intention
attempt
success
failure
abandonment
reason
friction and cost
assistance
self-authorship
representative action and mandate
ratification
explicit and inferred assent
payment sequencing
correction and re-administration
```

The same 95/5 disposition can conceal materially different intention and authorship states.

## Authority boundary

Even the positive world remains a synthetic control. Validated comprehension, self-authored choice, successful exit, and explicit assent do not create binding public authority over objectives or affected populations.

```text
validated choice
≠ public authorization

comprehension or exit failure
≠ coercion, manipulation, breach, misconduct, or intent
```

## Custody chain

Each world emits a SHA-256 hash-linked chain:

```text
affected population, release, disposition, payment, and public status
→ notice delivery, open, acknowledgement, and operative-release cross-reference
→ comprehension construct, instrument, transfer, retention, translation, and accessibility
→ default, deadline, urgency, friction, and assistance
→ intention, attempt, exit, authorship, assent, representation, and payment sequence
→ validation, audit, explanation, objection, appeal, re-administration, correction, and authority
→ recorded-surface and suppressed-intention consequence
→ choice-effectiveness classification
→ interpretation seal
```

## Publication boundary

The admissible laboratory statement is that one operative narrow release, one public `informed_choice_complete` status, one 100-person affected population, full notice delivery, one recorded 95-bound/5-exited disposition, and full payment can coexist with verified comprehension and agency, message-open substitution, recognition without transfer, translation failure, accessibility gaps, suppressed intended exit, representative substitution, or payment-before-assent. A real informed-choice claim requires complete population, comprehension construct and validity, scenario transfer, translation, accessibility, assistance, intention, choice architecture, exit, assent, payment sequencing, representation, validation, correction, durability, and authority custody.
