# Clifford Number core thesis

Clifford Number’s durable research thesis is:

> Post-Cold War governments externalized strategic capacity, specialist knowledge, risk, and implementation into private and hybrid institutions. Strategic ambiguity became a conversion technology through which actors could move among adviser, investor, vendor, public-mission partner, infrastructure provider, and rights holder while authorship, accountability, risk, and reward remained distributed. The project tests when actors deliberately shaped public option sets and operating layers, when the resulting arrangements produced capability or dependency, who retained residual rights and value, and whether the public could inspect, contest, substitute, terminate, or reverse the system.

In one line:

> The project reconstructs how interests become infrastructure, how infrastructure becomes necessity, and how necessity becomes power.

This is a falsifiable working thesis, not a finding about every actor, estate, program, or deployment.
