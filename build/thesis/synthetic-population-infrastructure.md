# Synthetic populations, state transfer, and modelled publics

**Working status:** collecting_evidence

**Research question:** Do independently receipted public records show synthetic-population systems becoming an infrastructure for decision-making across state and market through recurring pathways of personnel and institutional transfer, scientific and official legitimacy, deployment, technical substrate, and capital—and under what conditions do modelled publics supplement or replace observed people?

**Provisional thesis:** The repository tests whether synthetic-population and synthetic-audience systems are becoming an infrastructural layer across public and private decision processes. This is a provisional research proposition, not a finding: every pathway must be independently established, ordinary explanations and contradictions must remain visible, and cross-case recurrence cannot substitute for a shared bounded surface or source-explicit causal record.

> This dossier is a working research assembly, not a verdict. A case contract is not evidence; recurrence is not coordination; capability is not deployment; investment is not operational control; role overlap is not scientific compromise; supplier listing is not award; and source failure is not absence. Every factual statement must remain traceable to its evidence packet, receipt, date, selection rule, and review status.
