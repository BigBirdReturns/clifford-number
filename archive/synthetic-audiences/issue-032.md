## Case object

**Public-interest question:** How did experimental data-science and AI units inside No. 10 and the Cabinet Office become, merge into, or transfer functions to later central-government AI and digital institutions?

**Selection unit:** one formally named unit, programme, mandate, budget line, senior appointment, published strategy, machinery-of-government transfer, or official service.

**Selection universe:** all central UK government units and programmes recoverable from official records whose declared remit included data science, AI incubation, responsible AI, digital centre-of-government work, or cross-government AI implementation from 2019 onward.

The target is an institutional or capability pattern, not the private character of any person. The selection rule must admit an analogous actor or institution under the same records regardless of party, ideology, nationality, affiliation, investor identity, or anticipated news value. Missing records, failed queries, unresolved identities, ordinary career movement, and evidence against the working hypothesis remain first-class output.

## Task packet

1. Freeze the unit chronology, names, parent departments, legal or administrative status, dates, budgets, locations, and declared mandates.
2. Map senior appointments, staff transfers where officially disclosed, programme inheritances, shared services, procurement vehicles, and published outputs.
3. Distinguish renaming, merger, dissolution, expansion, parallel operation, and narrative continuity.
4. Resolve vendor, contractor, framework, grant, and partnership records attached to each bounded programme rather than treating departments as generic hops.
5. Compare the UK succession pattern with at least one matched central-government AI unit in another jurisdiction.
6. Produce a capability-continuity map that marks every inferred inheritance as a hypothesis until a receipt states the transfer.

## Source families

- GOV.UK organisational pages and announcements
- annual reports and spending data
- departmental organograms and transparency releases
- Contracts Finder and Find a Tender
- parliamentary committee and NAO material

## Predicate and inference boundary

**Allowed predicates:** `unit_created`, `unit_renamed`, `unit_closed`, `function_transferred`, `programme_inherited`, `appointed_to_lead`, `contract_awarded_for`.

**Forbidden inferences:** similar mandate proves direct inheritance; staff overlap proves coordination; departmental continuity proves policy agreement; procurement proves capture; absence of transfer notice proves no continuity.

Co-presence is never coordination. A broad department, company, programme family, or professional network is not a Clifford hop. Any eventual surface proposal must name the bounded object, participants, roles, dates, receipts, evidence class, temporal overlap, density treatment, and copy-ready caveat.

## Acceptance

A time-indexed institutional genealogy with separately evidenced mandate, personnel, programme, budget, and supplier continuity, plus a refusal ledger for apparent but unproved successions.

Promotion requires a human-reviewed candidate packet and the repository's selection, consumption, privacy, temporal, density, receipt, and release gates. Until then, all observations remain research intake with `graph_effect: none`.

## First bounded evidence receipt: chronology plus a refused succession

**Parent:** #30  
**Captured:** 2026-07-19  
**Status:** research intake only; `graph_effect: none`

### Baseline action

Exercised the smallest official chronology across unit creation and machinery-of-government records:

1. 10DS government recruitment record (formed mid-2020): https://findajob.dwp.gov.uk/details/16128142
2. i.AI announcement, delivered 20 Nov 2023: https://www.gov.uk/government/speeches/seizing-the-opportunities-of-ai-in-government
3. DSIT transfer announcement, 8 Jul 2024: https://www.gov.uk/government/news/dsit-bolstered-to-better-serve-the-british-public-through-science-and-technology
4. 2025 accounting-officer system statement: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/dsit-accounting-officer-system-statement-2025/dsit-accounting-officer-system-statement-2025-html
5. New-GDS description: https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/central-digital-and-data-office/about

### Observed artifact

| Date | Unit/action | What the official receipt establishes |
|---|---|---|
| mid-2020 | 10DS formed | No. 10 data-science team formed to improve how key decisions use data, analysis and evidence. |
| 20 Nov 2023 | i.AI announced | A new Incubator for AI team was announced with an initial target of 30 technical, programme, product and engagement staff. |
| 8 Jul 2024 | MoG transfer announced | GDS, CDDO and i.AI expertise would move from Cabinet Office into DSIT. |
| Jul 2024 / FY 2024-25 | Transfer implemented | DSIT's accounting statement records CDDO, GDS and i.AI transferring to DSIT and the integrated organisation operating under the GDS banner. |
| Jan 2025 onward | New GDS | The official CDDO page says CDDO, Geospatial Commission, GDS and i.AI merged into the new GDS; CDDO existed Apr 2021-Jan 2025. |

### Refused tempting link

No opened official receipt states that **10DS became i.AI**, transferred its staff or budget to i.AI, or merged into the new GDS. A 2025 government job advert still described 10DS as a No. 10 team, while the AI Playbook describes CDDO, 10DS and i.AI collaborating on red-teaming. Collaboration and similar remit are not inheritance.

Therefore the currently evidenced predicates are `unit_created` for 10DS/i.AI and `function_transferred`/merger for GDS-CDDO-i.AI into DSIT/new GDS. The proposed 10DS → i.AI edge remains a hypothesis.

### Comparator candidate, not yet a matched result

The US GSA Centers of Excellence record provides a plausible same-battery comparator: the programme was established in Oct 2017 and included an AI centre delivering cross-agency adoption support. Source: https://coe.gsa.gov/coe/centers-of-excellence.html. Its statutory, budget and personnel shape still needs normalisation before comparison.

### Nearest remaining boundary

Freeze budgets, administrative status, named leadership, personnel transfers, programmes and supplier records for each unit. Resolve the discrepancy in official descriptions of the 2025 merger perimeter (some name three predecessor bodies; another also names the Geospatial Commission) rather than forcing a single narrative.

No repository, canonical, or graph state changed.
