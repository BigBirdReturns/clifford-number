## Case object

**Public-interest question:** Which publishers and media companies deploy synthetic audiences or simulated readers, what data and lawful roles support them, which editorial, product, marketing, commercial, and advertiser decisions use them, and whether human research continues alongside the models?

**Selection unit:** one publisher, named product, vendor deployment, dataset class, privacy statement, internal use, advertiser offering, validation claim, procurement record, or documented decision.

**Selection universe:** all publisher and media deployments recovered from the frozen synthetic-population vendor cohort, reciprocal publisher announcements, industry reporting, and procurement sources, including displayed logos held as unresolved until confirmed.

## Task packet

1. Freeze the publisher/customer denominator and classify every logo, announcement, case study, reciprocal confirmation, pilot, expired deployment, and unresolved claim.
2. Build one deployment dossier per publisher with contracting entities, dates, product name, vendor, internal users, advertiser access, and geographical scope.
3. Map each dataset class, asserted controller/processor role, consent or lawful basis, retention, anonymisation, and whether individual- or segment-level outputs are possible.
4. Separate editorial, product, marketing, subscription, commercial, and advertiser use cases and identify any documented decisions.
5. Compare synthetic output with continuing surveys, panels, interviews, experiments, and actual behaviour; record supplement versus replacement.
6. Track complaints, corrections, regulator attention, policy changes, withdrawal, or clean operation as equal outcomes.

## Source families

- vendor and publisher announcements
- publisher privacy and data-policy notices
- trade press and advertiser materials
- ICO and other regulator records
- procurement and company filings

## Predicate and inference boundary

**Allowed predicates:** `deployed_at`, `built_on_dataset`, `used_by_team`, `made_available_to_advertisers`, `human_research_continued`, `deployment_ended`, `regulator_reviewed`.

**Forbidden inferences:** logo proves customer; segment is an individual replica; advertiser access proves manipulation; synthetic research replaced all consultation; publisher endorsement validates vendor metrics.

Every dossier must distinguish vendor claim, customer claim, privacy-policy statement, independent reporting, and regulator finding sentence by sentence.

## Acceptance

A source-complete publisher deployment table and claim-granular dossiers that distinguish vendor, customer, policy, regulator, and independent evidence.

Promotion requires a human-reviewed packet and all repository gates. Until then, every observation remains `graph_effect: none`.

### New observed source family

#### 17 June 2025 — Dentsu + Evidenza strategic deployment

Dentsu’s official announcement says it partnered with **Evidenza** to integrate synthetic audiences into next-generation media planning:

https://www.dentsu.com/sg/en/our-news/dentsu-partners-with-evidenza-to-integrate-synthetic-audiences-into-next-gen-media-planning

Customer/media-company controlled facts:

- deployment surface: dentsu media strategy and planning teams;
- vendor: Evidenza;
- integration: Evidenza’s synthetic-research platform plus **dentsu.Audiences**, including dentsu’s proprietary **CCS panel dataset**;
- stated uses: audience insight, segment discovery, media planning, and movement from insight toward activation;
- reported early comparison: **0.87 correlation with traditional research**;
- reciprocal signal: the same Dentsu release carries a named statement from Evidenza co-founder Peter Weinberg describing the combined Dentsu data/media scale and Evidenza technology.

This is stronger than a vendor logo because the media company itself names the vendor and workflow. The 0.87 figure remains a deployment-party claim: the release does not expose sample, question set, holdout design, denominator, confidence interval, or independent validator.

#### 29 January 2026 — Dentsu launches Generative Audiences in the UK

Dentsu later announced a production capability called **Generative Audiences**:

https://www.dentsu.com/news-releases/dentsu-launches-generative-audiences-ai-powered-growth-intelligence-that-thinks-like-consumers

The official launch identifies the UK as the first market, says the system creates statistical simulations and synthetic personas from multiple data sources, connects insight to media activation, and is powered by **dentsu.AI** with integration into **dentsu.Connect**.

### Critical lineage boundary

The 2026 launch does **not** name Evidenza. Therefore:

- the 2025 Dentsu/Evidenza integration is a reciprocally signalled media deployment;
- the 2026 Dentsu Generative Audiences launch is a confirmed Dentsu deployment;
- continuity from Evidenza into the 2026 production stack is **unresolved** and must not be inferred.

This preserved break is material: a customer can move from an externally named vendor partnership to an internally branded capability without public documentation of whether the vendor remains, was replaced, or became one component.

### Corpus effect

The recovered corpus now contains:

1. **News UK / Times Media — Times ExplorAItion / Electric Twin:** reciprocal publisher and vendor confirmation;
2. **Dentsu — 2025 Evidenza integration:** customer-controlled media-company confirmation with a named vendor and dataset family;
3. **Dentsu — 2026 Generative Audiences:** customer-controlled launch, vendor lineage unresolved.

Dentsu is a media/agency deployment, not a publisher. It expands the allowed media-company corpus but must not be counted as a second publisher.

### Unresolved fields

No source recovered here specifies controller/processor allocation, lawful basis for CCS-derived synthesis, retention, whether 2025 Evidenza remains in the 2026 stack, client-level deployments, or the complete geographic rollout. “Privacy-conscious” and commitment to privacy standards are assertions, not a completed privacy-role ledger.

## Fourth bounded findings receipt: observed currency, activation, and contestability remain three separate layers

Parent: #30  
Builds on: https://github.com/BigBirdReturns/clifford-number/issues/39#issuecomment-5016629514  
Captured: 2026-07-19  
State: `graph_effect: none`; public product/policy artifacts, not observed runtime evidence.

### Baseline action

Reran the two confirmed media paths against the smallest primary-source stack that could join (or fail to join) data currency, synthetic planning, activation, and correction:

- News UK Times ExplorAItion launch: https://www.news.co.uk/latest-news/news-uk-launches-times-exploraition-synthetic-audience-insight-tool/
- Electric Twin implementation account: https://www.electrictwin.com/blog/the-times-announces-its-new-synthetic-audience-tool-built-on-electric-twin
- News UK privacy policy, effective 19 May 2026: https://newsprivacy.co.uk/
- Times Media 2026 rate card: https://www.news.co.uk/app/uploads/2026/04/EXTERNAL-TIMES-MEDIA-RATECARD-2026-1.pdf
- PAMCo H1 2026 release notice: https://pamco.co.uk/pamco-h1-2026/
- Dentsu/Evidenza partnership and Dentsu production launch:
  - https://www.dentsu.com/sg/en/our-news/dentsu-partners-with-evidenza-to-integrate-synthetic-audiences-into-next-gen-media-planning
  - https://www.dentsu.com/news-releases/dentsu-launches-generative-audiences-ai-powered-growth-intelligence-that-thinks-like-consumers
  - https://www.dentsu.com/blog/why-leading-cmos-are-rewriting-their-growth-strategy-around-data-connectivity

### 1. Times input/currency lineage is more precise, but the exact vintage is still unresolved

Electric Twin names the Times input classes as stripped-back subscriber behaviour, reader panels, engagement data, and PAMCo industry data, fed into its modelling platform to produce synthetic versions of key Times segments. It says no personal data is used in that feed.

PAMCo's own current release notice establishes that **PAMCo is an observed-media Joint Industry Currency, not a synthetic population**. Its H1 2026 release:

- was published 1 April 2026;
- covers December 2023–December 2025 for print plus November 2025 Ipsos iris digital data;
- blends 50% of the new sample method with 50% of the prior method;
- expressly says trending/comparison with prior releases is not possible during the methodology transition, with a fully new-method release not expected until H1 2027.

Times ExplorAItion launched later that month. Neither News UK nor Electric Twin identifies the exact PAMCo release, tables, licence, variables, weights, transformations, or freeze date used to seed the audience model. The 2026 Times rate card itself cites **PAMCo H2 2025** for reach, while the then-current PAMCo release was H1 2026. That does not prove which vintage ExplorAItion used; it proves only that “PAMCo data” is a versioned currency dependency that cannot safely be treated as timeless.

This produces a measurable model-drift question: a synthetic audience seeded from a mixed-method currency needs a declared input vintage and rerun policy before outputs can be compared across PAMCo releases.

### 2. Planning-to-activation is adjacent, not publicly joined

The customer launch labels ExplorAItion a **decision-support** and pre-market testing tool. It says the synthetic output does not replace real-world research or performance data. Electric Twin describes it as a value-add within branded-content deals.

The Times rate card separately documents a live Nucleus activation stack:

- Nucleus first-party/contextual targeting for Times Media campaigns;
- Nucleus Fusion matching brand first-party data through InfoSum, LiveRamp, and Decentriq;
- direct or programmatic transaction;
- Nucleus Plan optimisation from 3,000+ prior campaigns;
- observed campaign KPIs and separate measurement vendors.

The rate card contains no “ExplorAItion,” “Electric Twin,” or “synthetic” entry. Therefore the public record supports **synthetic pre-test → human campaign planning** and separately supports **Nucleus targeting/activation/measurement**. It does **not** establish an automatic or deterministic ExplorAItion-output → target segment → media-buy handoff.

Dentsu remains the contrasting positive anchor: its own materials explicitly say Generative Audiences insights flow into audience definition and activation. But the public Dentsu sources still do not expose a client deployment, the mapping rule from a synthetic persona to a real addressable audience, the 0.87 validation corpus, or a correction/override route.

### 3. Reader rights exist, but are not product-specific synthetic-audience contestability

News UK's policy names **Times Media Limited** as controller for The Times and Sunday Times. It describes collection of device/engagement data, declared poll data, audience segmentation, commercial-partner data matching, market research/analytics, Ipsos iris measurement, and submission of audience figures to PAMCo.

It also exposes concrete individual controls:

- correction, deletion, restriction, access, portability, and objection rights;
- withdrawal of consent where consent is the basis;
- opt-out from commercial-partner data matching by contacting News UK;
- cookie controls for specified advertising partners;
- escalation of unresolved UK concerns to the ICO.

Those are real privacy and data-matching controls. However, the policy contains no occurrence of **Electric Twin**, **ExplorAItion**, or **synthetic**. It does not say whether an opt-out or correction propagates into a frozen model-training/seed dataset, triggers regeneration of a synthetic segment, changes an aggregate inference, or permits a group represented by the model to challenge its characterization.

Likewise, News UK's launch gives strategists visibility into inputs, assumptions, and limitations and invites them to challenge assumptions. That is **buyer/user-side interpretive control**, not an affected-reader appeal mechanism.

### Declared search boundary

Within the named public product launch, vendor implementation account, current News UK privacy notice, current Times commercial rate card, PAMCo release notice, and the three named Dentsu surfaces, no product-specific DPIA, model card, frozen PAMCo version, synthetic/observed output label shown to downstream buyers, customer validation protocol, correction log, override record, complaint route, or affected-audience appeal case was recovered.

This is a bounded public-source failure, **not evidence that those artifacts or controls do not exist privately**.

### Frontier verdict

- **F-03 contestability: not promotion-ready.** A named deployment, reader-data rights, buyer-side assumption challenge, and an ordinary activation stack now exist. The missing decisive artifact is still a product-linked correction/appeal/override route plus an affected-group or matched control case.
- **F-09 advertising measurement: not promotion-ready.** The measurement/simulation distinction and a versioned PAMCo transition are now explicit, but the exact PAMCo release/licence used by ExplorAItion, a bounded JIC/currency universe, and symmetric synthetic-versus-observed controls remain unresolved.

Acceptance for #39 remains incomplete. No repository, PR, canonical, graph, score, or chain state changed.
