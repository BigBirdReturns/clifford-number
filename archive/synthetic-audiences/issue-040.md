## Case object

**Public-interest question:** Where do synthetic populations, digital twins, virtual personas, agent panels, or simulated stakeholders supplement or replace surveys, interviews, focus groups, citizen consultation, user research, deliberation, or other direct human input?

**Selection unit:** one documented workflow, decision, consultation, procurement, policy, product feature, evaluation, disclosure, or affected stakeholder process.

**Selection universe:** all named deployments and use cases in the frozen vendor cohort plus source-complete public procurement and case-study records, classified before evaluating whether replacement occurred.

## Task packet

1. Define operational tests for supplementation, screening, acceleration, partial substitution, full replacement, and merely hypothetical capability.
2. Enumerate deployments and retain every case with enough evidence to classify, including explicit non-replacement statements.
3. Identify whose input was simulated, who commissioned the work, what decision was at stake, and who retained decision authority.
4. Measure disclosure to affected stakeholders and whether any appeal, correction, participation, or override route existed.
5. Compare cost, speed, volume, accuracy, representativeness, and failure reports against the human-input method displaced or supplemented.
6. Construct matched controls where organisations used synthetic and human methods on the same problem.

## Source families

- vendor case studies and product documentation
- customer announcements and research protocols
- public consultations and procurement records
- professional research standards
- regulator and audit material

## Predicate and inference boundary

**Allowed predicates:** `supplemented_human_research`, `replaced_human_panel`, `used_for_preliminary_screen`, `decision_informed_by`, `disclosure_provided`, `human_override_available`.

**Forbidden inferences:** marketing claim proves replacement; speed proves democratic value; simulation represents every affected person; use in one workflow proves organisation-wide substitution; absence of disclosure proves concealment.

The decisive evidence is the documented workflow, not the vendor's general capability description. Replacement must be demonstrated against a named prior or counterfactual human-input process.

## Acceptance

A workflow-level substitution ledger with explicit classification thresholds, affected populations, decision stakes, disclosure, appeal routes, and matched controls.

Promotion requires human review and all repository gates. Until then, every observation remains `graph_effect: none`.
