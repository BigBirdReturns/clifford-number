## Program contract

- `program_id`: `synthetic-population-infrastructure`
- `selection_lane`: `ai-policy-public-private-topology`
- `program_status`: `active_research_fanout`
- `graph_effect`: `none`
- `verification_status`: `machine_proposed_unverified`
- `publication_status`: `blocked_pending_receipts_human_review_and_selection_gate`

This program coordinates a set of independently bounded cases exposed by the Electric Twin capability-lineage investigation. The object is not one company, one political tendency, or a presumed conspiracy. The object is the documented infrastructure by which population-response models, synthetic audiences, behavioural simulation, public-service expertise, scientific authority, first-party data, capital, procurement, and governance mechanisms move across campaign, state, media, research, and commercial venues.

A seam enters this program only when it has its own neutral selection unit, source family, denominator or explicitly open universe, inclusion and exclusion rules, privacy boundary, symmetry rule, failure conditions, and acceptance test. Each child issue must remain coherent if Electric Twin is removed from the corpus. No child issue may create a Clifford hop, alter a score, or produce publication-ready narration merely because a person, company, institution, investor, adviser, or customer appears in another seam.

## Program-wide predicates

Allowed classes include dated public roles, appointments, filings, company control, board service, funding events, procurement records, published validation claims, disclosed metric definitions, named deployments, data-policy statements, professional standards, regulator findings, and documented capability statements.

Forbidden inferences include motive, secret planning, misuse of confidential information, ideological transfer, coordination from co-presence, influence from investment alone, scientific compromise from role overlap, procurement from supplier listing alone, wrongdoing from revolving-door movement, individual prediction from population-level distribution matching, or absence from an incomplete source search.

## Selection rule

For every seam, apply the same predicates and evidentiary burdens to the complete or declared comparator universe regardless of party, ideology, nationality, investor identity, founder biography, customer identity, or expected news value. A target may route discovery, but it cannot define membership in the research universe. Nulls, failures, source gaps, rejected matches, and ordinary explanations remain first-class output.

### Frontier queue

A final child issue will preserve second-order seams that are visible but not yet sufficiently bounded for a full case. Promotion from that queue requires a neutral denominator and at least one addressable positive anchor.

## Deliverables

1. One child issue per seam with a stable batch identifier, selection rule, bounded tasks, source families, permitted predicates, forbidden inferences, acceptance criteria, and publication gate.
2. A frozen comparator or denominator declaration for every source-complete or matched-comparator case.
3. Receipt-level observations and explicit source attrition, with `graph_effect: none` until canonical promotion.
4. Cross-seam recurrence analysis only after each seam has been independently compiled. Recurrence is structural context, never a substitute for a shared bounded surface.
5. A program delta that distinguishes newly verified public facts, unresolved questions, rejected tempting connections, and coverage changes.

## Promotion gate

Nothing in this program enters canonical ledgers until a human-reviewed candidate packet identifies the bounded surface or non-hop case object, actors or organizations, roles, dates, receipts, evidence class, temporal handling, density treatment, allowed language, forbidden inference, selection declaration, and review status. The repository constitution controls whenever a research issue, comment, generated artifact, or attractive narrative suggests a broader claim.
