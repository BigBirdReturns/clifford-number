This batch classifies Electric Twin as a **synthetic-population capability-lineage case**. The public-interest object is the documented movement of personnel, methods, authority claims, capital, customer data, validation practices, and governance wrappers across campaign, central-government, crisis-response, company, media, and potential public-sector venues. The actors presently in scope are Ben Warner, Alex Cooper, Michael Muthukrishna, Simon Case, Ben Blume, the other named ethics advisers, and any additional public actors established by addressable records. The institutions presently in scope are Electric Twin Ltd, No. 10 and the Cabinet Office only through bounded roles or records, the Department of Health and Social Care only through bounded programme records, the UK Covid-19 Inquiry, News UK and The Times only through a named deployment, LSE only through a named role or study, Atomico only through a funding or governance record, the Centre for Human Progress only through its filed board, and any public customer only through a contract, procurement notice, supplier listing, or official disclosure.

The mechanism under examination is capability lineage rather than a presumed coordinated plan. Each task must distinguish contemporaneous public-service records from later company origin stories, vendor performance claims from independent replication, corporate filings from press descriptions, displayed customer logos from verified deployments, ethics-board membership from enforceable oversight, and source silence from evidence of absence. A finding may establish a dated role, filing, statement, deployment, metric, policy, or contract. It may not infer motive, secret planning, misuse of confidential information, influence, collusion, illegality, corruption, wrongdoing, or individual conduct from institutional proximity.

The neutral selection rule is: apply the same predicates and evidentiary burdens to any synthetic-audience or synthetic-population vendor recovered from the frozen category or comparator roster, regardless of founder politics, nationality, investor identity, customer identity, or anticipated news value. The first comparator task therefore freezes the relevant vendor universe before cross-company conclusions are drawn.

## Promotion and publication gate

Every task remains `graph_effect: none` until a human-reviewed candidate packet names a bounded surface, participants, roles, dates, receipt IDs, evidence class, temporal overlap, density treatment, allowed language, and forbidden inference. Company posts and investor posts are primary evidence of what those organizations publicly claimed, not independent proof of the claim's accuracy. Official records establish only the legal, appointment, inquiry, procurement, or regulatory fact within their stated scope. A failed or unavailable search becomes a coverage result and cannot be narrated as absence.

The evidence ledger for the present batch is: official records and filings are the highest available tier for roles, corporate acts, inquiry artifacts, procurement, and post-employment restrictions; company, university, customer, investor, and hiring pages are primary public statements about their own claims and roles; journalism is reported corroboration; derived timelines and comparisons remain analysis. The target is the governance and evidentiary status of a population-simulation capability, not the private character of any person. The upside is a reproducible account of how the capability moved and acquired authority. The downside is that target-first abundance can make ordinary market practices look exceptional. The principal failure mode is converting role continuity, investment, validation ambiguity, or customer use into an insinuation of secret coordination or wrongdoing.
