## Case object

**Public-interest question:** How did analysts define synthetic population and behavioural simulation as a procurement category, which vendors were selected or excluded, what dimensions and tiers were used, and how did vendors convert paywalled analyst material into public legitimacy?

**Selection unit:** one analyst report, vendor reviewed, tier assignment, inclusion criterion, quoted analyst statement, licensed excerpt, vendor reproduction, customer procurement reference, or subsequent category change.

**Selection universe:** the complete 60-company reviewed universe and 33-company watched universe reported for Gartner document `7718657`, plus any later or predecessor category reports recovered under the same document family.

The report denominator must be frozen before company comparisons are made. Vendor reproductions establish what vendors publicly claimed, not the complete report context or an analyst endorsement beyond the recoverable source.

## Task packet

1. Recover and freeze the complete reviewed and watched vendor denominator, preserving unresolved names and paywall boundaries.
2. Extract report authorship, date, category definition, selection dimensions, tier logic, disclaimers, methodology, and declared intended use.
3. Archive every vendor claim quoting or paraphrasing the report and classify whether the tier, description, or inclusion is reciprocally verifiable.
4. Compare analyst categories from Gartner and other firms using equivalent terminology and map vendor movement across reports.
5. Search customer procurement, investor, and media materials for analyst-category reliance.
6. Measure selection effects without treating inclusion as quality, endorsement, market leadership, or scientific validation.

## Source families

- Gartner document metadata and licensed access
- vendor posts and archived pages
- other analyst reports and category taxonomies
- customer procurement and investor materials
- web archives and bibliographic databases

## Predicate and inference boundary

**Allowed predicates:** `reviewed_by_analyst`, `selected_to_watch`, `assigned_tier`, `vendor_quoted_report`, `category_defined_as`, `report_relied_on_in_procurement`.

**Forbidden inferences:** watch-list inclusion proves superiority; tier proves investment merit; paywall prevents scrutiny by design; vendor quote equals full report context; category membership proves equivalent capability.

## Acceptance

A frozen category denominator, report-method ledger, vendor-claim crosswalk, and category-diff history with every unrecovered portion visibly bounded.

Promotion requires a human-reviewed packet and all repository gates. Until then, every observation remains `graph_effect: none`.
