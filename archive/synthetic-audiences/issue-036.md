## Case object

**Public-interest question:** What programmes, funding, research, public-policy activity, commercial relationships, and personnel surfaces connect the Centre for Human Progress to behavioural science, cultural evolution, AI, philanthropy, government, and private decision tools?

**Selection unit:** one filed corporate event, director appointment, programme, publication, grant, donor disclosure, partnership, event, consultancy, company role, or public-policy output.

**Selection universe:** the Centre for Human Progress legal entity, all filed directors and persons of control if any, every publicly named programme and partner, and matched behavioural-science institutes selected under the same organisational predicates.

The case begins with filed governance and public programmes, not with assumed intellectual or commercial continuity. Shared directorship, shared research interests, formal transactions, and programme participation remain separate evidence objects.

## Task packet

1. Freeze the legal and governance chronology from Companies House and any charity or nonprofit registers.
2. Build dated director and officer profiles using public-role-only evidence, including concurrent company, academic, government, and nonprofit roles.
3. Inventory programmes, publications, events, grants, donors where lawfully disclosed, consultancies, and commercial partnerships.
4. Resolve whether Electric Twin or any other vendor supplies, funds, commissions, licenses, or receives work from the Centre, without inferring from shared directors.
5. Compare governance and funding disclosure against matched behavioural-science or cultural-evolution institutes.
6. Separate intellectual affinity, shared directorship, formal transaction, and public programme participation into distinct predicates.

## Source families

- Companies House and charity registers
- Centre publications and website archives
- academic institution profiles
- grant databases and funder reports
- contract, procurement, and company records

## Predicate and inference boundary

**Allowed predicates:** `incorporated_on`, `director_of`, `published_by`, `funded_by`, `partnered_on_programme`, `commissioned_research_from`, `shared_director_surface`.

**Forbidden inferences:** shared director proves transaction; research theme proves product lineage; nonprofit status proves independence; funding proves control; intellectual agreement proves coordination.

A bounded board or programme may create a reviewable surface only when the participation, dates, and receipts support it. The Centre as a broad institution is not itself a hop.

## Acceptance

A governance-first institutional dossier with a complete public programme and funding inventory, matched comparator, and explicit separation of co-directorship from any receipted transaction.

Promotion requires a human-reviewed candidate packet and all repository gates. Until then, every observation remains `graph_effect: none`.
