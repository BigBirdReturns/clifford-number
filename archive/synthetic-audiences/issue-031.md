## Case object

**Public-interest question:** Which people holding bounded data, digital, research, testing, behavioural, communications, or crisis-response roles in No. 10 and central pandemic machinery later entered private AI, data, consulting, investment, advisory, or governance roles, under one role-neutral battery?

**Selection unit:** one person named in an official appointment, inquiry artifact, programme record, departmental organogram, honours record, business-appointment decision, company filing, or employer biography.

**Selection universe:** the complete recoverable cohort from declared No. 10, Cabinet Office, DHSC, Covid Inquiry, 10DS, testing-programme, and central crisis-response source families for the frozen 2019-2022 window, before post-government destinations are queried.

The target is an institutional or capability pattern, not the private character of any person. The selection rule must admit an analogous actor or institution under the same records regardless of party, ideology, nationality, affiliation, investor identity, or anticipated news value. Missing records, failed queries, unresolved identities, ordinary career movement, and evidence against the working hypothesis remain first-class output.

## Task packet

1. Freeze the source-defined cohort and preserve every included, excluded, ambiguous, and unnamed role.
2. Build dated public-service role timelines from official records and separate formal appointment, borrowed staff, contractor, adviser, witness, and reported participation.
3. Resolve later company, board, advisory, investment, academic, nonprofit, and renewed public roles through official filings and primary employer records.
4. Collect ACOBA and Civil Service Commission decisions, restrictions, declared contacts, lobbying prohibitions, bid restrictions, and expiry dates.
5. Compare destination rates and role types against a matched cohort of equivalent senior officials outside data/AI or outside the pandemic period.
6. Emit role-transition observations and rejected tempting links; do not create actor adjacency from shared employer or broad institution.

## Source families

- UK Covid-19 Inquiry document corpus
- GOV.UK appointments and biographies
- Cabinet Office, No. 10 and DHSC records
- ACOBA and Civil Service Commission decisions
- Companies House and employer leadership pages

## Predicate and inference boundary

**Allowed predicates:** `served_in_public_role`, `named_in_official_record`, `left_public_role`, `appointed_to_private_role`, `appointed_to_board`, `business_appointment_restricted`, `role_first_observed_on`.

**Forbidden inferences:** public service proves later influence; later employment proves prior planning; same institution proves contact or coordination; business-appointment approval proves ethical sufficiency; missing biography proves concealment.

Co-presence is never coordination. A broad department, company, programme family, or professional network is not a Clifford hop. Any eventual surface proposal must name the bounded object, participants, roles, dates, receipts, evidence class, temporal overlap, density treatment, and copy-ready caveat.

## Acceptance

A reproducible cohort manifest, dated transition ledger, destination taxonomy, coverage report, and matched-comparator analysis in which every role is source-addressable and every unknown remains explicit.

Promotion requires a human-reviewed candidate packet and the repository's selection, consumption, privacy, temporal, density, receipt, and release gates. Until then, all observations remain research intake with `graph_effect: none`.
