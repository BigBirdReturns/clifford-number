## What the programme found (per trail row 41)

Acquired 150 official-source files (8.5 MB) from the Federal Register, White House, DOJ, GAO, OPM, USAspending, the Supreme Court, MSPB and district courts: executive orders 14151 and 14173, the OPM DEIA-office RIF guidance, the OFCCP stand-down, the FAR overhaul, DOJ's Civil Rights Fraud Initiative with the IBM $17M and PayPal $30M settlements, the Title VI disparate-impact rule, the university agreements and investigations, Thakur v. Trump, AFGE v. Trump, Ames v. Ohio and the SBIC, OSC, Anduril DPA and Polar CHIPS awards; seven routes and 31 tasks, 28 holding official components and 3 protected. Zero completion tests pass because each demands a complete institutional row (the full agency implementation file, the full action register, realised payment recovery) that is not public. This is the raw material under the civil-rights-rollback finding (trail row [29]).
