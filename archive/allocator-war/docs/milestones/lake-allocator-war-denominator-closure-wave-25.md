Every Wave 24 unavailable obligation becomes one stable task in source order. Named institutional packets proceed directly to denominator, instrument, entity, outcome, or correction acquisition. The two public-record packets must identify or terminally fail to identify a bounded institutional gate before their four downstream tasks can proceed.

The queues preserve Wave 24 source references, bounded windows, coverage, negative search, unavailable rows, and refused substitutions. They do not execute the acquisition, adjudicate evidence, create an estate finding, estimate prevalence, join actors or institutions, change the graph, or clear publication.

The next transition is targeted closure execution against the thirty-six ready tasks. Gate-first queues remain blocked beyond their G0 task until the institutional decision surface is source-addressed or the bounded search terminates with no qualifying gate.
