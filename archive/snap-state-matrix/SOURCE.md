## What is and is not here

- Here: the research files these branches add to or change from main (promotion ledgers, product manifests, field matrices, adjudications, capture-custody records, schemas, milestone notes), plus, from the one complete Oregon-Wisconsin carrier, the adjudication product decoded from its base64 shards (`branches/agent/ssc-rd04-or-wi-minimum-frontier-materializer-v2/decoded/`).

- Not here: tools, tests and workflows of these branches (`tools/`, `test/`, `.github/`), by rule.

The complete 8-shard carrier on `...-materializer-v2` decodes cleanly and is unpacked.
