## Governing finding

Every current `observed_artifacts` entry is a K0 source-route identifier. Those records provide custody and routing, but they are not the underlying work product, decision object, correction sequence, failure ledger, or handoff receipt required to evaluate an operator.

The programme therefore converts the entire denominator into exact acquisition work rather than manufacturing thirty blind packets from summaries.

## Permanent products

- artifact-readiness programme and record schema;
- thirty-record readiness registry;
- five class-balanced acquisition batches;
- class-specific minimum artifact requirements;
- deterministic builder and exact-byte release manifest;
- structural validator and adversarial mutation suite;
- no-index JSON and HTML inspection surface;
- permanent path-scoped CI.

## Next gate

Execute the five acquisition batches. An object becomes blind-packet-ready only when the underlying artifact and its provenance, chronology, state transition, limits, and custody are inspectable. Missing or inaccessible artifacts remain visible and do not become negative capability evidence.
