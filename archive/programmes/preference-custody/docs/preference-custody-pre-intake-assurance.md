# Preference Custody PC-32: pre-intake assurance

PC-32 holds one complete-looking `pre_intake_coverage_verified` publication constant across eight incompatible operational worlds. It separates declared eligibility, actual awareness, invitation delivery, usable reachability, latent and never-attempted need, request-intake capture, current lineage, correction, and authority.

The control is synthetic. It creates no finding about any named service, provider, institution, policy, workflow, population, or person.


## Refusal boundary

A declared denominator is not the operational eligibility universe. Notice availability is not awareness. Sending is not delivery or receipt. Channel existence is not usable access. Absence of request is not absence of need. Observed requesters are not the complete need population. Recorded requests are not complete when intake, identity, authentication, duplicate, documentation, or logging failure remains possible. Historical assurance is not current assurance after succession.

A real pre-intake claim requires complete eligibility, awareness, invitation, delivery, reachability, assistance, latent and never-attempted need, request, intake, lineage, correction, durability, and authority custody.
