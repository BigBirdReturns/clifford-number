# Preference representation, validation, and authority matrix v1

This matrix compiles four bounded real-world packets and separates three mechanisms often collapsed into the phrase “human in the loop”:

```text
representation fidelity
finding validation
decision authority
```

The matrix also preserves participant value return, collective power, and system lineage as separate dimensions.

It creates no graph effect, thesis evidence, product ranking, maturity score, market denominator, prevalence estimate, matched-study finding, public-authorization verdict, causal conclusion, or inference of manipulative intent.

## Representation fidelity

Representation fidelity asks who the modeled object represents and what the represented person can do to it.

```text
aggregate or modelled population
individually mapped real-person representation
participant-trained and reviewable representation
participant correction deletion withdrawal and portability
specific-task pre-execution approval
```

An individually mapped Twin can still leave the participant without task-specific client, purpose, question, or downstream-use control. Conversely, a participant can possess meaningful representation rights without the client running a contemporaneous live-human validation study.

## Human validation

Human validation asks which human evidence is present and what object it tests.

```text
parallel direct-human research
participant-owner fidelity review
same-represented-person verification
fresh or targeted finding validation
executed matched study
response distributions and uncertainty
discrepancy reconciliation
```

Participant-owner review tests whether the Twin remains faithful to the represented person. Finding validation tests whether a simulated client finding holds with live respondents. These are related but non-interchangeable objects.

## Decision authority

Decision authority asks whether human evidence can alter the consequential action.

```text
operational human override
binding affected-public objective control and remedy
```

Human research, Twin review, verification, and finding validation may all improve evidence while leaving the institution or client in sole control of the objective and disposition.

## Custody

The compiler reads and compiles the four source packets directly. It emits a SHA-256 hash-linked chain:

```text
matrix manifest sealed
→ each source case classified
→ dimension counts resolved
→ interpretation sealed
```

Each source row carries a hash of its compiled packet. Status drift, dimension promotion, aggregate mutation, or custody tampering fails validation.
