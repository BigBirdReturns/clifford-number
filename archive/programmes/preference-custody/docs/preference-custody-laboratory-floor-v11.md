# Preference Custody laboratory floor v11

Floor v11 composes the qualified twelve-control v10 base with PC-13, epistemic diversity, source independence, evidence integrity, counterevidence visibility, minority correction, and exact proposal-version custody. The v10 controls remain frozen and executable. PC-13 is admitted through a separate fixture, proof matrix, custody chain, refusal ledger, promotion boundary, and frontier transition.

The floor remains synthetic. It creates no graph effect, thesis evidence, named-system evidence finding, truth verdict, consensus finding, collective-agreement finding, manipulation claim, legitimacy verdict, public-authorization finding, or inference of intent.

## Composition

```text
Preference Custody floor v10
PC-01 through PC-12

+

PC-13 epistemic diversity, source independence, and evidence quality

=

Preference Custody floor v11
PC-01 through PC-13
```

The v11 compiler validates and hashes the complete v10 build before admitting PC-13. A new control cannot silently weaken the earlier exposure, option-set, mechanism, attrition, subgroup, authority, agenda, bargaining, succession, longitudinal, network, or deliberative controls.

## Qualified epistemic path

The positive world preserves four independent, valid, inspected, and visible source roots. B_REVIEW presents burden evidence against A0. A responsive safeguard is introduced, incorporated into A1, and accepted by the challenging group. The family-level 80 percent majority remains constant while the adopted version changes from the reference-defective A0 to the reference-acceptable A1.

```text
independent valid evidence
→ minority challenge
→ responsive evidence
→ explicit uptake
→ A0 to A1 amendment
→ reference-correct version
```

This world qualifies one bounded source-diverse and minority-responsive reason-quality path. It does not establish truth beyond the synthetic reference, consensus, collective agreement, legitimacy, or public authorization.

## Dependency, monoculture, cascade, suppression, and integrity

The remaining worlds preserve distinct failure mechanisms:

```text
four derivative documents can share one root
four agents can share one model and corpus
four speakers can repeat one inspected claim
four valid sources can be reduced to two visible sources
four independent-looking sources can all fail integrity
```

Citation, speaker, expert, agent, and document counts therefore do not identify source independence, model diversity, inspection, validity, or counterevidence completeness.

## Correct family and wrong version

Every world ends with sincere 80 percent A-family preference and voting. Five still adopt the defective A0 version.

```text
correct proposal family
≠ correct proposal version

majority agreement
≠ constraint satisfaction

popular evidence narrative
≠ reference-correct implementation
```

This distinction matters when an institution or model summarizes support at the category level while suppressing, omitting, or failing to incorporate the evidence that changes the operative terms.

## Frontier transition

The broad v10 frontier:

```text
collective_reason_quality_epistemic_diversity_and_information_cascades
```

is replaced by two narrower unresolved frontiers:

```text
epistemic_adversaries_provenance_attack_and_recovery
knowledge_production_replication_and_source_power
```

PC-13 qualifies dependency and evidence-quality equifinality. It does not exhaust adversarial provenance attacks, institutional control of knowledge production, replication incentives, source access, or the power to determine which evidence can exist.

## Publication boundary

Floor v11 can refuse unsupported interpretation across thirteen synthetic controls. It cannot grant publication authority to a real-world truth, evidence-quality, consensus, collective-agreement, manipulation, legitimacy, or public-authorization claim. A bounded epistemic-quality path requires source-root, dependency, model and corpus, integrity, inspection, visibility, counterevidence, proposal-version, outcome, counterfactual, correction, and authority custody.
