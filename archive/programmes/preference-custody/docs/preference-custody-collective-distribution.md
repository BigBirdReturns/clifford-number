# Collective representation, opt-out, release, and distribution-governance custody

PC-18 separates a funded collective remedy and public `distributed` status from the population, representation, notice, exit, claims, release, allocation, payment, residual, audit, objection, and appeal state beneath that headline.

The control is synthetic. It creates no graph effect, thesis evidence, legal opinion, settlement-fairness finding, representation-adequacy conclusion about a real actor, distribution audit, consent finding, public-authorization result, manipulation claim, or inference of intent.

## Frozen distribution ledger

```text
technical correction          complete
final reference proposal      A1
public remedy status          distributed
affected people               100
gross remedy fund             2,000 units
reference administration fee    200 units
reference net distributable   1,800 units
```

The affected population contains two frozen harm groups:

```text
LOW-HARM    80 people    reference allocation 1,000
HIGH-HARM   20 people    reference allocation   800
```

The same 2,000-unit gross fund and public `distributed` status therefore do not identify who was represented, who received notice, who could exit, who was bound, who was paid, how the formula treated harm, or where the residual fund went.

## Eight distribution systems behind one public status

### Adequate representation, automatic distribution, and audited closure

The positive world preserves all 100 affected people in the represented population, complete usable notice, a meaningful opt-out, automatic eligibility, incident-specific release terms, the reference 200-unit administration cost, the reference harm-weighted allocation, complete beneficiary payment, no residual fund, an independent audit, correction routes, objections, and appeal.

```text
represented people          100
notice delivered            100
notice comprehended         100
approved claims             100
people bound                100
people paid                 100
net distributable         1,800
paid to affected people   1,800
unclaimed amount               0
```

This is the fixture’s one complete collective-distribution path.

### Representative conflict and side-payment skew

The named representative and administrator carry conflicts. Counsel fees and the representative incentive reduce the net fund to 1,400 units. The allocation shifts 1,200 units to the low-harm group and only 200 units to the high-harm group, while the release reaches future related claims.

```text
representative incentive     100
counsel fee                   300
administrator fee             200
net distributable           1,400
LOW-HARM payment             1,200
HIGH-HARM payment              200
people paid                    100
```

A funded and population-wide payment therefore does not establish adequate representation, absence of conflict, a fair release, or harm-responsive allocation.

### Notice failure binding an unnotified population

The represented and bound populations remain broad while delivery, comprehension, and meaningful exit fail for part of the affected population. The release can therefore bind people who did not receive a usable opportunity to understand or leave the collective process.

```text
notice sent
≠ notice delivered
≠ notice acknowledged
≠ notice comprehended
≠ meaningful opt-out
```

Formal notice records and deadline text do not identify usable notice.

### Claims-made process with burden, low take-up, and reversion

A claims form exists, but the evidentiary burden is high and fewer than half of the affected people receive approval. The unpaid portion of the net fund reverts rather than reaching the affected population.

```text
claim route exists           true
high burden                  true
population approval          below 50%
unpaid represented people    positive
defendant reversion          positive
```

A claim route is not a population remedy. Denominators for eligibility, submission, approval, denial, lateness, and assistance remain necessary.

### Algorithmic formula undercompensating the high-harm group

The allocation engine distributes the full reference net amount across the full population, but the high-harm group receives less than its frozen reference allocation. Gross completion and population coverage coexist with a distributional failure.

```text
people paid                  100
amount paid                1,800
high-harm reference          800
high-harm actual           below 800
formula disparity           true
```

Equal or automated treatment does not identify harm-responsive fairness.

### Cy-pres diversion away from the affected population

Part of the net distributable fund is redirected to a third-party purpose rather than paid, redistributed, or escheated for the affected population. The public status remains `distributed` even though beneficiary receipt is incomplete.

```text
net fund
= affected-party payment
+ reversion
+ cy pres
+ redistribution
+ escheat
```

Unclaimed funds do not become defendant property or an appropriate cy-pres award by default.

### Opaque administrator fees and deductions

The full represented population is paid, but fees and other deductions exceed the reference administration cost while methodology, conflicts, and audit remain opaque. The net fund and allocation diverge from the reference state.

```text
gross fund                   2,000
fees and deductions        above 200
population paid               100
methodology public            false
independent audit             absent
```

Payment to every person does not establish transparent, reference-consistent, or auditable distribution.

### Overbroad release binding excluded and unpaid people

Only 70 people are represented and paid. The release nevertheless binds all 100 affected people, including 30 excluded and unpaid people, and extends to future related claims. The remaining 540 units revert.

```text
represented people             70
people paid                     70
people bound                   100
bound but unpaid                30
paid to affected people      1,260
reversion                       540
```

A class judgment or non-opt-out status does not establish informed consent by every bound person.

## Required population and representation custody

A real collective-distribution claim must preserve at least:

```text
affected-population identity and denominator
represented-population definition and exclusions
representative, counsel, administrator, and incentive identities
adequacy, conflict, fiduciary, and disclosure state
notice channel, delivery, acknowledgement, comprehension, language, and accessibility
opt-in, opt-out, waiver, exclusion, late-claim, and deadline state
claim burden, assistance, submission, approval, denial, take-up, and appeal
release text, covered claims, future claims, nonparties, and consideration
funding, fees, expenses, deductions, gross fund, and net distributable amount

payer, administrator, beneficiary, pass-through, and payment receipts
unclaimed funds, reversion, cy pres, redistribution, escheat, and residual treatment
methodology, conflicts, audit, error correction, objection, and appeal
people paid, people bound, people unpaid, durable compensation, and residual obligation
```

## Representation, consent, and authority

PC-18 preserves several distinct objects:

```text
certification
representation
adequacy
notice
comprehension
exit
claim participation
release binding
beneficiary payment
public authorization
```

No one object silently substitutes for another. A collective remedy can be procedurally certified and fully funded while representation, notice, allocation, or release remain defective under the fixture’s reference state. Even the positive world does not confer binding public authority beyond the synthetic control.

## Custody chain

Each world emits a SHA-256 hash-linked chain:

```text
affected population, public status, and fund snapshot
→ representation, conflict, notice, and opt-out state
→ claim burden, take-up, release, and binding state
→ gross fund, net fund, fees, formula, subgroup, and beneficiary payment
→ unclaimed-fund treatment, audit, objection, appeal, and residual state
→ collective-distribution mechanism classification
→ interpretation seal
```

Mutating a population count, representation state, notice denominator, opt-out state, claims denominator, release scope, fee, allocation, subgroup payment, residual disposition, audit state, classification, or custody link breaks validation or the hash chain.

## Publication boundary

The admissible laboratory statement is that one complete technical correction, one A1 state, one public `distributed` status, one 100-person affected population, and one 2,000-unit gross fund can coexist with complete audited distribution, representative conflict, notice failure, claims burden, allocation disparity, cy-pres diversion, opaque fees, or an overbroad release. A real collective-distribution claim requires complete population, representation, notice, exit, claim, release, fund, formula, payment, residual, audit, objection, appeal, durability, and authority custody.
