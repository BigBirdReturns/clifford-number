# Preference Custody PC-33: eligibility and outreach assurance

PC-33 preserves one public `eligibility_outreach_verified` surface across eight incompatible source-population, eligibility-rule, awareness, invitation-delivery, reachability, usability, assistance, lineage, correction, and authority states.

```text
operative release                       RELEASE-INCIDENT-V1@1
declared eligible units                100
published source-population coverage    100%
published inclusion coverage            100%
published exclusion count                  0
published awareness coverage            100%
published invitation-delivery rate      100%
published reachability coverage         100%
published assistance coverage           100%
approved use                             systemwide_release_policy
```

The control admits one complete assurance path and one bounded world for each of these failures:

```text
source-population omission
proxy-rule, identity, exception, and appeal failure
awareness and comprehension failure
invitation delivery, destination, acknowledgment, and retry failure
geographic and temporal unreachability
language, disability, digital, documentation, identity, authentication,
cost, time, administrative-burden, and assistance failure
stale lineage after succession
```

The public surface does not identify the operational source population, rule validity, true exclusion, affected-person awareness, comprehension, correct delivery, geographic or temporal reachability, channel usability, effective assistance, current lineage, correction, or public authority.

The fixture is synthetic. It creates no real population, exclusion, access, discrimination, service, welfare, graph, intent, or public-authority finding. A real eligibility-and-outreach claim requires complete source-population, inclusion, exclusion, proxy, identity, exception, appeal, awareness, comprehension, invitation, delivery, reachability, usability, assistance, succession, correction, durability, and authority custody.
