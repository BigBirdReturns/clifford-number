## Why this matters

A synthetic-audience deployment and a human panel can coexist under one institutional portfolio without forming a validation loop. Colocation does not establish that each synthetic result is checked by real people, that the same population is represented, that the questions match, or that disagreement changes the decision.

Conversely, the human panel is real counterevidence against a total-replacement narrative. The institution has created and commercialized a direct research surface whose stated purpose is to put humans at the center of the insight process. That evidence must travel with the substitution analysis rather than being discarded because it complicates the thesis.
