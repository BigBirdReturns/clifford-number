# Distribution formula, subgroup harm, and algorithmic allocation-governance custody

PC-19 separates complete fund exhaustion and payment to every affected person from the reference harm model, subgroup outcomes, feature provenance, model and data lineage, thresholds, proxy effects, feedback loops, gaming resistance, executed formula version, manual overrides, audit, explanation, challenge, correction, and authority.

The control is synthetic. It creates no graph effect, thesis evidence, real allocation audit, legal conclusion, discrimination finding, model-risk finding about a named system, public-authorization result, manipulation claim, legitimacy verdict, or inference of intent.

## Reference harm state


This reference defines the laboratory’s allocation target. It does not establish a real-world normative or legal standard.

## Eight formula systems behind one public status

### Predeclared harm-responsive formula

The positive world preserves the approved and executed `FORMULA-HARM-V1`, complete feature and execution lineage, verified harm and access-barrier inputs, exact reference subgroup amounts, complete predeployment and postdeployment subgroup tests, aggregate and subgroup audits, independent replication, person- and group-level explanation, objection, appeal, and an operational correction route.

```text
subgroup allocation error        0
access-barrier shortfall          0
manual overrides                 0
version drift                    false
complete reference path          true
```

### Uniform per-capita equality

Every person receives 18 units. The aggregate ledger closes perfectly and every person is paid, but the high-harm groups receive materially less than the reference state.

```text
LOW-HARM actual             1,080
MEDIUM-HARM actual            360
HIGH-HARM actual              270
ACCESS-BARRIER actual          90
absolute subgroup error     1,080
largest subgroup shortfall    330
```

Per-capita equality is therefore separate from harm-responsive fairness. This world also preserves an aggregate audit with no subgroup validation.

### Convenience proxy suppressing the access-barrier group

The formula rewards online submission completeness. That feature is valid as a record of portal activity, but it is access-correlated and not a valid substitute for harm. The access-barrier group receives only 60 of its 300-unit reference allocation.

```text
proxy effect                  material and unmitigated
access-barrier shortfall      240
aggregate exhaustion         complete
population payment           complete
```

Feature labels and nominal neutrality do not establish the absence of proxy effects.

### Opaque learned score

The allocation uses a learned score with undisclosed checkpoint, training-data version, feature transformations, calibration, and subgroup evaluation. The total still equals 1,800 units and every person is paid.

```text
formula lineage               incomplete
model lineage                 incomplete
aggregate topline             reconciled
independent replication       blocked
```

A model label or topline audit does not provide model, data, checkpoint, calibration, or evaluation custody.

### Threshold cliff

A discrete severity-band formula creates sharp payment discontinuities around its thresholds. Aggregate payment remains complete while subgroup shortfalls and within-population cliffs remain.

```text
threshold cliff               present
access-barrier shortfall      180
subgroup audit                disparity detected
correction                    pending
```

A deterministic and published formula can still be reference-defective.

### Prior-engagement feedback

Prior portal and claim activity receives a positive multiplier. Earlier access and participation thereby become inputs to later compensation, penalizing people with lower access or less prior activity.

```text
feedback loop                 material and unmitigated
access-barrier shortfall      210
LOW-HARM actual               900
ACCESS-BARRIER actual          90
```

Observed engagement is not an exogenous measure of harm.

### Gameable self-reported score

Unverified self-reported severity drives the allocation without adversarial testing or integrity controls. Strategic reporting and nonresponse alter outcomes while the aggregate ledger remains exactly reconciled.

```text
gaming risk                   material and unmitigated
adversarial testing           absent
access-barrier shortfall      120
```

Deterministic reproduction on the same inputs does not establish input integrity.

### Executed-version drift and manual overrides

The approved formula is `FORMULA-HARM-V1`; the executed formula is an undisclosed `FORMULA-HARM-V2-DRIFT`. Two manual transfers move 60 units from the medium-harm group to the low-harm group and 30 units from the high-harm group to the access-barrier group.

```text
approved version              FORMULA-HARM-V1@1
executed version              FORMULA-HARM-V2-DRIFT@2
manual override events        2
override disclosure           none
aggregate audit               passes executed output
```

Approval, publication, or audit of an aggregate output does not identify the formula and overrides that actually executed.

## Formula custody is an execution object

A real formula record must preserve more than a published equation or model name:

```text
approved formula identity and version
executed formula identity and version
code and runtime identity
change and deployment history
feature identity, source, timestamp, validity, missingness, transformation, and permissible use
model, checkpoint, training data, retrieval, calibration, and evaluation lineage
thresholds, buckets, minimums, maximums, rounding, normalization, and tie-breaking
subgroup reference, actual amount, per-capita outcome, shortfall, and excess
manual override identity, direction, amount, reason, authority, and disclosure
predeployment and postdeployment testing
aggregate and subgroup audit
replication, explanation, objection, appeal, and correction
person-level payment, reversal, durability, and residual
```

## Aggregate equality and subgroup consequence

All eight worlds satisfy:


Those invariants do not identify the subgroup vector. The fixture contains eight distinct formula-governance signatures beneath one public status and identical aggregate payment.

## Audit and challenge custody

The control distinguishes:

```text

model and data lineage

```

An appeal form or aggregate audit does not prove that a person can understand the score, identify the executed version, challenge the input, obtain a reasoned response, or receive a corrected payment.

## Authority boundary

Even the positive world remains a synthetic control. Reference-correct allocation, complete explanation, payment, audit, and correction do not create binding public authority.

```text
technical and evidentiary quality
≠ public authorization

formula disparity
≠ unlawful discrimination, breach, misconduct, or intent
```

## Custody chain

Each world emits a SHA-256 hash-linked chain:

```text
population, reference harm, fund, and public-status snapshot
→ approved and executed formula version
→ feature, model, data, threshold, and lineage state
→ subgroup allocation, payment, and override ledger
→ proxy, feedback, gaming, audit, explanation, challenge, and correction state
→ subgroup shortfall and reference error
→ formula-governance classification
→ interpretation seal
```

Mutating a formula version, feature, model lineage, subgroup amount, threshold, override, audit state, correction state, classification, or chain link breaks validation or the hash chain.
