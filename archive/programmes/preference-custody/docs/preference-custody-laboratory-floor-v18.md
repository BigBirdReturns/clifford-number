# Preference Custody laboratory floor v18

Preference Custody floor v18 composes the qualified nineteen-control v17 floor with PC-20, the notice comprehension, accessibility, meaningful exit, operative release, future and nonparticipant scope, assent, correction, durability, and binding-authority control.

The floor remains a synthetic laboratory. It creates no graph effect, thesis evidence, notice audit, consent finding, release-enforceability opinion, coercion finding, public-authorization result, manipulation claim, legitimacy verdict, or inference of intent.

## Composition

```text
qualified base             Preference Custody floor v17
base controls              19
extension                  PC-20
extension worlds            8
resulting controls         20
resulting status           laboratory_floor_v18_qualified
graph effect               none
thesis-evidence use        false
real-world evidence state  none
```

The v17 build is compiled, validated, and hashed as a frozen base. PC-20 is compiled and admitted separately. The v18 compiler creates a new control summary, frontier transition, promotion boundary, and hash-linked composition chain without rewriting the prior nineteen controls.

## PC-20 frozen state

Every PC-20 world preserves:


The same notice-delivery count, payment count, payment total, and public release label therefore coexist with materially different comprehension, exit, release, assent, and authority states.

## Eight release-authority worlds

```text
complete accessible, comprehended, exitable narrow release
notice delivered but mostly unread and unacknowledged
material release and exit terms buried in an overloaded notice
language and accessibility gaps blocking comprehension and exit
formal opt-out with short deadline, high friction, and failed attempts
approved narrow release replaced by a broader future-claims version
affiliates and nonparticipants bound without notice or consideration
payment acceptance treated as assent without complete understanding
```

The positive world joins plain-language and prominent material terms, complete language and accessibility coverage, verified comprehension by all 100 people, a meaningful 60-day exit route, disclosure and comprehension of the operative narrow release, explicit agreement by every bound person, independent review, objection, appeal, correction, reformation, and complete public explanation.

The negative worlds isolate delivered but unread notice, document overload, accessibility failure, formal but unusable exit, binding-version drift, future and nonparticipant scope, and payment without informed assent.

## Refusal contract

Floor v18 preserves these separations:

```text
notice sent or delivered
≠ notice received, understood, accessible, or usable

formal opt-out right
≠ meaningful exit opportunity

payment acceptance
≠ informed agreement to release terms

settlement approval or collective judgment
≠ consent by every bound person

approved or disclosed release
≠ executed or binding release

release label
≠ operative text, version, scope, or time horizon

narrow disclosed release
≠ narrow binding release

representation
≠ authority to bind nonparticipants or future claimants

full compensation
≠ informed release or legitimate objective control

objection or appeal route
≠ effective explanation, correction, or exit

release overbreadth or comprehension failure
≠ breach, coercion, manipulation, misconduct, or intent

public all-claims-released status
≠ complete, informed, accessible, exit-capable, or publicly authorized release
```

Forty-two uniquely prefixed requirements are added to the existing real-case promotion boundary. The compiler calculates the genuinely new requirement count against the complete v17 boundary and refuses duplicate inflation.

## Frontier transition

The broad v17 frontier:

```text
release_scope_notice_comprehension_and_collective_exit_authority
```

is removed and replaced by:

```text
notice_comprehension_accessibility_exit_and_assent_effectiveness
release_text_future_unknown_claim_scope_and_nonparty_binding_governance
```

The first successor isolates communication, assistance, comprehension measurement, meaningful exit, explicit assent, and correction effectiveness. The second isolates operative release text, version, time horizon, future and unknown claims, affiliates, successors, nonparticipants, and the authority to bind them.

The separate v17 frontiers for harm-reference governance and allocation execution assurance remain open, as do prior frontiers involving remedy security, insolvency, successor enforcement, contribution, insurance succession, and service substitution.

## Publication boundary

The admissible laboratory statement is that one complete technical correction, one A1 state, one public `all_claims_released` status, one 100-person affected population, payment to every person, and one 1,800-unit beneficiary total can coexist with complete informed notice and exit, unread notice, overloaded terms, accessibility gaps, unusable opt-out, binding-version drift, future and nonparticipant scope, or payment-as-assent. A real release claim requires complete population, notice, comprehension, accessibility, exit, operative release, future and nonparty scope, assent, payment, representation, binding, correction, durability, succession, and authority custody.
