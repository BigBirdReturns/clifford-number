# Preference Custody laboratory floor v16

Preference Custody floor v16 composes the qualified seventeen-control v15 floor with PC-18, the collective representation, notice, opt-out, release, allocation, payment, residual, audit, objection, and appeal control.

The floor remains a synthetic laboratory. It creates no graph effect, thesis evidence, named-process representation finding, settlement-fairness determination, distribution audit, consent conclusion, public-authorization result, manipulation claim, legitimacy verdict, or inference of intent.

## Composition

```text
qualified base             Preference Custody floor v15
base controls              17
extension                  PC-18
extension worlds            8
resulting controls         18
resulting status           laboratory_floor_v16_qualified
graph effect               none
thesis-evidence use        false
real-world evidence state  none
```

The v15 build is compiled, validated, and hashed as a frozen base. PC-18 is compiled and admitted separately. The v16 compiler then creates a new control summary, frontier transition, promotion boundary, and hash-linked composition chain without rewriting the prior seventeen controls.

## PC-18 frozen state

Every PC-18 world preserves:

```text
technical correction          complete
final reference proposal      A1
public remedy status          distributed
affected people               100
gross remedy fund             2,000 units
reference administration fee    200 units
reference net distributable   1,800 units
```

The population contains two harm groups:

```text
LOW-HARM    80 people    reference payment 1,000
HIGH-HARM   20 people    reference payment   800
```

The public status and gross fund therefore identify neither the represented population nor the actual governance and payment path.

## Eight distribution-governance worlds

```text
adequate representation, automatic distribution, and audited closure
representative conflict and side-payment skew
notice failure binding an unnotified population
claims-made process with high burden, low take-up, and reversion
algorithmic formula undercompensating the high-harm group
cy-pres diversion away from affected people
opaque administrator fees and deductions
overbroad release binding excluded and unpaid people
```

The positive world joins adequate and conflict-free representation, complete usable notice, meaningful exit, automatic population coverage, an incident-specific release, reference fees, the reference harm-weighted formula, complete beneficiary payment, zero residual, independent audit, correction, objection, and appeal.

The remaining worlds separate each of those states. A process can pay all 100 people while preserving conflicted representation, opaque deductions, or a subgroup-skewed formula. A process can bind all 100 while paying only 70. A process can retain the same `distributed` label while redirecting residual funds through reversion or cy pres.

## Refusal contract

Floor v16 preserves the following separations:

```text
class certification
≠ adequate representation

representative appointment
≠ absence of conflict

notice sent
≠ notice received, understood, accessible, or usable

formal opt-out right
≠ meaningful exit opportunity

claim route
≠ population take-up or remedy

settlement approval
≠ fair allocation or complete payment

gross fund
≠ net distributable fund
≠ beneficiary payment

administrator payment file
≠ accurate audited distribution

unclaimed fund
≠ defendant property
≠ appropriate cy pres by default

pro-rata or automated equality
≠ harm-responsive fairness

release or class judgment
≠ informed consent by every bound person

public distributed status
≠ complete, fair, auditable, appealable, or publicly authorized remedy
```

## Identification stage added

Floor v16 adds:

```text
collective_representation_notice_optout_release_and_distribution_governance
```

A real claim at this stage requires custody for:

```text

release text, scope, future claims, nonparties, consideration, and binding authority
gross fund, fees, deductions, net fund, funding account, and receipt
allocation code, inputs, weights, thresholds, subgroup effects, and rounding
payer, administrator, beneficiary, payment attempt, return, and reissue

methodology, conflict disclosure, audit, sampling, correction, objection, and appeal
bound, paid, unpaid, excluded, opted-out, unreachable, and residual populations
durability, correction, succession, and public authority
```

Thirty-five uniquely prefixed requirements are added to the existing real-case promotion boundary. The compiler refuses duplicate laundering by calculating the genuinely new requirement count against the complete v15 boundary.

## Frontier transition

The broad v15 frontier:

```text
collective_claim_representation_opt_out_and_distribution_governance
```

is removed and replaced by:

```text
distribution_formula_subgroup_harm_and_algorithmic_allocation_governance
release_scope_notice_comprehension_and_collective_exit_authority
```

The first successor isolates formula design, reference harm, subgroup error, algorithmic allocation, and correction. The second isolates notice comprehension, release scope, meaningful exit, and the authority to bind nonparticipants or future claims.

Other v15 frontiers remain open, including remedy security, insolvency priority, successor enforcement, cross-jurisdiction contribution, subrogation, insurance succession, and multi-party service substitution.

## Custody chain

The v16 composition emits:

```text
qualified v15 floor snapshot
→ PC-18 admission
→ collective-distribution frontier transition
→ collective-distribution real-case promotion boundary
→ interpretation seal
```

Every event carries its prior-event hash and references only earlier events. Mutation of the base snapshot, PC-18 summary, frontier set, promotion requirements, interpretation, or chain links breaks validation.

Each PC-18 world independently emits:

```text

→ representation, conflict, notice, and exit state
→ claim burden, take-up, release, and binding state
→ fees, net fund, formula, subgroup, and beneficiary payment

```

## Publication boundary

The admissible laboratory statement is that one complete technical correction, one A1 state, one public `distributed` status, one 100-person affected population, and one 2,000-unit gross fund can coexist with complete audited distribution, conflicted representation, notice failure, claims burden, subgroup allocation disparity, cy-pres diversion, opaque deductions, or an overbroad release. A real collective-distribution claim requires complete population, representation, notice, exit, claim, release, fund, formula, payment, residual, audit, objection, appeal, durability, succession, and authority custody.
