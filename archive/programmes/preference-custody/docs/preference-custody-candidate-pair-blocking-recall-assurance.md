# Candidate-pair universe, blocking recall, search-space, and missed-match custody

PC-41 is a synthetic eight-world control. Every world publishes the same complete-looking candidate-search surface while preserving a different eligible-pair, source-combination, blocking, partition, resource-pruning, audit, falsification, or lineage state.

Only the complete world supports complete candidate-search assurance. The control creates no real identity map, recall estimate, match probability, exposure trajectory, causal conclusion, graph effect, named-actor finding, or public-authority verdict.

A real candidate-search claim requires complete eligible-pair and source-combination denominators; blocking-key and normalization provenance; rare, noisy, transliterated, versioned, and missing-identifier coverage; partition, window, top-k, cap, budget, cost, and early-stop recall custody; omitted-pair and missed-match audit; independent labels and representative samples; alternate search, falsification, subgroup analysis, correction, current lineage, and separate authority receipts.
