It remains a synthetic laboratory floor with no graph effect, no thesis-evidence consumption, no real-world policy or welfare conclusion, and no public authority.

```text

capacity stock, flow, utilization, queues, rationing, denial, quality, and unmet need

strategic anticipation, gaming, compliance adaptation, and provider response

independent equivalent replication and market/population transport
release, policy, workflow, supplier, market, population, and scale succession
monitoring, correction, rollback, appeal, certificate withdrawal, durability, and authority
```

The qualified aggregate contains one complete assurance world, seven bounded failure worlds, one public-status signature, eight equilibrium-governance signatures, and 700 unsupported equilibrium decisions across the negative worlds.

## Promotion boundary

PC-29 adds seventy-one machine-addressable requirements covering complete market, capacity, access, price, availability, response, substitution, equilibrium, welfare, replication, scale, correction, and authority custody. The floor refuses promotion from any of the following alone:

```text

nominal untreated units
zero published capacity or price change
completed-service counts
stable uptake
nominal compliance
within-market gains
one solver output
aggregate welfare gain
replication count

```
