# Criterion independence, external validation, transport, and score-use custody

PC-25 separates an external-validation label from the criterion, temporal, population, use, and succession transactions required to support it. The control holds one public `externally_validated` status, a published 0.80 criterion coefficient, two replications, one hundred scored people, one hundred decisions, one consequential score-use label, and an 80-percent decision-agreement claim constant while changing how the criterion was produced and how the score was used.

The fixture is synthetic. It creates no graph effect, thesis evidence, named-validator audit, score-use opinion, consent finding, discrimination finding, coercion finding, manipulation conclusion, real-world causal effect, legitimacy verdict, public-authorization finding, or inference of intent.

### Same-team criterion presented as external

The decision owner designs, collects, adjudicates, scores, stores, and publishes the criterion. Two internal units are described as replications, but both belong to the same legal entity and control neither data nor publication independently.

```text
criterion entities                 1 institutional center
replication legal entities         1
blind adjudication                 false
independent criterion records      0 of 100
```

An external-sounding unit name does not establish independent design, data, analysis, or publication authority.

### Post-decision outcome used as criterion

Independent organizations collect and analyze the result, but the criterion is observed only after the score-informed decision and implementation. The decision shapes the outcome later used to validate the score.

```text

decision-feedback observations     100
published coefficient              0.80
independent predecision reference  0.30
```

Organizational separation does not repair post-treatment timing.

### Shared labels, features, records, and answer material

The validation organization is legally separate, but the criterion derives from the score and shares its data estate.

```text

```

Shared data and answer material can reproduce the score’s own logic rather than provide an independent criterion.

### Independent but construct-mismatched proxy

The proxy is independently designed, collected, adjudicated, and replicated. It predicts payment retention rather than independent operative choice quality.


Organizational independence and statistical agreement do not establish construct relevance.

### Nonrepresentative external replication

Two independent validators replicate the result among forty selected high-literacy volunteers. The deployed population contains one hundred people and lacks a valid transport crosswalk for sixty of them.

```text

transported people                40
selection-bias count              60
```

External replication is not transport to the deployed population.

### Valid bounded prediction used for an unsupported consequential purpose

The score is validated for advisory screening but executed as a binding consequential release choice.

### Historical validation inherited after system succession

The criterion and replications qualified model v1, threshold 0.7, policy v1, and workflow v1. The executed system uses model v2, threshold 0.6, policy v2, and workflow v2 without a succession receipt or revalidation.

## External organization and actual independence

PC-25 preserves several independent control objects:

```text
organizational identity
legal-entity separation
design control
data control
analysis control
publication control
funding and conflicts
criterion timing
criterion data overlap
```

An external organization can remain dependent on the decision owner’s design, data, labels, analysis plan, publication permission, or funding. The validator’s name therefore cannot substitute for the complete authority and custody record.

## Criterion timing and feedback

A criterion must be temporally situated relative to scoring, decision, implementation, and outcome.

```text
predecision criterion
≠ post-decision outcome

independent outcome
≠ outcome caused by the score-informed action

agreement with a decision
≠ independent validity when the score helped determine the decision
```

The temporal ledger must preserve treatment leakage, decision feedback, post-treatment bias, and any path by which the score changed the criterion later used to validate it.

## Construct relevance and proxy outcomes

A criterion may be independent and reproducible while measuring the wrong object. Payment retention, compliance, engagement, absence of appeal, or decision agreement may correlate with a score without representing the intended affected-person outcome.

A real validation packet must specify:

```text
criterion construct
intended interpretation
operational definition
relationship to the score use
known proxy limitations
reference outcome or independent adjudication
```

## Replication and transport

Replication must preserve who controlled the design, data, analysis, and publication. Transport must separately preserve the relationship between the validation population and the deployed population.

```text
replication count
≠ replication independence

external replication
≠ representative replication

representative validation sample
≠ transport after geography, channel, time, or subgroup change
```

A transport packet requires eligibility, population, geography, time, channel, subgroup, selection, missingness, and uncertainty crosswalks.

## Score-use governance

PC-25 separates the validation target from the action authorized by the score.

```text
valid prediction
≠ authority for every decision purpose

advisory screening
≠ binding disposition

reported approval
≠ validated executed use
```

A consequential use requires the exact decision object, threshold, abstention rule, human authority, override, affected population, consequence, and approved-use boundary. Expansion from advisory use into a binding action requires a new validation transaction.

## Succession

Current validation depends on current identity across:

```text
model
score
threshold
policy
workflow
population
use case
```

Any changed object requires an exact succession receipt, a bounded invariance argument, or revalidation. A historical badge does not migrate automatically.

## Real-case promotion boundary

A real external-validation claim requires:

```text

criterion identity, construct, owner, designer, collector, adjudicator, scorer, and repository
criterion timing relative to scoring, intervention, decision, implementation, and outcome
label, feature, record, answer-material, and derivation overlap

external validator identity, legal entity, funding, conflict, and authority
replication design, data, analysis, publication, sample, population, and result

threshold, abstention, human authority, override, consequence, and decision receipt
score-to-decision and decision-to-criterion feedback
approved-to-executed model, threshold, policy, workflow, population, and use-case lineage
monitoring, drift, revalidation, rollback, correction, appeal, and certificate withdrawal

```

## Custody chain

Each world emits a SHA-256 hash-linked chain:

```text
public validation, score-use, and decision surface
→ criterion identity, independence, timing, and overlap
→ external replication identity, control, and result
→ validation-to-deployment population transport
→ validated, approved, and executed score use
→ system and use succession
→ monitoring, correction, appeal, withdrawal, and authority
→ mechanism classification
→ interpretation seal
```

Mutating a criterion owner, timestamp, overlap count, replication control, population crosswalk, score use, threshold, succession field, correction state, or classification breaks the chain.

## Publication boundary

The admissible laboratory statement is that one public 0.80 criterion coefficient, two replications, one hundred decisions, one approved consequential use, and one `externally_validated` status can coexist with complete assurance, same-team validation, post-decision criteria, shared-data contamination, proxy criteria, transport failure, unsupported score use, or stale validation lineage.

A real external-validation claim requires complete criterion, temporal, overlap, replication, transport, score-use, feedback, succession, correction, and authority custody.
