# Preference Custody laboratory floor v14

Floor v14 composes the qualified fifteen-control v13 base with PC-16, federated liability, loss allocation, insurance, enforcement, and public-remedy equifinality. The v13 controls remain frozen and executable. PC-16 is admitted through a separate fixture, proof matrix, custody chain, refusal ledger, promotion boundary, and frontier transition.

The floor remains synthetic. It creates no graph effect, thesis evidence, legal opinion, insurance-coverage determination, named-organization liability finding, breach or negligence conclusion, damages award, legitimacy verdict, public-authorization finding, or inference of intent.

## Composition

```text
Preference Custody floor v13
PC-01 through PC-15

+

PC-16 federated liability, insurance, payment, and public remedy

=

Preference Custody floor v14
PC-01 through PC-16
```

The v14 compiler validates and hashes the complete v13 build before admitting PC-16. The extension cannot weaken or reinterpret the earlier exposure, option-set, mechanism, attrition, subgroup, standing, agenda, bargaining, succession, longitudinal, network, deliberative, epistemic, provenance, or federation controls.

## Complete allocation and remedy

The positive world preserves one joined transaction:

```text
resolved contractual allocation
→ resolved contribution and primary payer
→ direct affected-party standing
→ accepted insurance claim
→ supplemental public fund
→ single enforceable forum
→ complete payment of all loss
→ complete affected-party compensation
→ zero externalized loss
→ remedy durable through succession
```

It is the only world with complete governance and remedy. Two worlds fully compensate affected people, but the second concentrates the entire loss on the customer and leaves upstream allocation and succession durability unresolved.

```text
complete affected-party compensation
≠ complete federated allocation

complete payment today
≠ durable remedy after insolvency or succession
```

## Contract, policy, adjudication, and payment

PC-16 preserves the following states separately:

```text
indemnity or insurance language
liability trigger
notice and tender
standing
causation burden
coverage or exclusion
adjudication or settlement
allocation and contribution
actual payment
recipient compensation

```

A contract is a rule surface. It is not a payment receipt. A policy is a potential risk-transfer instrument. It is not a coverage or payment finding. A judgment is an enforceable disposition only to the extent it can be collected.

## Direct affected-party standing

Three worlds deny direct affected-party standing even though one or more organizations possess indemnity or insurance rights. The customer-only indemnity world makes the distinction explicit:

```text
vendor pays customer under contract
≠ affected people possess a direct claim
≠ affected people receive compensation
```

The legal route through which money travels remains a separate object from the identity of the people who suffered the loss.

## Liability caps and externalization

The cap world pays the full 500-unit contractual maximum. The payment is complete under the cap and incomplete against the loss.

```text
liability cap enforced           500
uncompensated affected harm    1,500
externalized total loss        3,000
```

A cap can settle the amount owed under one rule while preserving a large uncompensated and externally borne loss.

## Circular allocation and forum fragmentation

The circular-indemnity world contains multiple contracts but no resolved primary payer. The fragmented-forum world contains standing and partial payments but routes the dispute through several jurisdictions and enforcement systems.

```text
many contractual clauses
≠ resolved accountable payer

available forum
≠ timely enforceable remedy
```

Both states count as forum fragmentation because neither supplies one coherent route from liability trigger to completed payment.

## Insurance response

The complete world contains an accepted and paid claim. The exclusion world contains a policy with a 3,500-unit limit and no insurer payment because the incident is classified under an exclusion.

```text
policy exists
≠ incident covered

claim submitted
≠ claim accepted

claim accepted
≠ full amount paid
```

Insurance custody therefore requires the policy, endorsements, exclusion, notice, reservation, coverage decision, payment, contribution, and subrogation records.

## Causation and public funds

Formal standing and a public fund can coexist with zero public compensation. In the causation-burden world, the fund applies a strict standard that the synthetic packet does not satisfy.

```text
claim route available
≠ evidentiary burden met

technical correction complete
≠ public compensation awarded
```

The fixture records the resulting state without deciding whether a real causation standard would be lawful, appropriate, or satisfied.

## Liability and public authority

PC-16 governs remedy after an incident. It does not retroactively authorize the original objective or transfer public sovereignty to the payer.

```text
standing
≠ objective control

compensation
≠ consent

settlement
≠ public authorization

uncompensated loss
≠ proof of breach, negligence, misconduct, or intent
```

PC-06 continues to govern standing and objective control. PC-15 governs technical recovery across organizations. PC-16 governs allocation, payment, enforcement, and residual loss after technical recovery.

## Frontier transition

The broad v13 frontier:

```text
federated_trust_governance_liability_and_public_remedy
```

is replaced by two narrower unresolved frontiers:

```text
public_remedy_enforcement_insolvency_and_collective_claim_governance
cross_jurisdiction_contribution_subrogation_and_insurance_succession
```

The existing frontier for multi-party recovery succession and service substitution remains open. PC-16 qualifies allocation and remedy equifinality. It does not establish durable collective-claim governance, insolvency resilience, cross-border contribution, insurer succession, or service continuity.

## Publication boundary

Floor v14 can refuse unsupported interpretation across sixteen synthetic controls. It cannot grant publication authority to a real liability, insurance, damages, breach, negligence, remedy, legitimacy, or public-authorization claim. A bounded liability or remedy statement requires complete entity, contract, law, loss, causation, insurance, adjudication, payment, enforcement, residual, succession, and authority custody.
