Causal fault lines

  1. ELITE geographic prioritization does not prove ELITE individual identification.
  2. A Palantir-developed application does not make Palantir the field arresting authority.
  3. ICE use of ELITE does not prove every displayed record materially shaped the stop.
  4. Mobile Fortify facial recognition is a separate system and causal input.
  5. A preliminary injunction against warrantless arrest practices does not prove ELITE correction, deletion or termination.
  6. The official congressional inquiry is evidence of oversight and an acquisition route, not a completed technical audit.
