CLIFFORD NUMBER · M-05

Story Ecology and Answerable Power

CANDIDATE CROSS-ESTATE PROGRAM

Governing question

Can affected people or accountable institutions inspect, pause, challenge, reverse, replace, govern, and recover value from systems that can otherwise act on them?

Power-answer ladder

R0 · representation

The person or public is visible to the system but has no demonstrated authority over it.

R1 · notice_and_voice

The affected party receives timely notice and can speak before irreversible consequence.

R2 · inspection_and_correction

The affected party or representative can inspect material evidence and correct records or classifications.

R3 · pause_and_burden_shift

An independent actor can stay action and require the acting institution to justify the threshold, category, or necessity.

R4 · reversal_and_remedy

A decision or consequence can be reversed with restoration, compensation, deletion, or other effective remedy.

R5 · substitution_and_public_capacity

The system or vendor can be replaced while preserving data, workflow, continuity, accountability, and service quality.

R6 · co_governance

Affected people possess enforceable authority over purposes, categories, thresholds, oversight, and termination.

R7 · value_recovery

Public or affected parties recover a proportionate share of value, learning, IP, or institutional benefit created through public authority, data, risk, labor, or injury.
