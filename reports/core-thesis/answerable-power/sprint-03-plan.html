R1–R7 answer constitution

LevelNameMinimum mechanismFailure mode
R1timely_notice_and_direct_voiceA person or accountable representative receives usable notice before an avoidable consequential action and may submit relevant facts without retaliation.Notice arrives only after action or voice is received as nonbinding feedback.
R2evidence_inspection_and_correctionThe rights holder can inspect the material data, rule, version, threshold, provenance, user action and rationale and compel timely correction of relevant error.The institution controls the operative record or correction does not reach downstream copies and decisions.
R3automatic_pause_and_institutional_burdenA contestable action pauses before irreversible consequence unless the institution proves a bounded emergency exception to an independent authority.The subject must obtain extraordinary litigation after harm or the emergency exception becomes the ordinary route.
R4binding_reversal_and_remedyAn independent authority can reverse, prohibit, restore, delete, compensate or otherwise remedy the consequence and its residual effects.A formal appeal exists without timely, affordable and effective relief or without correction of the underlying system.
R5durable_substitution_and_public_capacityThe system or vendor can be replaced while preserving data, schema, workflow, users, accreditation, responsibility, service continuity and public accountability.Exit changes the contract or logo while preserving hidden custody, lost capability or dependency on another private layer.
R6represented_party_co_governanceAffected people, through an accountable and removable representative constitution, hold enforceable authority over purpose, category, threshold, acceptable use, exception or termination.Representation is advisory, captured, centralized away from affected people, bypassed technically or unable to bind the operator.
R7proportionate_value_recoveryPublic institutions and affected parties receive enforceable ownership, return, license, IP, compensation, governance or institutional-capacity rights proportionate to authority, data, labor, risk, error, injury and operational learning supplied.Aggregate repayment, tax revenue, symbolic upside or public accounting is mistaken for affected-party recovery or just distribution.

Composition requirements

  1. name the rights holder, represented population, operator, public authority and independent counter-sovereign
  2. define the sequence, triggers and dependencies among R1 through R7
  3. preserve source data, model or rule version, threshold, exception, user action, rationale and rejected alternatives
  4. allocate the burden of proof before and after action
  5. define bounded emergency authority, expiry, review and compensation
  6. define representative selection, removal, minority protection, conflict and anti-capture rules
  7. define technical anti-bypass and successor-system rules
  8. define funding, staffing and public operating capacity
  9. define value-recovery instruments and distribution
  10. define measurable works, regression and stopping standards
