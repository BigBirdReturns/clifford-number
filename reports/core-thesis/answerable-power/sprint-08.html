Lifecycle states

StateMeaning
activeAn externally approved A1 entry remains within its support and validity envelope.
challengedA timely challenge is open; the prior status remains visible but cannot be represented as uncontested.
suspendedMaterial support is absent, unreliable, or under mandatory pause; A1 may not be operationally claimed.
revokedExternal adjudication found the A1 support claim materially invalid or captured.
expiredThe approved validity period elapsed without a timely renewed transaction.
restoration_pendingA previously unsupported entry has supplied a correction package but has not completed renewed external adjudication.
restoredA renewed, separately adjudicated transaction restored support for a bounded validity period.
supersededA material reference or successor-system change ended the prior entry's governing scope.
