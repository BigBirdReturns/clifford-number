Problem and purpose

Consequential institutions may convert uncertain representations into intervention before affected people can inspect the evidence, challenge the category, obtain a pause, compel a remedy, replace the operating layer, participate in governance, or recover value created through their authority, data, labor, risk, error, injury, and feedback.

Convert represented people from downstream proof bearers and feedback sources into rights holders with enforceable pre-action, remedial, operating, constitutional, and distributive power.

R1–R7 sequence

LevelNameTriggerCompulsory mechanismFailure test
R1timely_notice_and_direct_voiceA represented person becomes a candidate for a consequential action that is not within a valid emergency exception.The action window does not begin until notice is demonstrably delivered through an accessible channel.Notice follows rather than precedes the consequence, or voice cannot affect the action packet.
R2evidence_inspection_and_correctionNotice, system registration, a detected error, or a person request.No disputed material fact may be treated as established before correction review; missing records create an adverse presumption against the acting institution unless the person caused the absence.The person sees only a summary, cannot reach operative records, or correction leaves downstream copies or conclusions intact.
R3automatic_pause_and_institutional_proof_burdenA timely contest, material missing record, disputed identity or category, unresolved threshold error, or credible claim of disproportionate consequence.The pause is automatic for covered actions and can be lifted only by a reasoned independent decision or a valid emergency exception.The person must obtain extraordinary relief after the consequence or the institution's assertion is treated as self-proving.
R4binding_reversal_and_effective_remedyAn unlawful, unsupported, erroneous, disproportionate, unreviewable, or procedurally defective action or system use.Orders bind the public authority, operator, vendor, evidence custodians, and downstream recipients; noncompliance triggers sanctions and renewed suspension.Relief exists only on paper, arrives after irreversible harm, or does not reach the system, vendor, copies, or successor use.
R5durable_substitution_and_public_operating_capacityPersistent noncompliance, failed performance, unacceptable dependency, rights violation, public decision to insource, or expiry of the operating arrangement.Portability, escrow, public staffing, modular interfaces, transition funding, and fallback operation are conditions of deployment rather than negotiated only at exit.The institution changes the contract or vendor but cannot independently operate, inspect, migrate, or preserve accountability.
R6represented_party_co_governanceSystem formation, new purpose, new category, threshold change, data or interface expansion, new consequential use, emergency renewal, material update, or proposed termination.The covered function cannot begin or continue without co-governance approval; the representative body can condition, narrow, prohibit, suspend, or terminate it.The body is advisory, institution-selected, under-resourced, centralized away from the constituency, technically bypassed, or unable to bind the operator.
R7proportionate_value_recoveryPublic or affected-party authority, capital, data, labor, infrastructure, risk, error, injury, operational feedback, or institutional legitimacy materially contributes to system value or capability.Preferred returns, warrants, royalties, equity, data or model dividends, IP licenses, clawbacks, compensation funds, and capacity-reinvestment rights are embedded in the operative instrument.Repayment, tax revenue, public spending, symbolic upside, unused clauses, or aggregate fiscal return substitutes for realized and fairly distributed rights.

Anti-bypass rules

IDNameRule
APC-BYPASS-01functional_equivalenceA successor, manual workaround, new vendor, new model, changed product name, new legal entity, or shifted data source remains covered when it performs materially equivalent representation, inference, action, custody, or consequence functions.
APC-BYPASS-02federated_chainRights apply across the full joined action chain even when source data, model, user, decision authority, vendor, and consequence are held by separate entities.
APC-BYPASS-03human_proxyA nominal human approval does not remove coverage when the person lacks material evidence, time, discretion, institutional protection, or a logged ability to reject.
APC-BYPASS-04update_and_purpose_driftMaterial updates, telemetry changes, new categories, new thresholds, new integrations, new users, or secondary uses retrigger R1, R2, and R6 review.
APC-BYPASS-05downstream_copy_and_derived_outputCorrection, deletion, access, remedy, and value rights follow copied, transformed, inferred, embedded, exported, or model-derived records to every recipient.
APC-BYPASS-06jurisdiction_and_contractPublic-private contracting, outsourcing, choice of law, IP claims, secrecy clauses, or cross-border hosting may not eliminate minimum constitutional rights.
APC-BYPASS-07representative_captureAffected people retain direct petition, minority challenge, removal, independent audit, and judicial review when the representative body is conflicted, captured, absent, or structurally remote.
APC-BYPASS-08value_displacementR7 analysis follows value through affiliates, contractors, IP vehicles, data licenses, valuation, cost savings, public capacity, and successor arrangements rather than only direct cash payment.
