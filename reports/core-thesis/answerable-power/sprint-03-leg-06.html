Governing question

Can public capital, demand, data, infrastructure, accreditation, operational feedback, error, and risk be converted into enforceable residual rights that are actually realized and distributed in AI, defense, public-data, and critical-infrastructure finance?

Transfer rule

No cross-sector candidate reaches R7 without a bounded contribution → enforceable residual right → realized value → transparent public or affected-party distribution chain.
