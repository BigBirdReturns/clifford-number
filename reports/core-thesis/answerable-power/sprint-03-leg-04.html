Governing question

Does APC-01's R6 design survive the strongest observed workplace co-governance control when technical architecture centralizes authority, monitoring is framed as capability rather than active use, systems update or migrate, representative bodies become more remote from local workers, and deletion, compensation, exit, and value rights remain separate?

WORK-FAULT-01centralization_shifts_governanceAn indivisible central tenant can move competence from local councils to a central works council.Formal co-determination survives while decision-makers become more distant from locally affected workers.Technical indivisibility proof, local petition and information rights, representative accountability, minority protection, delegated local authority where possible, and independent review.
WORK-FAULT-02capability_not_recordingMonitoring coverage can attach to a system capable of real-time listening even when no conversation is recorded.Employers and vendors may reframe monitoring as ephemeral, safety-related, passive, optional, or unrecorded to avoid governance.Functional-equivalence review based on capability, access, inference, action, and worker consequence rather than storage alone.
WORK-FAULT-03product_update_and_purpose_driftCloud and platform products can gain new modules, telemetry, analytics, integrations, and defaults after the original agreement.A static agreement may legitimize future monitoring that was never actually negotiated.Version registry, material-change trigger, update freeze, recurring review, rollback, audit, and renewed consent.
WORK-FAULT-04representative_capture_and_absenceThe mechanism depends on an existing competent council with sufficient resources and accountability.Workers without a council, contractors, minority groups, remote workers, or workers represented by a captured or distant body may lack equivalent power.Direct rights floor, formation support, union and expert access, conflicts, removal, minority appeal, and independent counter-sovereign.
WORK-FAULT-05remedy_and_value_gapA council can stop use without necessarily controlling deletion, compensation, exit, ownership, IP, or distribution.The institution learns and retains value while workers receive only future-use conditions.Joined R4, R5, and R7 instruments separate from R6 consent.
