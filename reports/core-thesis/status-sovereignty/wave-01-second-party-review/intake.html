Separated second-party reviewer intake

This page invites packet-specific reviewer nominations and review receipts. It does not publish held evidence or confer reviewer eligibility.

Counting rule

No invitation, nonresponse, refusal, acceptance, or unvalidated submission counts as second-party review.

Public route

Use GitHub issue #507 to nominate a reviewer or declare interest. Identify the campaign and packet IDs, provide a public contact route, and do not post private or held source material.
