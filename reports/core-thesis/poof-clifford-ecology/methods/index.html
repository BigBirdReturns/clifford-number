Constitution and method

Projection is not promotion

The ecology adds public usability, reciprocal challenge, and machine access while leaving evidentiary authority in Clifford.

One-way law

Evidence authority moves outward. Challenges move inward. Publication never writes facts backward into the evidence estate.

Evidence authority moves outward

  • canonical receipts, claims, events, identities, surfaces, and reviewed cases
  • deterministic Clifford compilations and structured reports
  • POOF passages, Decision Files, REAL STEEL and Steel Mirror projections
  • human and machine publication interfaces

Challenges move inward

  • reader, subject, journalist, examiner, accessibility, browser, or machine-audit observation
  • typed graph-inert referral packet
  • ordinary Clifford intake, source, selection, temporal, privacy, and human-review gates
  • canonical correction or evidence change only after normal promotion

Prohibited transformations

  • POOF prose cannot create a canonical claim
  • a reader interaction cannot create a finding
  • a WebsiteIQ score cannot establish substantive truth
  • a comprehension receipt cannot establish agreement or case truth
  • REAL STEEL or Steel Mirror similarity cannot define a denominator
  • MCP or API narration cannot add facts

Operational-effect constitution

Graph-inert is not power-inert. Every transaction must declare each operational effect it may exercise.
