Four jurisdictions, one evidence authority

POOF is not a second factual ledger. Each jurisdiction receives only the authority needed for its work.

POOF-J1-REPORT

Report

Stable linear argument compiled from source-addressable projection manifests.

May: Sequence reviewed evidence, analytical judgments, boundaries, counterevidence, and accepted ceilings.

May not: Invent evidence, silently promote a candidate, or hide source and qualification state.

POOF-J2-READER-FILE

Reader File

Local, contestable record of the reader’s declared role, objective, threshold, inspected objects, route changes, and teach-back.

May: Support export, erasure, rerouting, comprehension testing, and voluntary challenge submission.

May not: Infer motive, ideology, intelligence, agreement, or substantive case truth.

POOF-J3-EXAMINATION

Examination

Interdisciplinary proceedings that expose unresolved propositions, external audits, counterevidence, and repair obligations.

May: Issue graph-inert referrals naming the required record, test, custodian, and consequence of nonresolution.

May not: Bypass canonical promotion or impersonate adjudication not actually performed.

POOF-J4-ESTATE

Estate

Durable custody for Person Files, Decision Files, receipts, maps, audits, versions, corrections, counterfactuals, and exit drills.

May: Preserve conflicting versions and exact release custody.

May not: Become an independent factual ledger that diverges from Clifford.
