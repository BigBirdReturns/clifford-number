CLIFFORD NUMBER · CS-P01 · AT-2 CANDIDATE PROTOCOL

Counter-Selector

CS-W00 · ZERO CANDIDATE RECORDS · NO PERSON RANKING · GRAPH EFFECT NONE

Find and test high-output, low-legibility operators and teams by operational residue rather than conventional status metadata, while refusing to create a hidden-genius ranking, identity-based preference system, or self-sealing selector.

Governing rule

Evaluate the artifact and state transition before evaluating the biography. An evaluator's inability to reconstruct a capability creates an acquisition requirement, not a negative capability finding.
