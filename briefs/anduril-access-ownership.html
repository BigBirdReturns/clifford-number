Anduril: access, ownership, and the government gate

A finite map for reporting the company without asking the reader to understand “AI.” It follows two separate questions: how close a record sits to a government decision gate, and how much of the technical stack Anduril owns.

Working proposition

Anduril buys missing pieces of an integrated defense stack while operating in the institutions that decide when that stack becomes eligible, accepted, standard, or orderable. The open question is whether those systems merely developed in parallel or whether records connect access to a particular decision.

Evidence boundary

Lobbying, PAC activity, former-official experience, acquisitions, and temporal proximity do not establish influence, causation, coordination, or wrongdoing. The bridge must be a communication, requirement, acceptance file, ethics record, or signed acquisition decision.

Bypass the word “AI”

  • Detection: what enters, what is classified, and the false-positive / false-negative record.
  • Autonomy: what happens without a new human command.
  • Human in the loop: which person must approve exactly what, and when.
  • Open architecture: whether government can replace Anduril and preserve the mission.

The decisive records

  • Requirement definition, market research, and competitor responses.
  • Acceptance criteria, successful-completion files, and baseline decisions.
  • Data, interface, substitution, scope-expansion, and newly acquired-product rules.
  • Communications and ethics controls tied to the exact decision window.
