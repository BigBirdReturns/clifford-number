## Recorded facts

- The advice concerns a paid CeraCare commission under Jones's independent consultancy after her government service.
- The official record identifies prior roles including Expert Adviser on Health to the Prime Minister, Interim Permanent Secretary and Chief Operating Officer at No. 10, and a final seven-week Expert Adviser role at DHSC.
- The record states that Cera held two DHSC contracts from April to October 2020, before Jones joined government, and that Jones was not involved in those contracts.
- The record states that Jones did not meet CeraCare and did not make decisions specifically affecting it.
- The Committee recorded no evidence that the later commission could reasonably be perceived as a reward for decisions or actions taken in office.
- The record describes more than thirty years of prior healthcare experience as an ordinary explanation for the expertise used in the commission.
- The record states that Jones was formally recused from AI-related matters during her final seven-week DHSC role.
- The Committee imposed conditions concerning privileged information, lobbying, government bids and contracts, use of official contacts, and advance advice for later consultancy commissions.

## Scope and limitations

- ACOBA advice is not an endorsement of the appointment.
- The conditions identify and mitigate risks; they do not prove that a prohibited act occurred.
- The official absence-of-evidence statement is bounded to the material considered by the Committee and is not proof of universal absence.
