## Recorded facts

- The Commission recorded one consolidated Business Appointment Rules breach concerning management of later work through Samantha Harrison's independent consultancy.
- The letter identifies Carnall Farrar work begun before final advice and Newmarket, Huma, and System C work undertaken without prior advice or application.
- The Commission recorded that Harrison engaged constructively and transparently and proactively disclosed several historic matters.
- The letter records Harrison's explanation that the omissions were accidental and that she did not knowingly contravene the Rules.
- The Commission stated that it had no evidence that the substantive conditions governing the work were not followed.

## Scope and limitations

- This 2026 compliance record concerns four later consultancy matters and does not concern the separate CeraCare commission.
- A procedural breach cannot be used retroactively to characterize an earlier appointment as reward, steering, lobbying, misuse of information, or deliberate misconduct.
