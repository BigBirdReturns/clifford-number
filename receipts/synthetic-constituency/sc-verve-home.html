6 KEY FEATUREs OF VERVE

01

Accurate, not just plausible

VIPS are built for decisions of consequence — accurate, auditable and constantly updated. Not black box: built off data clients already trust and validated against humans for specific use cases, giving predictive validity of >90%.

02

A proprietary asset — providing clients with competitive advantage

We provide clients with something genuinely their own. Synthetic populations/panels give the same outputs to all clients and their competitors.

03

Qual and quant — market-leading capabilities in one platform

Not just predictive quant but conjoint and choice-based techniques - a truly full-spectrum research capability.

04

AI + Human Intelligence + Cultural Intelligence

A fully consultative approach for success — globally scalable and individually market-relevant.

05

Insight specialists first, AI builders second

We entered the market understanding client problems deeply and developed AI to solve them — not tech entering the insights industry looking for problems it doesn't understand.

06

A fully formed, market-leading proposition – four years in the making

Our third-generation Verve Vero platform is enterprise-grade, fully agentic and fully scalable — developed over nearly four years with significant but considered investment.
