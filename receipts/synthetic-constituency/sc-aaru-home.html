Ask people, and they answer.Simulate them, and they act.

Humans are unreliable narrators of their own behavior. Memory fails, incentives distort answers, and social pressure warps what people say away from what they actually do. Aaru leverages measurable outcomes and actions to become more accurate than any survey or traditional research.

A home goods company was evaluating two appliance design concepts.

Stated

Participants preferred the more novel design; the extra features won the first look.

Simulated

Agents used it for weeks. Once it became routine, the extra features went untouched.

OutcomeThe team shipped a simpler design with greater confidence and less development effort.

Questions

  • 01
    Product Innovation
    • Which unmet need or recurring frustration would make people choose a new product or feature?
    • Which product concept is most likely to drive adoption, sustained usage, and preference over existing alternatives?
    • How would different audiences respond to the product’s positioning, pricing, packaging, and business model?
  • 02
    Marketing & Brand
    • Which positioning is most likely to make our brand feel distinctive, relevant, and worth choosing over competitors?
    • With this new product line, where can I justify international expansion, and how can I change my positioning for the best outcome?
    • How would changes in price, promotion, channel, or customer experience affect brand perception, conversion, and loyalty?
  • 03
    Audience Segmentation
    • Which distinct consumer segments exist in this category, and what behaviors, needs, and attitudes most clearly separate them?
    • Which segments should the business prioritize for acquisition, growth, retention, or reactivation?
    • Which messages, benefits, channels, and offers are most likely to resonate with each segment?
  • 04
    Scenario Planning
    • How would demand, retention, and price sensitivity change under a recession, a period of rapid growth, or sustained inflation?
    • What would happen if a major competitor lowered prices, entered a priority market, or introduced a disruptive product?
    • Which elements of our strategy remain effective across the widest range of regulatory, economic, and competitive conditions?
  • 05
    Strategic Communications
    • Which narrative most clearly and credibly communicates who we are, what we stand for, and why our strategy matters?
    • How will employees, customers, investors, and the media interpret this announcement, and where might their interpretations differ?
    • During a crisis, which combination of action, timing, tone, and spokesperson is most likely to stabilize trust?
