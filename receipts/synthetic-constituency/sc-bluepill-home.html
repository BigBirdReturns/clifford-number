Frequently asked questions

What is BluePill?

BluePill is an AI-powered consumer insights platform that creates AI consumers — digital twins of real audiences - to help brands predict how people may respond to products, packaging, campaigns, claims, and creative ideas.

How does BluePill work?

BluePill lets teams run surveys, focus groups, packaging tests, claims tests, concept tests, and behavioral studies with AI Twins that think and react like target consumers.

What can I test with BluePill?

You can test product concepts, new SKUs, packaging designs, brand claims, campaign ideas, ad messaging, product positioning, flavors, variants, and purchase drivers.

Is BluePill a replacement for traditional market research?

BluePill is best used for fast exploration, early screening, and iteration. Many teams use BluePill first to improve ideas quickly, then use human research for final validation when needed.

How is BluePill different from a normal survey tool?

Traditional survey tools collect responses from recruited people. BluePill simulates consumer behavior using AI consumer panels, helping teams get faster feedback without recruiting respondents for every early-stage decision.

Who should use BluePill?

BluePill is built for consumer insights, brand, innovation, product marketing, and growth teams - especially in CPG, FMCG, DTC, beauty, food, beverage, healthcare, media, and ecommerce.

Can BluePill help reduce research costs?

Yes. BluePill helps teams test more ideas earlier using AI consumers, reducing the need to run expensive traditional studies for every early-stage concept, message, claim, or packaging route.
