NightDragon and Capital Factory Partner to Accelerate Technology for National Defense

Mon, 18 Aug 2025 13:00:00 GMT · Published by Capital Factory

Partnership will help NightDragon portfolio companies grow by increasing access to Dual-Use innovation decision makers in government and industry

SAN FRANCISCO and AUSTIN, Texas, Aug. 18, 2025 /PRNewswire/ -- NightDragon, a dedicated investment and advisory firm, today announced a new strategic partnership with Capital Factory, the most active investor in Texas and the "center of gravity" for dual-use innovation in the U.S. This collaboration supports NightDragon's evolving strategy, which increasingly recognizes the critical role that defense and dual-use technologies play in addressing today's most pressing national security challenges.

As global geopolitical dynamics grow complex and the need for robust defense capabilities becomes increasingly urgent, both firms recognize the critical importance of dual-use innovation in addressing emerging threats. This collaboration builds on their shared legacy of empowering founders and innovators who are shaping the future of global security, as well as a mutual mission to secure our world for tomorrow for the U.S. and its allies.

"Defense technology is no longer just a government imperative — it's a strategic national asset that must be cultivated with the same urgency as commercial tech," said Dave DeWalt, Founder and CEO of NightDragon.

Together, the two firms provide a full-lifecycle support model of capital, expertise, and market access — designed to empower mission-driven cybersecurity and national security startups at every stage of their journey. From early-stage commercialization to growth-stage scale and exit, this partnership ensures founders have the backing they need to build and lead the next generation of national security leaders.

With its thousands of connections to entrepreneurs, investors, and defense-innovation partners, Capital Factory enables NightDragon with deep insights into the dual-use ecosystem, helping to surface emerging leaders and uncover opportunities earlier in the innovation cycle. As companies mature, NightDragon will apply growth capital and hands-on support through its NightScale platform — which helps founders expand their go-to-market, scale operations, and positions them for long-term success.

The partnership also enhances support for NightDragon's existing portfolio companies by providing them access to Capital Factory's extensive defense and national security network — including relationships with defense primes and national security accelerators like the Defense Innovation Unit (DIU), Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency (DARPA), etc. This network will help accelerate portfolio companies' paths to achieving programs of record and broader government adoption, domestically and internationally.

"At Capital Factory, we measure success by the introductions we make between entrepreneurs and the investors, customers, and talent they need," stated Joshua Baer, Founder and CEO of Capital Factory. "NightDragon shares our passion."

The collaboration is rooted in an active and engaged ecosystem. The two firms have already jointly invested in companies like Saronic, a leading innovator in autonomous maritime systems, and have already co-hosted key industry events such as a dinner focused on "Defending the Next Horizon" at SXSW this year. Additionally, activations are planned for Fed Supernova in Austin, TX from August 19-21, 2025. Learn more at FedSupernova.com.

About NightDragon

NightDragon is an investment and advisory firm focused on growth and late-stage investments within the SecureTech industry, including cybersecurity, safety, security and privacy technologies. Its platform and vast industry network provide unparalleled threat insights, deal flow, market leverage and operating expertise to drive portfolio company growth and increase shareholder value. Founded by Dave DeWalt, the NightDragon team has more than 25 years of operational and market expertise leading technology companies such as Documentum, EMC, Siebel Systems (Oracle), McAfee, Mandiant, Avast and FireEye. Read more about NightDragon at www.nightdragon.com.
