The Future of Human-Technology Interaction

Monday 8 June 2026
11:20 - 11:40
AI Arena
Visionaries
  • Exploring why audio is poised to surpass text as the primary interface between user and technology and how AI is driving this transformation.
  • Detailing how this groundbreaking shift is elevating the potential of AI agents, creating smarter, more adaptable and universally accessible support for businesses.
  • Assessing how to build user trust and demonstrate the tangible value of audio-first technology to prepare populations for a dramatic shift in the way we use technology.
