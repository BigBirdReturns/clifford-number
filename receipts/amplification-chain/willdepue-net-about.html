About Me

←

Hi, I’m Will DePue. Some more about me:

I was born in 2003 in Seattle, Washington, where I lived until moving to Buenos Aires, Argentina as a child. I lived in BA for 5 years, having moved there with my family as part of a radical Christian intentional community. I’m no longer religious.

Later, my family moved to Los Angeles, where I went to middle and high school. Both of my parents are from the south, Georgia and Alabama respectively, which I have great appreciation for.

I am immensely grateful for the privilege of being homeschooled with my two brothers until the 6th grade by my mother. If there’s anything you do for your kids, let it be some form of individualized, holistic, curious learning environment, even for a short while. It’s made a tremendous difference to my life today.

My older brother, Charlie, passed away by suicide when he was 19. I wish you all could’ve gotten to know him. I miss him dearly. You can listen to the speech I gave at his funeral.

I attended the University of Michigan for a year, studying Computer Science, before dropping out. At the time, I was working on the Discord community-analytics company I had started in high school, which I later sold to Commsor from my college dorm.

I randomly landed myself in OpenAI, having been obsessively following since pre-GPT-3, via my friend Alexey Guzey, through the OpenAI Residency program. What I had expected to be a 6-month in-and-out adventure turned into 3 years at the company, and I got to touch a number of incredible projects.

I was tremendously lucky to be a core contributor to Sora and Sora 2, to join the post-training team to help build o3, o4-mini, 4o, and GPT-5. Later, I started a moonshot pretraining team there with Eric Luhman and Troy Luhman to work on human-level sample efficiency.

In 2026, I left OpenAI after three years at the company to take a break and figure out what I really wanted to focus on next for a long time. I currently live between San Francisco and New York.

My main projects today are Macrogrants and Unemployment Labs.

You can reach me at will@depue.net; I’d love to hear from you. Following Alexey’s tradition, I don’t think I’m necessarily good at advice nor worth receiving advice from, but if you’d like advice please email will+advice@depue.net.
