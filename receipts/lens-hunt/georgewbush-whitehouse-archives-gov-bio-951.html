David Norquist -- Department of Homeland Security
Chief Financial Officer

David L. Norquist, of Virginia, was confirmed by the United States Senate on May 26, 2006 as Department of Homeland Security, Chief Financial Officer. Mr. Norquist served as Deputy Under Secretary of Defense (Budget and Appropriations Affairs). Prior to this, he served as Acting Principal Deputy Under Secretary of Defense (Comptroller), as well as Deputy Under Secretary of Defense (Financial Management). Earlier in his career, Mr. Norquist served on the professional staff of the House Appropriations Committee. Mr. Norquist received his bachelor's degree and master's degree from the University of Michigan. He went on to receive a second master's degree from Georgetown University.
