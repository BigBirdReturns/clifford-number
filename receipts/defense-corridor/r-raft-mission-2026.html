We are the software foundation of modern warfare.

At Raft, we build data and AI software that adapts to the mission and conforms to the operator, existing systems, and mission demands—acting as a trusted, transparent partner wherever and however the fight unfolds.

Modern warfare is no longer defined by platforms or programs. It’s defined by software.

Software now determines how data moves, how decisions are made, and how operators accomplish missions under pressure. At Raft, we build the software that defines how the fight unfolds.

Raft was born from a relentless drive to solve the hardest problems in modern warfare — the software foundation that modern warfare is built on.

Founded by Shubhi Mishra with a simple philosophy — cut what doesn’t work and amplify what does — Raft builds software that adapts to the operator and scales to the call of the mission. Our products empower the U.S. government and public agencies to move faster, see clearer, and act with confidence across complex, data-saturated environments.

Focused on the Department of Defense’s most critical problems, Raft helps operators navigate vast, fragmented data ecosystems without forcing them to change how they fight. From the tactical edge to strategic command, Raft ensures every decision is informed, trusted, and mission-ready.
