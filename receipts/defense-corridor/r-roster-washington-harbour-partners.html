Investment Team

Mina Faltas

Founder and Chief Executive Officer

Mina Faltas is the Founder and CEO of Washington Harbour Partners LP, a Washington, D.C., private investment firm managing over $6 billion in assets. Under his leadership, the firm invests in modern commercial technologies, platforms, and capabilities that are shaping America’s future competitiveness across Space, Cyber, Sensing, Resilient Communications, AI/ML and Data, Autonomous Systems, Battle Management Command & Control, Industrial Capacity, and Resilient Energy. In 2026, Washington Harbour Partners was the highest ranked non-government affiliated firm to also fund private equity investments and ranked sixth among all defense technology investors, in the Silicon Valley Defense Group’s NatSec100 report.

With more than 25 years of Technology investing experience, Mr. Faltas has built and scaled technology companies across national security and commercial sectors, spanning services, software, and hardware. Prior to founding Washington Harbour Partners in 2019, he co-founded Nokota Management LP, a $2.7 billion investment firm. Earlier in his career, he was an investor at Viking Global Investors and a Vice President in JPMorgan’s Proprietary Positioning Business.

Mr. Faltas is a recognized voice on the role of private capital in strengthening the national security innovation base and accelerating the adoption of critical technologies across government and industry. He was recognized by Washingtonian Magazine as one of DC’s Leading Defense-Tech Innovators. He serves on multiple advisory boards, including the University of Virginia’s McIntire School of Commerce and National Security Data and Policy Institute, the Silverado Policy Accelerator, and the Ronald Reagan Institute’s National Security Innovation Base initiative.

He holds a B.S. in Commerce (Finance) from the University of Virginia and lives in Virginia with his wife and children.

Tim Morrison

Managing Director

Tim Morrison is a Managing Director at Washington Harbour Partners, bringing more than two decades of experience shaping U.S. national security and defense policy across government and industry. His expertise spans national defense strategy, space policy, nuclear deterrence, arms control, and the development and adoption of technologies critical to U.S. national security.

Before joining Washington Harbour, Tim served as a Vice President for Government Operations for The Boeing Company, where he supported the company’s defense, space, and security business. Previously, he served as Deputy Assistant to the President for National Security during the first Trump administration. At the National Security Council, he served as Senior Director for European Affairs, overseeing U.S. policy coordination for 52 countries and three multilateral organizations. He also served as Senior Director for Counterproliferation and Biodefense, where he coordinated policy on arms control, weapons of mass destruction, export controls, and technology transfers.

Prior to his White House service, Tim spent 17 years on Capitol Hill, including as Policy Director of the House Armed Services Committee, Staff Lead for the Strategic Forces Subcommittee, and National Security Advisor to Senator Jon Kyl. He helped advance key national defense priorities, including implementation of the National Defense Strategy, nuclear modernization, and efforts that contributed to the establishment of the U.S. Space Force.

Tim is a Senior Fellow at the Hudson Institute and serves on an advisory body for the Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory. He previously served as an intelligence officer in the U.S. Navy Reserve. He holds a B.A. in Political Science and History from the University of Minnesota and a J.D. from George Washington University Law School.
