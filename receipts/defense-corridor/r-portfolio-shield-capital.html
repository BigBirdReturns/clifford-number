Airspace Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence
www.airspace-intelligence.com

Airspace Intelligence is bringing the power of machine learning to air controllers and pilots to optimize air traffic management. Airspace Intelligence’s AI-driven platform integrates real-time data with predictive modeling capabilities to help airlines save fuel and gain efficiencies across all their flight operations. Up in the tower, flight controllers use the company’s software to conduct inbound and outbound traffic with unprecedented precision. Airspace Intelligence is organizing the world’s skies one flight at a time.

 
 
 
 

The Mission

Airspace Intelligence is empowering aviation operators and air traffic controllers to increase volume and gain environmental efficiencies so travelers can get to where they want to go safely and ontime.

 
 
 
 

Progress

Airspace Intelligence continues to deploy its platform in cockpits and control towers around the United States. It has already inked a contract with Alaskan Airlines and received significant interest from airlines like United and Delta that are looking for new ways to boost the efficiency of their flight operations. Airspace Intelligence continues to expand its software features and has recently added updates for vertical flight profiles, route histories, and taxi times to enable more accurate real-time air traffic predictions.

Key Applications

Resilient command and control platform to optimize air planning across multiple networks and domains
Scalable for DoD needs to update legacy airspace management systems
