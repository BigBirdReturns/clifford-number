Raft Lands $349M USSOCOM Contract for AI & Data Delivery

  • Miles Jamison
  • September 24, 2025
  • Contract Awards,News
    Author: Miles Jamison || Date Published: September 24, 2025

    Raft, a defense technology company, announced on LinkedIn Tuesday that it has secured a $349 million indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract from the U.S. Special Operations Command to enhance the delivery of edge-to-enterprise data and artificial intelligence technologies across the Department of Defense.

    Enhancing Battlefield Decision-Making

    The single-award Integration of Digital Ecosystem Applications, or IDEA, contract, issued under the General Services Administration Federal Acquisition Service Assisted Acquisition Services Innovation Office, aims to accelerate the delivery of mission-critical technologies to the warfighter.

    Through the GSA Phase III SBIR contract, Raft will provide critical data to enhance decision-making in the battlefield, particularly in connected and denied, degraded, intermittent and limited-bandwidth, or DDIL, environments.

    Raft CEO Shubhi Mishra on Agreement with USSOCOM

    “Together, we’ve delivered forward-leaning defense innovation with real-world impact. This award puts proven defense solutions into the hands of the warfighter faster,” said Raft Founder and CEO Shubhi Mishra. “It’s a reflection of our long-standing partnership with SOCOM, driven by the mission and our nation’s security,” the two-time Wash100 Award winner added.
