# GOV.UK — Simon Case appointed independent Chair of the Barrow Delivery Board

- **Publisher:** Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government
- **Published:** 10 February 2025
- **Official page:** https://www.gov.uk/government/news/simon-case-takes-leading-role-in-plan-to-transform-barrow
- **Retrieved:** 12 August 2026

## Source-supported propositions

The government announced Simon Case's appointment as independent Chair of the Barrow Delivery Board on 10 February 2025. The Delivery Board is responsible for delivering the Barrow Transformation Fund, described as a £200 million government package. The announcement also records that Case had previously acted as interim Chair while Cabinet Secretary.

## Adjudication boundary

The announcement supports Case's appointment, the public source of the £200 million fund, and the Board's delivery function. It does not identify BAE Systems or Westmorland and Furness Council as funders of that government package, and it does not create actor-to-actor adjacency with unnamed board members, officials, industry representatives, or local partners.
