## Source-bounded extract

The official release records that Defence Secretary John Healey visited HM Naval Base Portsmouth to unveil early work on Atlantic Bastion. It separately says First Sea Lord General Sir Gwyn Jenkins would set out the Royal Navy vision at the International Sea Power Conference that day. The release attributes Atlantic Bastion statements to John Healey, Gwyn Jenkins, Dr Rich Drake of Anduril UK, Scott Jamieson of BAE Systems Defence Solutions, and Amelia Gould of Helsing.

The release reports that 26 firms submitted proposals and 20 companies were showcasing technology demonstrators. Only the three named companies above are retained in the relevant organization slice; the broader industry denominator is not reconstructed as a person roster.
