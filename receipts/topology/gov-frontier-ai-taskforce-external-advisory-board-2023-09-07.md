# GOV.UK — Frontier AI Taskforce External Advisory Board appointments (7 September 2023)

- Source: https://www.gov.uk/government/news/industry-and-national-security-heavyweights-to-power-uks-frontier-ai-taskforce
- Publisher: Department for Science, Innovation and Technology / Frontier AI Taskforce
- Published and observed appointment date: 7 September 2023
- Retrieved: 13 August 2026
- Evidence class: official

## Named board cohort

The official announcement lists seven External Advisory Board appointees:

1. Matt Clifford, External Advisory Board Vice-Chair and Prime Minister's Representative for the AI Safety Summit.
2. Yoshua Bengio, Turing Prize Laureate and founder and scientific director of Mila.
3. Anne Keast-Butler, Director of GCHQ.
4. Alex Van Someren, Chief Scientific Adviser for National Security.
5. Matt Collins, Deputy National Security Adviser.
6. Paul Christiano, Head of the Alignment Research Centre.
7. Dame Helen Stokes-Lampard, former chair of the Royal College of General Practitioners and the Academy of Medical Royal Colleges.

The terms published with the announcement state that members join as individuals after due diligence, will play an active role in the Taskforce's work, and are expected to contribute to all board meetings while disclosing conflicts and withdrawing from conflicted procurement conversations.

## Boundary

This receipt supports one exact-date formal co-appointment surface containing the seven named board appointees and the Frontier AI Taskforce as the host institution. It does not establish the date of a first meeting, attendance at every later meeting, continuous tenure, agreement on any issue, procurement participation, employer representation, private contact, shared control, motive, wrongdoing, common purpose, or causation. The announcement separately names Ian Hogarth as Taskforce Chair, Yarin Gal as Research Director, and David Kreuger in a consultative role; it does not list them among the seven External Advisory Board members represented by this surface.
