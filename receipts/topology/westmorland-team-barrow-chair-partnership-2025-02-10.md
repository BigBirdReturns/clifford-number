# Westmorland and Furness Council — Simon Case appointed chair of Team Barrow

- **Publisher:** Westmorland and Furness Council
- **Published:** 10 February 2025
- **Official page:** https://www.westmorlandandfurness.gov.uk/news/2025/former-cabinet-secretary-dr-simon-case-appointed-chair-team-barrow
- **Retrieved:** 12 August 2026

## Source-supported propositions

The council confirms Simon Case's appointment as Chair of the Team Barrow delivery board. It describes Team Barrow as a partnership involving Government, Westmorland and Furness Council, and BAE Systems, backed by more than £200 million of investment.

## Role discrimination

The page identifies the council and BAE Systems as partnership participants. It does not state that either organization supplied the government's £200 million Transformation Fund. The council is retained as the local-government partner and BAE Systems as the industry partner, rather than relabeling both as funders.

## Adjudication boundary

An organization-level partnership does not establish that named employees or officers personally co-participated, met, coordinated, agreed, exercised common control, or shared a purpose.
