# Find a Tender — Project TALOS Phase II contract conclusion and award record

- Voluntary ex ante transparency notice: `https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/020710-2021`

- Retrieved: 2026-08-12

## Preserved source facts

The notices describe a hardware subscription service for perimeter security, software updates, vendor support, and integration with the Ministry of Defence's SAPIENT software. The voluntary ex ante notice records an estimated £3.8 million value and a negotiated procedure without prior publication for stated technical reasons. The later award notice records a final value of £3,736,687.50 and identifies Anduril Industries, Inc. as the contractor.

## Evidentiary use

This receipt preserves the procurement instrument, its distinct dates, the supplier organization, stated procedure, and stated value.

## Limits

The notices identify organizations rather than a bounded natural-person pair. The authority's technical-uniqueness rationale is preserved as the authority's stated justification, not independently adopted as a finding. The record does not prove personal access, favoritism, operational success, continuous use, influence, wrongdoing, common purpose, or causation.
