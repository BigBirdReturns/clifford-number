# GOV.UK: Simon Case appointed Cabinet Secretary and Head of the Civil Service

- **Publisher:** Prime Minister's Office, 10 Downing Street and Cabinet Office
- **Published:** 1 September 2020
- **Effective date:** 9 September 2020
- **Official page:** https://www.gov.uk/government/news/simon-case-appointed-as-cabinet-secretary-and-head-of-the-civil-service

## Source-supported proposition

The Prime Minister appointed Simon Case as Cabinet Secretary and Head of the Civil Service. The official announcement states that he would take up the post on 9 September 2020.
