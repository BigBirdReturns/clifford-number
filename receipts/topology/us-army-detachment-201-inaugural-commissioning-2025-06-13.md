## Named commissioning cohort

The Army article identifies four new Army Reserve lieutenant colonels in the inaugural Detachment 201 cohort:

1. Shyam Sankar, identified as Chief Technology Officer for Palantir.
2. Andrew Bosworth, identified as Chief Technology Officer of Meta.
3. Kevin Weil, identified as Chief Product Officer of OpenAI.
4. Bob McGrew, identified as an adviser at Thinking Machines Lab and former Chief Research Officer for OpenAI.

The Army Reserve page independently records the four-person commissioning ceremony on 13 June 2025. Its official captions show Sankar and Weil reciting the oath together, Bosworth and McGrew reciting the oath together, and the four newly commissioned officers together after the ceremony. The same page states that the four officers would serve in the Army Reserve's newly created Detachment 201 as advisers for efforts including the Army Transformation Initiative.
