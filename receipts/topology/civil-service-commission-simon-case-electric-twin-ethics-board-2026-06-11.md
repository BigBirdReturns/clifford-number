# Civil Service Commission — Simon Case appointment to Electric Twin Ethics Board

- **Publisher:** Civil Service Commission

## Source-supported proposition

The Civil Service Commission considered Simon Case's application under the Business Appointment Rules for an unpaid, part-time role as a member of Electric Twin's Ethics Board. The published case states that the role involves advising the company on the ethics of using AI, with meetings expected approximately twice a year, and that the role will not involve contact with or lobbying of government.

The case also records that Simon Case's last day of Civil Service employment was 31 March 2025.

## Adjudication boundary

This official record supports one actor's appointment to a company advisory body. It names Simon Case and Electric Twin, but it does not identify Ben Warner, Alex Cooper, or any other person as a co-participant on the ethics-board appointment surface.

The record therefore preserves the advisory role as source-addressable company context. It does not support an actor-to-actor hop, a continuous tenure beyond the dated public observation, a government mandate, privileged access, influence, coordination, agreement, motive, wrongdoing, or common purpose.
