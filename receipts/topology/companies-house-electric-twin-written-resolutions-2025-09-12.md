## Source-bounded observations

The filed written resolutions authorize the directors to allot shares or grant rights to subscribe for or convert securities into shares up to an aggregate nominal amount of £1.197496. The authority expires on the fifth anniversary of the circulation date unless renewed, varied, or revoked, while allowing completion of pre-expiry offers or agreements. The resolution substitutes for prior unused section 551 authorities.

The special resolutions adopt replacement articles, waive or disapply pre-emption rights for allotments and rights issued under that authority, and authorize redesignation of up to 741,051 ordinary shares of £0.000001 each into the same number of Seed 1 Preferred shares of £0.000001 each with the rights in the new articles.

## Boundaries

The signatory pages establish eligible-member status for these written resolutions. They do not identify the recipients of any particular Seed 2 allotment, prove beneficial ownership after the transaction, or state that Atomico held shares on 12 September. The resolutions create allotment authority and adopt governance instruments; they do not independently establish the transaction closing date, cash received, or the later public round total.
