# GOV.UK — Anduril Programme TALOS Phase 3 contract announcement and named principals

## Source

- Public URL: `https://www.gov.uk/government/news/17million-contract-awarded-for-force-protection-technology`
- Publisher: UK Strategic Command
- Published: 2 November 2023
- Retrieved: 2026-08-12

## Preserved source facts

The official announcement says the Ministry of Defence awarded Anduril Industries a 31-month, £17 million contract for Phase 3 of Programme TALOS. It identifies Strategic Command's innovation team, jHub, as the public route working with Anduril and describes experimentation on integrated force-protection capabilities.

The same announcement attributes statements to Lieutenant Colonel Dan Sawyers, Head of jHub, Strategic Command, and Greg Kausner, Anduril's Head of Global Defense. Both statements concern the Phase 3 contract and the jHub-Anduril relationship. Matt Clifford is not named.
