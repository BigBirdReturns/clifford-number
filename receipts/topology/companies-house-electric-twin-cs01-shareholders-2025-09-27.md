## Allotment and beneficial-ownership boundary

The separate SH01 forms record Seed 2 allotment quantities of 604,294, 86,327, and 48,559 on 16, 17, and 26 September 2025. Those quantities are numerically congruent with the grouped Atomico, Mercuri, and LocalGlobe Seed 2 registered holdings shown by the later confirmation-date table. The CS01 is nevertheless a point-in-time register-state instrument, not an allotment ledger. It does not state which holder received any particular dated allotment, exclude an intervening transfer or other register movement, identify the beneficial owner, identify the source of funds, or prove settlement. No dated SH01 allottee is therefore attributed from numerical congruence alone.

The governing control question is whether a subscription agreement, allotment schedule, register-of-allotments entry, stock-transfer instrument, or equivalent transactional record directly connects a named holder to a particular allotment. Until such a bridge is recovered, the repository should preserve the exact confirmation-date holdings without converting them into transaction attribution.
