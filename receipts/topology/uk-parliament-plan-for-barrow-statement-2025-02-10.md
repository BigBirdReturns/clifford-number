# UK Parliament — Plan for Barrow written ministerial statement

- **Publisher:** UK Parliament
- **Statement date:** 10 February 2025
- **House of Commons statement:** HCWS428
- **House of Lords statement:** HLWS424
- **Official page:** https://questions-statements.parliament.uk/written-statements/detail/2025-02-10/hlws424
- **Retrieved:** 12 August 2026

## Source-supported propositions

The written statement describes the £200 million Barrow Transformation Fund as government investment. It identifies the Barrow Delivery Board as the local governance institution that will decide how the fund is used and deliver the Plan for Barrow, with representation from the Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government and the Ministry of Defence. It announces Simon Case as Chair.

## Adjudication boundary

The statement supports the public-funding source, the Board's governance function, and Case's chair appointment. It does not provide a complete personal roster and does not justify pairwise actor hops among government, council, defence-industry, or board participants.
