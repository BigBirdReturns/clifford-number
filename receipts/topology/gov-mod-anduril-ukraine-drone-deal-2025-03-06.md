# GOV.UK — UK government and Anduril Ukraine drone deal announcement

## Source

- Public URL: `https://www.gov.uk/government/news/advanced-attack-drones-for-ukraine-in-new-deal-struck-by-uk-government-and-anduril-uk`
- Publisher: Ministry of Defence and Defence Equipment & Support
- Published: 6 March 2025
- Retrieved: 2026-08-12

## Preserved source facts

The official announcement describes contracts totalling nearly £30 million for Anduril UK to supply Altius 600m and 700m systems, launchers, and spare parts for Ukraine, backed by the International Fund for Ukraine and led by Defence Equipment & Support. It identifies Defence Secretary John Healey and Dr Rich Drake, Managing Director of Anduril UK and Europe, as named speakers in the same announcement.

The source also says Healey visited Anduril in Washington on the preceding day and spoke with American and British staff. It does not say that Rich Drake attended that visit.

## Evidentiary use

This receipt supports a one-day official joint-announcement surface for John Healey and Rich Drake. It also supports the organization-level contract facts and the exact Anduril UK executive role stated by the source.

## Limits

The receipt does not establish a personal meeting between Healey and Drake, negotiation history, private communication, authorship of the procurement, contract-signing authority, operational effectiveness, influence over policy, agreement outside the public announcement, motive, wrongdoing, common purpose, or causation.
