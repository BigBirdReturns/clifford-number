## Preserved source facts

The notice records a £6,323,364 service contract for Programme TALOS force-protection capabilities at British Forces Cyprus. It says one supplier received the award. The supplier label is “Anduril Industries Inc.” and the address is in Irvine, California, while the same supplier block gives Companies House number 12316056, which is the identifier of Anduril Industries UK Ltd.

## Evidentiary use

This receipt preserves the award and exposes the internal supplier-identity conflict rather than silently choosing the U.S. corporation or UK subsidiary.

## Limits

The source inconsistency prevents an exact legal-entity resolution from this notice alone. It names no natural-person participant. It does not establish who executed the instrument, who selected the supplier, operational effectiveness, personal influence, motive, wrongdoing, common purpose, or causation.
