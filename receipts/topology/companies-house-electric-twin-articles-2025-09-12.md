## Source-bounded observations

The filed articles identify Alexander Cooper and Ben Warner as the Founders. They define Atomico as Atomico Venture VI SCSp and Atomico Venture VI (Parallel) S.C.A., SICAV-RAIF, together with permitted transferees holding shares, and define LocalGlobe as LocalGlobe XII, L.P., together with permitted transferees holding shares.

Article 25 limits the board to five directors unless the company determines otherwise by ordinary resolution with prior Founder Majority consent. Founder Majority may appoint a majority of the directors then appointed. While Atomico and its permitted transferees hold equity shares, Atomico may appoint and maintain one director and may appoint one non-voting board observer who may speak and receive board papers. While LocalGlobe and its permitted transferees hold equity shares, LocalGlobe may appoint and maintain one director. Article 25.6 permits directors and the investor observer, at their request, to serve on board committees and on boards or committees of subsidiary undertakings.

Article 27 states that the initial quorum for a directors' meeting is three directors and must include at least one Investor Director if one is appointed. If the meeting lacks a quorum, it is adjourned; if the adjourned meeting still lacks a quorum after half an hour, it proceeds. The articles therefore create an investor-presence requirement for the initial meeting, not an unconditional permanent veto over every board meeting.

Article 13 gives each Major Investor a pro rata first offer over New Securities unless Investor Majority Consent permits otherwise, subject to stated exceptions. The articles define a Major Investor as an Investor holding at least 78,251 Equity Shares with permitted transferees, adjusted for reorganisations. They define the Seed 2 Preferred Preference Amount as £9.267 per share. Articles 5 and 6 place Seed Preferred holders ahead of Ordinary holders in the liquidation and exit waterfall for the greater of the applicable Preference Amount and the as-converted Ordinary distribution.

The articles also require Investor Majority Consent for specified actions including purchases of the company's own shares and distributions of available profits. Other provisions refer to a Shareholders' Agreement and Subscription Agreement that are not included in this Companies House filing.

## Boundaries

The articles establish instrument-level rights and conditional appointment powers. They do not establish that any right was exercised, identify the recipients of the September Seed 2 allotments, prove beneficial ownership, disclose the unfiled Shareholders' Agreement or Subscription Agreement, or identify Ben Blume as the Atomico or LocalGlobe appointee. The named investor rights must remain separate from the officer-register evidence for his directorship.
