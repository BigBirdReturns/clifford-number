## Defined parties and qualifying conditions

The filed articles identify Alexander Cooper and Ben Warner as the Founders. They define Atomico as Atomico Venture VI SCSp and Atomico Venture VI (Parallel) S.C.A., SICAV-RAIF, together with permitted transferees holding shares. They define LocalGlobe as LocalGlobe XII, L.P., together with permitted transferees holding shares. Those definitions establish instrument parties and conditional rights. They do not independently establish the qualifying holdings on a later date, the identity of any allottee, or beneficial ownership.

## Board constitution and appointment rights

Article 25 limits the board to five directors unless the company determines otherwise by ordinary resolution with prior Founder Majority consent. Founder Majority may appoint a majority of the directors then appointed. While Atomico and its permitted transferees hold Equity Shares, Atomico may appoint and maintain one director and one non-voting board observer. The observer may speak and receive board papers. While LocalGlobe and its permitted transferees hold Equity Shares, LocalGlobe may appoint and maintain one director. Article 25.6 permits directors and the investor observer, at their request, to serve on board committees and on boards or committees of subsidiary undertakings.

These provisions create conditional appointment and information rights. The filing does not show that Atomico or LocalGlobe exercised a right, identify a particular nominee, or link Ben Blume's separately filed directorship to either investor.

## Quorum and investor-presence mechanism

Article 27 sets the initial quorum for a directors' meeting at three directors and requires at least one Investor Director if one is appointed. A meeting without a quorum is adjourned. If the adjourned meeting remains inquorate after half an hour, it may proceed. The mechanism therefore creates an initial investor-presence condition with an adjourned-meeting fallback. It is not an unconditional permanent veto over every board meeting.

## New-securities and preference rights

Article 13 gives each Major Investor a pro rata first offer over New Securities, subject to stated exceptions, unless Investor Majority Consent permits otherwise. A Major Investor is defined as an Investor holding at least 78,251 Equity Shares with permitted transferees, adjusted for reorganisations. The Seed 2 Preferred Preference Amount is GBP 9.267 per share.

Articles 5 and 6 place Seed Preferred holders ahead of Ordinary holders on a liquidation or exit for the greater of the applicable Preference Amount and the distribution they would receive as converted Ordinary shares. The articles also require Investor Majority Consent for specified actions, including purchases of the company's own shares and distributions of available profits.

## Missing instruments and adjudication boundary

The filed articles refer to a Shareholders' Agreement and a Subscription Agreement that are not included in this Companies House filing. The articles establish instrument-level rights, defined parties, thresholds, and conditional appointment powers. They do not establish that a right was exercised, that a qualifying holding continued on any later date, who received the September 2025 or 2026 allotments, who beneficially owned any shares, who supplied consideration, or whether a liquidation or exit occurred. They create no person-level topology beyond the two founders expressly named by the instrument.
