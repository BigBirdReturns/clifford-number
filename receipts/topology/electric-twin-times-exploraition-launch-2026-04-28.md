## Source-supported propositions

Electric Twin describes Times ExplorAItion as a product announced with News UK and built by News UK on top of Electric Twin's modelling platform. The vendor states that the product combines stripped-back subscriber behaviour, reader panels, engagement data, and PAMCo data to generate synthetic versions of Times audience segments. It says the product sits on News UK's first-party data infrastructure, is available to advertisers and agencies, and is also used by News UK internal teams.

The vendor states that the product uses no personal data. That is preserved as a first-party representation, not as an independent privacy or security audit. The post is authored by Alex Cooper and does not identify Ben Warner as a participant in the News UK deployment.
