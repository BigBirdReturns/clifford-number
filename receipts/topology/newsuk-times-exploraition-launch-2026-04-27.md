# News UK — launch of Times ExplorAItion synthetic audience insight tool

- **Publisher:** News UK
- **Published and launched:** 27 April 2026
- **Official page:** https://www.news.co.uk/latest-news/news-uk-launches-times-exploraition-synthetic-audience-insight-tool/
- **Retrieved:** 12 August 2026

## Source-supported propositions

News UK announced Times ExplorAItion as a synthetic-audience product within its Nucleus data platform, rolling out across Times Media properties. The client describes the product as built in partnership with Electric Twin and as a decision-support tool for advertisers and agencies. It models News UK's real-world audiences using first-party and third-party data signals so users can run surveys, message tests, focus-group-style exercises, and campaign scenario tests before committing market budgets.

News UK expressly states that synthetic audiences are not intended to replace real-world research or performance data. The launch page quotes Alex Cooper as Electric Twin's CEO and co-founder, but it does not identify Ben Warner as a participant in this deployment.

## Adjudication boundary

The source establishes a dated organization-to-organization customer-vendor deployment and a product launch. It does not establish Ben Warner's personal participation, a personal relationship between executives, the accuracy of any synthetic result, replacement of all human research, influence over editorial decisions, coordination beyond the disclosed product partnership, motive, wrongdoing, or common purpose.
