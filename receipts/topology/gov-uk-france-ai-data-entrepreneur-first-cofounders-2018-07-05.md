## Source-bounded extract

The official GOV.UK notes to editors identify Entrepreneur First as founded in London in 2011 by Matt Clifford and Alice Bentinck. The same release attributes an Entrepreneur First statement to Matt Clifford as cofounder and CEO. Entrepreneur First’s current first-party About page independently identifies Alice Bentinck and Matt Clifford as its cofounders and states that each started the organization with the other in 2011.

## Bounded use

This receipt supports one dated official cofounder observation containing Alice Bentinck, Matt Clifford, and Entrepreneur First. It preserves 2011 as the reported foundation year, rather than inventing an exact founding day or treating the 2018 publication as the foundation event.

It does not establish continuous shared management from 2011 through the present, current ownership, beneficial interests, board or investment rights, participation in every cohort or investment decision, a meeting on 5 July 2018, private contact, agreement, coordination outside the reported cofounding relationship, motive, wrongdoing, common purpose, or causation.
