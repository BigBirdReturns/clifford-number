## Preserved source facts

The official register of Matt Clifford's outside interests lists Faculty Science Ltd among his direct investments. The publication landing page records that the declaration was first published on 10 October 2024 and that, by the 24 February 2025 update, Clifford had divested his Faculty Science Ltd shareholding.

The declaration's mitigation terms later required Clifford to use reasonable endeavours to divest from Faculty. This extract uses 10 October 2024 only as the dated public observation that his Faculty Science shareholding then existed. It does not infer when he acquired it.

## Limits

The declaration establishes a financial interest, not management authority, attendance at a company meeting, contact with any director, participation in company operations, influence over company decisions, influence over government decisions, procurement involvement, agreement, coordination, motive, wrongdoing, or common purpose. The later divestment notice prevents the observation from being represented as an open-ended holding.
