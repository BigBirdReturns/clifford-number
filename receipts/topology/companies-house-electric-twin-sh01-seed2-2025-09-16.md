## Boundaries

The SH01 does not identify the allottees. The field labelled amount paid is a filed per-share field; multiplying it by the allotted quantity is arithmetic, not independent evidence of cash received, settlement, beneficial ownership, or equivalence to the later company-announced round. The statement does not yet display the separately filed Seed 1 redesignation dated the same day.
