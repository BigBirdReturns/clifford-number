# UK Parliament — Christopher Wylie oral evidence on ASI Data Science and Vote Leave

- **Publisher:** House of Commons Digital, Culture, Media and Sport Committee
- **Proceeding:** Oral evidence: Fake News, HC 363
- **Evidence date:** 27 March 2018
- **Official transcript:** https://committees.parliament.uk/oralevidence/7803/html/
- **Retrieved:** 12 August 2026

## Source-supported proposition

In oral evidence, Christopher Wylie stated that ASI Data Science worked for Vote Leave and described ASI as one of the entities used by the campaign.

## Adjudication boundary

The transcript supports an organization-level service relationship between ASI Data Science and Vote Leave. It does not identify Ben Warner or Marc Warner as Vote Leave campaign participants, assign either man a campaign role, or establish a bounded period in which either personally co-participated with Dominic Cummings.

The record therefore supports campaign and vendor context only. It does not support a Ben Warner–Dominic Cummings hop, a Marc Warner–Dominic Cummings hop, or any inference of meeting, coordination, agreement, influence, motive, wrongdoing, or common purpose.
