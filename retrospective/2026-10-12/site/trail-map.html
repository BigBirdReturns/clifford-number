Verdict

You have something, and it's more than the first pass said. Seventeen findings are solid enough to show a journalist or researcher today, with their caveats. Sixteen more are well-sourced partial work worth finishing.

What's real is careful compilation of public records, including several honest negatives. None of it is a scoop, and nobody outside your sessions has checked it yet. By volume the repo is mostly scaffolding and self-tests. The good material is concentrated in two subjects.

The log you insisted on is why any of this is recoverable. Every step was recorded with its source, so six agents could rebuild eleven weeks of work in an afternoon and check the numbers against the files. That record is an asset in its own right.
