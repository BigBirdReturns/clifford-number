Heat Band Test

The hypothesis: put every list of people the project gathered on one map and the same names recur across lists far more often than chance allows. This page runs that test on what the repo holds, with the universe size as the one free parameter.

Result

In the lists that were produced independently of each other, overlap sits at chance. Every hot cell in the full union is there by construction: the list was built from another list, so the recurrence was put there by the method, not found in the world.

That does not refute the band. It means the test has not been run yet, and the repo's rule against cross-case identity joins hid that instead of surfacing it. The last section says what running it takes; three of the five rosters needed are already here.

The matrix

Each cell: the number of people who appear in both lists, shaded by how far that is above what chance predicts for a universe of U people. Chance for a pair is (size of A × size of B) ÷ U. Hatched cells were built from each other, so their heat is manufactured.

What running the real test takes

  1. Freeze the target first.The Dialog directory as exposed on 16 Jun 2026 (115 people), hashed, with the as-of date fixed before anything else is looked at.
  2. Build the candidate universe from independent public rosters only.Already held: 70 people on eleven fund team pages, 196 NatSec100 companies, 222 NATO DIANA companies, 837 Capital Factory companies, 100 senators and 441 House offices, the Detachment 201 cohorts. Missing: a complete roster of UK AI advisers and appointees 2023–26, and summit attendee lists. Companies need their founders and officers resolved to people.
  3. Predict from footprint and timelines, then score.Pick the top k by the rule (shared dated records, straight timelines). Report precision and recall against three baselines: random draw, prominence only (page views), and role-matched.
  4. Run it forward.Predict the next published appointment or attendee list before it appears. A forward hit is the only result nobody can argue was fitted afterwards.

Lists: dated UK-AI ledger records (50 people, excluding the Dialog listing), the Dialog directory (115), eleven fund team pages (70), 30 router names seeded from the projection, the Austin corridor (19), BVVC leadership (27), the LinkedIn captures (31), NatSec100 seed people (4). Name matching is by normalised label and is rough. Source data and the computation are in retrospective/2026-10-12/analyses/heat-band-test.json on branch claude/kind-gauss-mgmf99.
