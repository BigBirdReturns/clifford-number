Evidence boundary

What a meeting record proves

An announcement establishes the organizer's stated plans. A post-event organizer account establishes the organizer's report of what occurred. A named company means the source specifically named that company; a stakeholder category does not identify individual attendees.

Shared attendance is a research lead, not a contract, financial transaction, decision, causal connection or Clifford Number hop. Those conclusions require independent, decision-specific receipts and legal-entity resolution. This public selection makes no claim of complete historical coverage.
