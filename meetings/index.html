Evidence rules, exclusions and export semantics

Occurrence, names and relationships

Each source establishes its own scope: an invitation, reported event, government meeting row or selection record. A speaker is not automatically an investor; a sponsor is not automatically an attendee. Names remain source labels until legal identity is independently resolved. Organizer accounts and official reports remain distinguishable from independent corroboration.

Money and outcomes

The index distinguishes source-reported event awards, prior funding, later programme results, potential contract opportunities, investment asks and offered prizes. Amounts preserve their original currency and scope. Aggregate press-release figures are not silently converted into an award-level recipient ledger. Source inconsistencies remain visible.

Export and continuation

JSON exports include the selected records and their source metadata. CSV exports flatten the visible selection, retaining evidence states and provenance URLs; they do not create new relationships. Search links preserve filters and comparison choices. The wider Clifford historical campaign has a separate owner and coverage state.
